Overview

The Top 25 HaxBall Players of All Time ranking was created from ranked ballots submitted by experienced and accomplished players representing different generations of competitive HaxBall.

Voters were specifically instructed to avoid personal bias. Major achievements, tournament success, consistency, longevity, individual ability and overall career legacy were considered when creating each personal ranking.

The Top 10 was determined through a 10-to-1 points system, with 10 points awarded for 1st place and 1 point for 10th. Rankings submitted beyond the Top 10 did not receive main-ranking points and were instead retained as supplementary data for the continuation of the list.

After the Top 10 was established, the ranking was extended through positions 11-25 using the remaining Top 10 support together with the extended placements submitted by voters.

1. Misaj 247 Points

#1 All-Time

247 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, 👑 Misaj was selected as the greatest HaxBall player of all time, finishing No.1 in the ranking. His position at the top was built on a combination of major championship success, consistency across different eras and an extraordinary level of individual talent.

Misaj holds the all-time record for the most HaxBall Champions League titles, winning the competition five times in editions #15, #19, #21, #22 and #23. He also holds the record for the most National Team Cup titles with five, while his record in major finals stands at 10 appearances and 10 victories. His Champions League career culminated with three consecutive titles in #21, #22 and #23, achieved with three different teams.

His legacy was not defined by trophies alone. Misaj was one of the most naturally gifted players the game had ever seen, combining exceptional technique, creativity and game intelligence with a fluid and unpredictable style. From the defensive midfield role, he could dominate the entire game, constantly drive play forward, create chances from almost nothing and change the course of difficult matches through individual quality, often making complex plays look effortless. What separated him from almost every other great player was the way elite talent and a relentless winning mentality existed together at the highest level. He was not only capable of producing moments that very few players could replicate, but also of repeatedly turning that ability into victories when the stakes were highest. That combination of brilliance, control and competitive instinct is what ultimately defined him as the No.1 player in HaxBall history.

2. Dexter 188 Points

#2 All-Time

188 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, Dexter was selected as the No.2 player in HaxBall history, finishing with 188 points. His position near the top of the ranking reflected a career defined by elite individual ability, competitive success and an influence on the way HaxBall was played that extended far beyond his own results.

A defining figure of the Flash era, Dexter became closely associated with the rise of Silas Beavers and later continued that success with Zebras. His career included a remarkable run of three consecutive HaxBall Champions League titles. At international level, he was also part of the Turkey team that won ESL 2015, the first major national-team championship in Turkish HaxBall history. These achievements helped establish him as one of the most successful and recognizable players of the early competitive period.

Dexter's legacy, however, extends well beyond championships. He became one of the earliest examples of a defensive midfielder functioning as the central star of a team, combining creativity, technical ability and an unusually advanced understanding of the game. Several elite players have credited him with pioneering or popularizing mechanics and ideas that later became standard parts of high-level HaxBall, giving him an influence on the game's development matched by very few players.

4. xSha 106 Points

#4 All-Time

106 POINTS

According to the voting panel, xSha was ranked as the No.4 player in HaxBall history. Despite a comparatively short career at the very top, his peak established him as one of the most dangerous and accomplished strikers the game has seen.

xSha won the HaxBall Champions League twice, with Klub Emeryta in #17 and Smokin' Aces in #20. Remarkably, both championships ended with xSha scoring the Golden Goal that decided the final. His performance in HCL #20 became one of the defining displays of his career, completing a hat-trick and delivering the decisive Golden Goal for the second time in his Champions League career.

xSha combined individual finishing ability with the composure to decide the biggest matches, leaving behind one of the most distinctive striker careers in competitive HaxBall.

Whenever the ball reached him, he could change the course of a game on his own, constantly threaten the opposing defense and take responsibility in the most critical moments. Among strikers, he stood apart not only because of his goals and scoring output, but because his mere presence could force opponents to change their game plan. In the all-time ranking vote, SAKY was selected as the No.5 player in HaxBall history.

SAKY built one of the strongest championship records among the leading attacking players of his generation. Across different teams and eras, SAKY became known for his direct attacking presence, relentless pressure around goal and ability to influence decisive moments, qualities that made him one of the most accomplished attacking players of his era.

In the all-time ranking vote, ROCKY BALBOA was selected as the No.6 player in HaxBall history.

ROCKY BALBOA built one of the longest and most successful careers among the leading midfielders in competitive HaxBall history. Although his career continued into the HTML5 era, all of his major achievements came during the Flash era, where he established the strongest part of his competitive legacy. 7. Doctore 70 Points

#7 All-Time

70 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, Doctore was selected as the No.7 player in HaxBall history.

Doctore was one of the defining figures of early competitive HaxBall and one of the leading attacking midfielders of the Flash era. A two-time HaxBall Champions League winner and the captain of Silas Beavers, he combined individual quality with leadership, becoming one of the central figures behind one of the strongest teams of the early competitive scene.

His importance, however, extended far beyond results. At a time when the technical possibilities of HaxBall were still being explored, Doctore was one of the players who helped push the game forward. He became closely associated with the early competitive use and popularization of techniques such as trickshots and doubleshots, while also contributing to the development of ideas such as dropball and more advanced ways of striking and controlling the ball.

Combined with his reputation as one of the best players in his position, his leadership and technical influence made Doctore one of the major figures in HaxBall's early evolution. Many of the techniques and concepts that later became familiar parts of high-level play were still unusual during his prime, giving his impact a significance that went beyond his own career.

8. Marble 57 Points

#8 All-Time

57 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, Marble was selected as the No.8 player in HaxBall history.

Marble established himself as one of the most complete and statistically dominant attacking players in competitive HaxBall history. His domestic career produced extraordinary numbers, including the Division 1 all-time records for both goals and assists, with 285 goals and 185 assists, alongside eight Division 1 championships.

His success also extended to the biggest international competitions. After reaching consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals in #17 and #18, Marble eventually won the tournament with Slappers in #21 and Flaviu 1997 in #23. His first Champions League title produced one of the most remarkable individual final performances in the competition's history, scoring all seven of Slappers' goals in the HCL #21 final.

Capable of playing both as an attacking midfielder and as a frontline striker, Marble combined goalscoring with elite playmaking production. That versatility, record-breaking domestic output and ability to produce extraordinary performances on the biggest stage made him one of the defining attacking players of his generation.

9. Isco 52 Points

#9 All-Time

52 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, Isco was selected as the No.9 player in HaxBall history.

Isco established himself as one of the leading defensive midfielders of his generation and one of the central figures behind the rise of Seeders. His career also included back-to-back HaxBall Champions League finals in #17 and #18, with both titles ultimately slipping away in Golden Goal. Despite never winning the competition, Isco remained a major presence at the highest level, while his career also included an international championship with Israel in Nations Cup 2019.

Originally beginning his career as a goalkeeper before moving into DM, Isco built his game around positioning, decision-making and quick, simple passing rather than individual showmanship. His ability to control matches through structure and team play, combined with longevity across both the Flash and HTML5 eras, made him one of the most respected and highly regarded players of his generation.

In the all-time ranking vote, Altidore was selected as the No.24 player in HaxBall history.

Altidore established himself as one of the most consistently competitive attacking players of his generation, combining versatility with the ability to remain effective at a high level across both the Flash and HTML5 eras. Capable of playing as both a striker and AM, he could contribute through goals, passing and intelligent attacking movement.

His strongest European period came between HaxBall Champions League #13 and #16, where he reached three finals in four editions. Altidore won the competition with Stiff Wind in #13 and #16, while also reaching the #15 final with Skins. His international career added another major achievement when he became a Nations Cup champion with Turkey in 2021, after previously finishing runner-up in 2018.

What defined Altidore most was his ability to remain valuable in different attacking roles and different competitive environments. He could finish chances, create for teammates and adapt his responsibilities depending on the structure around him, qualities that helped him remain an important player in successful teams across multiple periods of HaxBall.

#25 All-Time

7 POINTS

In the all-time ranking vote, Gilbert was selected as the No.25 player in HaxBall history.

Gilbert established himself as one of the most talented DMs of his generation, reaching the highest level during the Flash era before remaining competitive well into HTML5. Most closely associated with Skins, where he served as captain, he combined individual quality with the consistency required to remain part of strong teams across different periods of HaxBall.

He reached three HaxBall Champions League finals, winning #12 with Skins before returning to the final in #15 and later reaching another with Stiff Wind in #19. His international career was similarly consistent, with three major finals for Turkey, including a Nations Cup championship in 2017 and further final appearances in 2021 and 2022.

Gilbert's career was defined by more than his final trophy count. His overall level, longevity and ability to remain competitive across both Flash and HTML5 made him one of the most underrated DMs of his generation, with a body of work that reflected sustained quality over a long period at the top level.

Hulk

tonnnek

R9

Honourable Mention

One of the strongest DMs of the early Turkish scene, R9 reached three HCL finals during his Flash-era career. After two runner-up finishes with Angels of Death, he finally won the competition with Stiff Wind in #9 and retired shortly after completing the major goal that had defined the final stage of his career.

Ced

Honourable Mention

An early example of a more modern striker, Ced combined individual attacking ability with intelligent team play at a time when advanced tricks and powerful shots were less common. He reached two HCL finals and scored two goals in the #6 final as Hellhounds won the championship.

NukroyS

Honourable Mention

One of the elite goalkeepers of early competitive HaxBall, NukroyS also stood out for exceptional versatility and could contribute across virtually any position. He won consecutive HCL titles with two different teams, Silas Beavers in #4 and Zebras in #5.

Kranjcar

Honourable Mention

One of the best and most recognizable players of the Flash era, Kranjcar combined high-level attacking ability with an influence that extended beyond competition. A HCL #5 champion and ESL 2015 winner with Turkey, he also became one of HaxBall's defining video creators, helping present players, teams and the competitive scene to a wider audience.
