📜 Archive Policy

All HaxBall Champions League editions documented on this page are based on the historical records of HaxBallEU.

For the purposes of HaxBall Database, only competitions belonging to the HaxBallEU HaxBall Champions League lineage are recognized as part of the official HCL historical record.

Tournaments organized independently by other organizations under the HCL or HaxBall Champions League name will not be recognized as continuations of this competition.
