Hellhounds was a competitive European HaxBall team best remembered for winning the HaxBall Champions League #6.

The club reached the highest point of European competition with a championship lineup consisting of aguero10, Adriano, Stingox and Ced. Their title run culminated in a dominant Grand Final against Die Galaktischen, which Hellhounds won 4–0.

The championship also became an important part of aguero10's historic Champions League career. Having already won the first two editions with Nerdlucks, his success with Hellhounds gave him a third HCL title and demonstrated his ability to win European championships with different teams.

Overview

Hellhounds' place in competitive HaxBall history is primarily defined by the team's success in HaxBall Champions League #6.

The championship side brought together aguero10, Adriano, Stingox and Ced. The lineup successfully reached the Grand Final and produced an emphatic performance when the European championship was on the line.

Hellhounds combined sustained attacking pressure with an ability to capitalize quickly whenever Die Galaktischen lost control around their defensive area. The result was a 4–0 victory and the club's first HaxBall Champions League championship.

HaxBall Champions League

Hellhounds became HaxBall Champions League winners in Edition #6, joining the group of clubs that established themselves as European champions during the formative years of the competition.

HaxBall Champions League #6: A 4–0 Championship Performance

In HaxBall Champions League #6, Hellhounds reached the Grand Final with a lineup of aguero10, Adriano, Stingox and Ced.

Their opponents were Die Galaktischen, whose final lineup consisted of Caccapupu, iNoob, Nino and Razmo.

Hellhounds took control of the final and never allowed Die Galaktischen to recover. The opening goal came during the first half after sustained pressure around the opposition goal. The situation eventually forced Nino into an own goal, giving Hellhounds a 1–0 lead.

The second goal demonstrated the connection between aguero10 and Ced. aguero10 found Ced with a precise pass, and Ced finished cleanly into the corner to double the advantage.

Hellhounds continued to attack rather than simply protecting the two-goal lead. Stingox created the next opening and found Ced, who once again placed his finish into the corner. Ced's second goal of the final made the score 3–0 and put Hellhounds firmly in control of the championship.

The fourth and final goal came from another chaotic battle around the Die Galaktischen penalty area. Pressure involving aguero10 and iNoob caused the ball to take a decisive deflection and end up in the net.

With the score at 4–0, Hellhounds completed the final without conceding a single goal and secured the HaxBall Champions League trophy.

Ced finished the final with two goals, while aguero10 and Stingox directly contributed to the construction of two of the team's goals. The performance reflected a complete team victory rather than success built around a single individual.

Legacy

Hellhounds' legacy is centered around its HaxBall Champions League #6 championship.

The championship lineup of aguero10, Adriano, Stingox and Ced delivered its defining performance in the Grand Final, defeating Die Galaktischen 4–0 and completing the match without conceding.

The final also connected Hellhounds with several major figures from the early competitive era. aguero10 arrived as an already established two-time European champion, while Ced emerged as one of the central figures of the championship match by scoring twice.

Although Hellhounds did not accumulate the multiple Champions League championships associated with some of the competition's longest-running dynasties, the club permanently secured its place in HCL history by becoming the champion of Edition #6.
