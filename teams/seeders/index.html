Seeders is widely regarded as one of the best and most dominant teams in HaxBall history. While their success eventually extended beyond domestic competition, the foundation of their reputation was built through the Feed-Me League, where Seeders established itself as one of the defining dynasties of competitive HaxBall.

For an extended period, Seeders dominated the Feed-Me HaxBall League, establishing one of the most impressive runs in the competition's history. Their success stemmed from sustained tactical dominance, remarkable attacking output, and an iconic, possession-based passing style.

Led by captain Isco, Seeders developed a distinct team identity that dictated terms against every opponent they faced, both domestically and on the European stage.

Overview

Seeders emerged from a squad that had already tasted success under the banner of QuickSwans. After capturing the Feed-Me League title under that name, the entire roster transitioned together into the following season as Seeders, with Isco officially assuming the captaincy.

🛡️ Prime Seeders Roster:
Isco (C) • Yannex • Marble • Pringles • aguero10 • shaxlele

Together, this core group carried their momentum into a new identity and immediately set the standard for competitive excellence across the scene.

Feed-Me Dominance

The Rise of Seeders

Following their initial championship as QuickSwans, Seeders continued their complete reign over domestic competition. Seeders won the Feed-Me League in Seasons 24 and 25, extending their run to three consecutive league titles (the "3-season embargo") and establishing one of the most dominant periods in the history of the competition.

The Art of Passing

Seeders built its identity around an exceptionally fluid passing game. The ball rarely seemed to stop moving, with the team constantly circulating possession and creating passing angles from every position. For opposing teams, defending against Seeders was suffocating, as their continuous movement and relentless ball circulation made regaining control nearly impossible.

📊 All-Time Scoring Record:

Seeders holds the undisputed Feed-Me League Division 1 record for most goals scored in a single season, reaching an astonishing 118 goals.

HaxBall Champions League #17

While Seeders continued its domestic reign, the same squad entered European competition. For HaxBall Champions League #17, they competed under the name BLACKOUT (using another team's slot, similar to their QuickSwans campaign) with their full core lineup: Isco, Yannex, Marble, Pringles, aguero10, shaxlele, and Echo.

HCL #17 Final: BLACKOUT vs Klub Emeryta

In the Grand Final, BLACKOUT faced Klub Emeryta, featuring SAKY, xSha, Kojo, and Ter. SAKY and xSha were still in the early stages of their careers—having played in Division 2 just seasons prior—but were rapidly developing into elite attackers.

⚔️ Final Match Summary (BLACKOUT vs Klub Emeryta):

  • Domination & 1–0: BLACKOUT dictated early play with high possession, leading to Isco setting up Yannex for the opener.
  • 1–1 & 1–2: Klub Emeryta responded on a counter-attack through SAKY assisting xSha, before SAKY converted a corner-spam to put Emeryta ahead 2–1.
  • 2–2 Equalizer: With under 60 seconds remaining, aguero10 supplied Isco for a dramatic equalizer to send the final into Golden Goal.
  • Golden Goal Heartbreak: On a rapid 1v1 transition, xSha struck the winning Golden Goal to seal a 3–2 win for Emeryta.

HaxBall Champions League #18

Seeders returned for HCL #18 officially under their own name, determined to conquer Europe alongside their ongoing Feed-Me league campaign.

Quarter-final: Seeders vs Stiff Wind

In the quarter-finals, Seeders faced Stiff Wind (Liéds, Liebe, ROCKY BALBOA, Catrice)—the most decorated team in Champions League history at the time.

Despite Catrice giving Stiff Wind an early lead, Seeders mounted a relentless comeback. A combined effort from Yannex and Isco leveled the match, before an Isco shot deflected in to take the lead. Marble added the third to seal a monumental 3–1 victory, knocking out the defending giants.

HCL #18 Final: The Golden Goal Nightmare

🥀 The 15-Second Golden Goal Nightmare (Seeders vs The Elders)

Facing The Elders (LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY, Vak) in the Grand Final:

  • 1–0 Lead: After a 0–0 regular time, Marble set up Yannex in extra time to put Seeders ahead.
  • 1–1: Vak equalized for The Elders with a spam goal.
  • 2–1 Lead: Marble created chaos from a corner, resulting in an Isco/Yannex deflection goal to regain the lead.
  • 2–2 Late Drama: With under 2 minutes remaining in extra time, SAKY equalized to push the match into Golden Goal.
  • 15-Second Heartbreak (2–3): Just 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY struck again to complete the comeback for The Elders.

Losing back-to-back Champions League finals in Golden Goal extra time—despite outplaying their opponents for long stretches—remains one of the most tragic narratives in HaxBall history.

Legacy

Seeders' legacy is defined by one of the most dominant periods ever seen in competitive HaxBall. Their three consecutive league titles, record-breaking 118-goal Division 1 season, and revolutionary possession-based passing style established them as a true powerhouse of their era.

Despite never lifting the HaxBall Champions League trophy—suffering two agonizing Golden Goal defeats in back-to-back finals—Seeders earned immense, universal respect across the entire community. Their ability to consistently dominate matches through pure tactical superiority, fluid passing, and incredible teamwork ensured that, even without an HCL title, Seeders remains widely remembered as one of the most feared, admired, and iconic teams in HaxBall history.
