Overview

Group of Specialist was a Czech team that competed during the early competitive era of European HaxBall.

The club's principal recorded achievement came in HaxBall Champions League #10, where Group of Specialist became European champions.

The winning squad featured Forsbi, Lukyno, Bonaparte and Doupi.

HaxBall Champions League #10

Group of Specialist won HaxBall Champions League #10, securing the only Champions League title currently recorded in the club's history.

Championship Lineup (#10): Forsbi • Lukyno • Bonaparte • Doupi

Legacy

Although relatively little information about Group of Specialist has been preserved, the club holds a permanent place in HaxBall Champions League history as the champion of Edition #10.

The championship also represents one of the notable achievements of Czech competitive HaxBall during the early European era.

The title-winning lineup of Forsbi, Lukyno, Bonaparte and Doupi remains the central surviving record of the team.
