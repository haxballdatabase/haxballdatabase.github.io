Flaviu 1997 was a Romanian HaxBall team led by its captain and founder, Flaviu.

Originally a Romanian team, the club changed its identity as Flaviu began assembling an international squad of elite players. The arrival of Misaj, widely regarded as the best player in the game at the time, proved to be a major turning point.

Misaj entered Flaviu 1997 having already won the Champions League in the previous two editions. Flaviu then surrounded him with players considered among the best in their respective positions, creating a genuine all-star team built to dominate the competition.

The lineup featured Flaviu, Misaj, PiperSalvia, SAKY and Marble. Flaviu had assembled the stars; Misaj brought a championship pedigree that the rest of the team would soon share.

HaxBall Champions League

HaxBall Champions League #23

HaxBall Champions League #23 became the defining moment in Flaviu 1997's history.

The all-star lineup fulfilled its purpose and won the HaxBall Champions League, securing the team's only Champions League title.

For Misaj, it was a remarkable continuation of an extraordinary run. After winning the competition in the two previous editions, he joined Flaviu 1997 and immediately lifted another trophy, helping give Flaviu his own Champions League triumph.

The victory completed Flaviu's ambitious project, as the former Romanian team conquered the competition with a lineup assembled from some of the strongest players of its generation.

Championship Lineup (#23): PiperSalvia • Misaj • SAKY • Marble

Legacy

Flaviu 1997 is remembered for its transformation from a Romanian team into an international all-star squad.

The club's Champions League #23 triumph, combined with Misaj's continuation of his three-edition title streak, made Flaviu 1997 one of the most star-studded champions of its era.
