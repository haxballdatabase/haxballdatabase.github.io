Skins is a Turkish HaxBall team that competed across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

The club became one of the more notable Turkish teams of the Flash era, particularly through its performances in the HaxBall Champions League. Skins reached two HaxBall Champions League finals during that period, winning Edition #12 before returning to the final in Edition #15.

Its championship in Edition #12 and second final appearance three editions later established Skins as a respected and competitive presence within the Turkish scene during the later years of the Flash era.

Overview

Skins competed as part of the Turkish HaxBall scene and built its strongest competitive record during the Flash era.

🎬 Skins Movie: Watch on YouTube

The club's most important period came through two HaxBall Champions League final appearances. Skins first reached the championship match in Edition #12 and won the competition, before returning to the final again in Edition #15.

Gilbert became one of the players most closely associated with the team and served as captain during its major Champions League period.

HaxBall Champions League History

Skins reached two HaxBall Champions League finals, recording one championship and one runner-up finish.

Both final appearances came during the Flash era. The club won the competition in Edition #12 against Pas De Nom, then returned three editions later in Edition #15, where it was defeated by Angry Bulls.

HaxBall Champions League #12: Champions

Skins reached the final of HaxBall Champions League #12 with a lineup of Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Their opponents were Pas De Nom, who lined up with Tonnnek, Leu, Kranjcar and Tino.

Skins opened the scoring through a combination between Elysion and Catrice. Elysion supplied the ball into Catrice, who finished into the corner to give Skins a 1–0 lead.

Shortly after the second half began, the same partnership produced another goal. Elysion again found Catrice, who converted to make it 2–0.

Catrice later added a third goal after reacting to a rebound, completing his hat-trick. Pas De Nom pulled one goal back, but Skins remained in control of the match.

The final action came in the closing seconds, when Catrice reacted first to another loose ball and scored his fourth goal of the match. The goal ended the contest immediately, with no further kick-off taking place, and Skins secured a 4–1 victory.

Championship Lineup (#12): Liéds • Gilbert • Elysion • Catrice

HaxBall Champions League #15: A Second Final

Skins returned to the final in HaxBall Champions League #15.

The final lineup consisted of kojo, Gilbert, Vanessa and Altidore.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, whose final lineup featured Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Angry Bulls won the final 3–1. Misaj recorded two assists during the match, while Müllewa scored twice.

The defeat left Skins as runner-up and denied the club a second HaxBall Champions League championship.

Legacy

Skins' place in Turkish HaxBall history is defined primarily by its Flash-era success. Reaching two HaxBall Champions League finals in a relatively short span demonstrated the club's ability to remain competitive at the highest European level.

The Edition #12 championship remains the greatest achievement in the club's history, while the return to the final in Edition #15 confirmed that the earlier title was not an isolated deep tournament run.

With one championship, one runner-up finish and a competitive presence that later extended into the HTML5 era, Skins remains one of the notable Turkish teams associated with the later Flash period.
