Stiff Wind is one of the most decorated and historically significant teams in competitive HaxBall history.

The club captured the HaxBall Champions League title three times, securing memorable triumphs in Editions 9, 13, and 16. Built upon a foundation of long-standing chemistry, decisive counter-attacking efficiency, and elite leadership, Stiff Wind established itself as one of the defining dynasties of European HaxBall.

A distinct feature of Stiff Wind's legacy is that all of the club's major titles were achieved during the Flash era of HaxBall. The team exercised complete dominance during this period, standing as the premier powerhouse of the Flash era while remaining without major silverware in the subsequent HTML era.

Throughout its history, Stiff Wind was anchored by legendary goalkeepers such as Tonnnek, Liéds, and PiperSalvia. Outside of international competition, the club achieved massive success across premier leagues including HaxLife, Feed Me, and League1.

Origins & The AoD Rebrand

Before establishing their monumental legacy under the Stiff Wind banner, ROCKY BALBOA and R9 competed together for AoD (Angels of Death)—one of the most established and respected clubs in early competitive HaxBall.

During their tenure with AoD, the duo suffered two heartbreaking Champions League final defeats across different seasons in high-stakes rivalries against top-tier powerhouses Silas Beavers and Pas De Nom. Determined to break through their championship ceiling, they opted for a complete fresh start, rebranding the team as Stiff Wind. To complete their new project, they recruited creative forward Catrice and reliable goalkeeper Tonnnek.

Champions League Campaigns & Flash Era Dominance

Central to Stiff Wind's identity was captain and master strategist ROCKY BALBOA. Combining deep tactical reading with psychological management, he alongside long-time partner R9 forged a ruthlessly competitive identity that propelled Stiff Wind to unprecedented heights.

HaxBall Champions League #9: The Historic Breakthrough

In the grand final of HaxBall Champions League #9, Stiff Wind faced Antarctic Lions, a formidable European powerhouse led by star player Yaadon. Driven by their past final losses in AoD, ROCKY BALBOA and R9 refused to let another opportunity slip away.

The match delivered high-level tactical intensity:

  • Catrice's Masterclass: Catrice delivered a spectacular attacking performance, opening the scoring before later setting up a crucial second goal.
  • Opponent Response: Antarctic Lions equalized when Yaadon set up Meyer, but Stiff Wind's lethal counter-attacks restored their lead to 2–1.
  • R9's Tactical Lockdown: In an iconic tactical adjustment during the final three minutes, R9 completely refrained from pushing into attack, anchoring the defense to prevent any late drama.

Stiff Wind secured a 2–1 victory, lifting their first Champions League trophy under their new name. Following the historic victory, R9 announced his retirement from competitive play.

Championship Roster (#9): Tonnnek (GK) • R9 (DM) • ROCKY BALBOA (AM) • Catrice (ST)

HaxBall Champions League #13: The Second Crown

Four editions after their first championship, Stiff Wind returned as a major contender in HaxBall Champions League #13. The new lineup featured Liéds, Liebe, ROCKY BALBOA and Altidore, with ROCKY BALBOA once again at the centre of the team's competitive identity.

Semi-final: Golden Goal Against Gladiators

In the semi-final, Stiff Wind faced Gladiators, a strong and physical Italian side whose lineup consisted of Maldo, gio, William and 2pa.

The match developed into a difficult defensive battle, with neither side able to find a decisive breakthrough during regulation time. With the score still level, the semi-final continued into Golden Goal.

The decisive moment came when ROCKY BALBOA played the ball toward Altidore. Altidore met the pass first time with a powerful strike and scored the Golden Goal, giving Stiff Wind a 1–0 victory and sending the club into another HaxBall Champions League final.

Final: Stiff Wind vs Lux Aeterna

In the final, Stiff Wind faced Lux Aeterna. The championship lineups were:

  • Stiff Wind: Liéds, Liebe, ROCKY BALBOA, Altidore
  • Lux Aeterna: SPAMMEUR FOU, Falk, Echo, elite

The opening goal arrived at 09:16. Altidore created the opportunity for ROCKY BALBOA, who converted to put Stiff Wind 1–0 ahead.

Lux Aeterna responded quickly. At 10:22, Echo found elite, who scored the equalizer and brought the final back to 1–1.

Stiff Wind needed little time to restore their advantage. At 12:23, Altidore once again provided the assist for ROCKY BALBOA, who scored his second goal of the final to make it 2–1.

The championship was finally secured at 30:00, when Altidore added Stiff Wind's third goal. The match ended 3–1, giving Stiff Wind their second HaxBall Champions League title.

Championship Roster (#13): Liéds (GK) • Liebe (DM) • ROCKY BALBOA (AM) • Altidore (ST)

HaxBall Champions League #16: Historical Comeback

In HaxBall Champions League #16, Stiff Wind lined up with PiperSalvia (GK), ROCKY BALBOA (DM), Altidore (AM), and Catrice (ST). To maximize the full potential of his squad, captain ROCKY BALBOA made a crucial tactical pivot: despite naturally playing as an attacking midfielder (AM), he stepped back into the DM role. Delivering an outstanding defensive display, ROCKY BALBOA anchored a highly disciplined defense while controlling the game's tempo. Combined with clinical conversion of key scoring opportunities, Stiff Wind proved virtually unstoppable.

In the grand final, Stiff Wind met Klub Emeryta, a star-studded side consisting of Per, kojo, ANDREA, and the legendary, trophy-laden icon aguero10.

Early Setback (0–2):
Everything seemed to be going perfectly for Klub Emeryta. In the 6th minute of the first half, ANDREA set up aguero10, who cleanly placed the ball into the corner to break the deadlock (1–0). Shortly after, an uncharacteristic miscue by goalkeeper PiperSalvia saw the ball drop directly in front of aguero10, who capitalized on the chance to make it 2–0. Klub Emeryta had total control, and Stiff Wind appeared to be on the verge of collapse.

Dramatic 60-Second Fightback (2–2):
Right as the first half drew to a close, Stiff Wind launched a legendary response. Altidore slipped a brilliant pass to Catrice, who finished past Per's desperate save to pull one back (2–1). Before Klub Emeryta could even reset, Catrice returned the favor, setting up Altidore to level the score at 2–2 in the dying seconds of the half. Scoring two goals in under a minute stunned Klub Emeryta and completely shifted the momentum of the final.

Golden Goal Triumph:
With the match tied 2–2, the final headed into extra time under Golden Goal rules. Capitalizing on their psychological momentum, Catrice delivered a swift, decisive goal in extra time to seal a miraculous 3–2 victory.

The result handed aguero10 a rare, heartbreaking final defeat while crowning Stiff Wind as the first team in Champions League history to win three titles.

Championship Roster (#16): PiperSalvia (GK) • ROCKY BALBOA (DM) • Altidore (AM) • Catrice (ST)

HaxBall Champions League #19: The Battle for Supremacy

HaxBall Champions League #19 represented a massive historical crossroads. Reaching the grand final once more, Stiff Wind aimed to claim an unprecedented 4th title and cement their record at the pinnacle of HaxBall history.

Their opponents were arch-rivals Angry Bulls, who were chasing their own 3rd title to match Stiff Wind's record. The grand final developed into an intense tactical duel:

  • Early Lead: Stiff Wind took early control, striking first to establish a 1–0 lead.
  • Equalizer: Angry Bulls responded as Misaj assisted Necro for an equalizer, forcing the championship decider into extra time.
  • Heartbreak: In the dying seconds of extra time, with less than 30 seconds left on the clock, Misaj struck the winning goal for Angry Bulls.

The defeat saw Stiff Wind finish as runners-up, allowing Angry Bulls to join them at the top with 3 Champions League titles each in one of the most iconic rivalries in HaxBall history.

Dominance in Feed Me

🔗 Feed-Me Team Profile: Stiff Wind

Beyond their legendary continental campaigns in the Champions League, Stiff Wind demonstrated immense dominance across major league formats, most prominently in Feed Me.

The club established an era of supreme consistency, claiming the Feed Me league title three times, lifting the championship trophy in Seasons 28, 34, and 35. Their mastery of the league format solidified Stiff Wind as an all-around powerhouse capable of long-term stability and high-level execution over extended seasons.

In knockout cup competition within the same ecosystem, Stiff Wind maintained a terrifying presence. They captured the Feed Me Cup twice while reaching the grand final as runners-up on four additional occasions. This remarkable collection of league and cup silverware proved that Stiff Wind's dominance was not limited to international tournaments, but extended seamlessly across every competitive arena they entered.

Legacy

Stiff Wind's legacy is defined by its three HaxBall Champions League titles, tactical discipline, and unprecedented Flash era dominance.

Key figures like ROCKY BALBOA, R9, Catrice, Tonnnek, Liéds, PiperSalvia, Liebe, and Altidore carved their names into competitive history. The club's goalkeeping lineage—featuring Tonnnek, Liéds, and PiperSalvia—provided unmatched stability across different championship cycles, while league crowns in HaxLife, Feed Me, and League1 established their complete versatility.

ROCKY BALBOA's legacy extends far beyond his playing achievements. Recognized for his unmatched tactical intellect, psychological edge, and game control, his executive leadership as an administrator in HaxLife also played a pivotal role in shaping the competitive scene for years.

As the first club in history to achieve three Champions League trophies, Stiff Wind remains an immortal benchmark of excellence in competitive HaxBall.
