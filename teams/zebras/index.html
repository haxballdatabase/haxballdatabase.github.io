Zebras was a Turkish HaxBall team from the early Flash era, best remembered for winning HaxBall Champions League #5.

The team was closely connected with the group that had represented Silas Beavers, winners of the previous two HaxBall Champions League editions. After Silas Beavers had won Edition #3 and Edition #4, several members of that successful generation returned to the competition under the Zebras name and once again reached the final.

The championship lineup consisted of Иukroyš, Dexter, David Luiz and Kranjcar. The player competing under the name David Luiz was actually Doctore, one of the central figures of the earlier Silas Beavers generation.

The campaign was particularly significant for Kranjcar. Although he had been closely associated with Silas Beavers, the club already possessed an established four-player structure, limiting his opportunities in the primary lineup. With Zebras, Kranjcar received a regular place in the team and made the opportunity count by becoming a HaxBall Champions League champion.

Overview

Zebras became part of early HaxBall history through its championship victory in HaxBall Champions League #5.

The club appeared directly after the consecutive championships of Silas Beavers. Silas had won Edition #3 and Edition #4, and players from that successful generation remained together around Zebras for the following campaign.

Dexter and Иukroyš had both been members of the Silas Beavers lineup that won Edition #4. The Zebras lineup also featured Kranjcar and David Luiz, with David Luiz being the name used by Doctore during the competition.

This created a direct connection between Zebras and the dominant Turkish group that had won the previous two editions of the competition.

HaxBall Champions League

Zebras' defining campaign came in HaxBall Champions League #5, when the club reached the championship match against TI SPARTA!.

HaxBall Champions League #5: A Chaotic Final

The final brought Zebras against TI SPARTA!, whose side included players such as Rooney1878, Alan10 and iNoob.

Zebras lined up with Иukroyš, Dexter, David Luiz and Kranjcar. David Luiz was the name being used by Doctore during the competition.

The First Restart

Zebras appeared to take the lead almost immediately. Kranjcar supplied the pass and David Luiz finished the move to score.

However, a ping problem had occurred during the sequence. Zebras chose to respond with fair play and agreed to restart the match, removing their own opening goal.

The Second Restart Controversy

After the restart, Rooney1878 became involved in a spamming battle with Иukroyš. The ball struck Иukroyš and travelled backwards into the Zebras goal.

Иukroyš argued that he had experienced a ping problem during the play. TI SPARTA! did not believe that a significant ping issue had occurred and therefore did not return the fair-play gesture that Zebras had shown earlier.

Despite this, the match was restarted for a second time. The decision was controversial, as the restart did not appear to follow the normal competitive procedure.

The issue was not finished, however. Tournament administrators later reviewed the situation and intervened before the beginning of the second half.

Zebras Take Control

With play continuing after the second restart, Zebras began to take control of the final.

A shot from David Luiz was blocked and the loose ball fell directly into the path of Kranjcar. Kranjcar reacted quickly and scored to give Zebras a 1–0 lead.

The second goal came from a well-executed counter-attack. David Luiz moved the ball forward and found Kranjcar, who struck toward goal and finished the attack to make it 2–0.

Kranjcar then became the provider. His pass reached David Luiz, who converted the chance and extended Zebras' advantage to 3–0.

TI SPARTA! responded with one of their strongest passing sequences of the half. Alan10 supplied the final pass to Rooney1878, who scored to reduce the deficit to 3–1.

Administrative Decision Before the Second Half

Before the second half began, the tournament administrators returned to the earlier incident involving Rooney1878 and Иukroyš.

They ruled that the goal scored before the second restart should have remained valid.

As a result, TI SPARTA!'s previously removed goal was restored and the match resumed for the second half with Zebras leading only 3–2.

Second Half

Early in the second half, Alan10 attempted a reverse touch but sent the ball into his own goal, restoring Zebras' two-goal advantage at 4–2.

TI SPARTA! continued to push forward and produced an excellent counter-attack. Иukroyš initially prevented the chance from going directly into the net, but the ball rebounded into the path of Rooney1878.

Rooney1878 converted the rebound and brought TI SPARTA! back into the match at 4–3.

The final decisive moment came from another spamming battle, this time involving David Luiz and iNoob.

The ball eventually came off iNoob and travelled backwards into the TI SPARTA! goal for an own goal, giving Zebras a 5–3 lead.

That became the final score. Zebras defeated TI SPARTA! 5–3 and became HaxBall Champions League #5 champions.

Kranjcar: Making the Most of His Opportunity

The Zebras championship became a particularly important moment in Kranjcar's playing career.

Kranjcar had maintained a close connection with Silas Beavers, but the team already possessed an established four-player core. As a result, he was more frequently used as a substitute or rotational player than as a permanent member of the primary lineup.

With Zebras, his situation changed. Kranjcar received a regular place in the team's HaxBall Champions League lineup and became directly involved in the club's championship run.

He played a major role in the final itself. Kranjcar scored Zebras' first two counted goals, first converting a rebound from a David Luiz shot and then finishing a counter-attack after receiving a pass from David Luiz.

He also assisted David Luiz for the goal that initially extended Zebras' lead to 3–0.

The performance made the championship particularly meaningful for Kranjcar. After struggling to break into the established Silas Beavers starting structure, he was given his opportunity with Zebras and directly contributed to a HaxBall Champions League final victory.

Legacy

Zebras' legacy is built around its HaxBall Champions League #5 championship and its close connection with the successful Silas Beavers generation that preceded it.

The final against TI SPARTA! became particularly memorable because of its unusual sequence of restarts, fair-play decisions and administrative intervention.

Zebras voluntarily restarted the match after a ping problem affected its own opening goal. A second restart later became controversial after TI SPARTA! had scored through the incident involving Rooney1878 and Иukroyš. Tournament administrators eventually restored that goal before the beginning of the second half.

Despite the unusual circumstances, Zebras maintained its advantage and eventually won the match 5–3.

The victory also carried important individual significance. Dexter completed a run of three consecutive HaxBall Champions League championships across Silas Beavers and Zebras, while Иukroyš won consecutive editions with two different team names.

For Kranjcar, Edition #5 became one of the defining achievements of his playing career. After receiving fewer opportunities inside Silas Beavers' established lineup, he became a central part of Zebras' championship side and produced two goals and an assist during the final.

Although Zebras did not build a long multi-edition history, its championship ensured that the team remained permanently connected with one of the most successful early generations of Turkish HaxBall.
