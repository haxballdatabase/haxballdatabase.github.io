Slappers is an Italian HaxBall club founded by Enzo and Pazzo. Originally built around Italian players, the club later went through several different periods and roster changes before eventually achieving the greatest success in its history.

Slappers reached its historical peak in HaxBall Champions League #21, when a newly constructed lineup led by Misaj defeated Angry Bulls 7–2 in the Grand Final.

During the same wider period, Slappers also achieved success in Feed Me competition, winning the Season 28 Feed Me Cup. Misaj later became the club's all-time assist leader with 72 assists, while Necro became its all-time leading goalscorer with 88 goals.

Overview

Slappers began as an Italian team, with Italian players forming the core of the club during its original period. The club was founded by Enzo and Pazzo, who established the team long before its eventual Champions League breakthrough.

Over time, Slappers became more open to international lineups. During one period, players associated with Angry Bulls spent a season representing Slappers before later leaving the club.

That connection would eventually become historically significant. Several seasons later, events surrounding Angry Bulls indirectly led to Slappers assembling the lineup that would produce the greatest achievement in the club's history.

Early History

Slappers' original identity was strongly connected to the Italian HaxBall scene. The team was created by Enzo and Pazzo and initially featured a predominantly Italian player base.

At a later stage, the club temporarily became home to a group of players connected with Angry Bulls. Their stay lasted for approximately one season before the players moved on, and Slappers subsequently continued under its existing club identity.

The connection between Slappers and those players appeared to have ended at the time. However, circumstances surrounding HaxBall Champions League #20 eventually brought one of them back to the club under completely different circumstances.

The Return of Misaj

The turning point came after the events of HaxBall Champions League #20. During Angry Bulls' semi-final against Deathwish, a controversial sequence surrounding a ping problem contributed to growing tensions between several players. Angry Bulls subsequently shut down.

Approximately one to two months later, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction and revived Angry Bulls.

Following those developments, Misaj spoke with Slappers founder Enzo about returning to the club. Enzo gave Misaj the freedom to construct the lineup he wanted, effectively allowing him to build a new Slappers side for the upcoming Champions League campaign.

Misaj accepted and assembled a new roster with one particularly clear objective: defeating the revived Angry Bulls.

HaxBall Champions League

HaxBall Champions League #21: Slappers' Greatest Triumph

Slappers entered HaxBall Champions League #21 with the newly assembled lineup of NORBIGO, Misaj, Vichoexe and Marble.

Their route to the championship included a difficult semi-final against reigning champions Smokin' Aces. The match remained scoreless throughout regular time before Slappers eventually secured a 1–0 victory in extra time.

The result sent Slappers into the Grand Final, where the story surrounding the newly built team reached an unlikely conclusion: their opponent was Angry Bulls.

Angry Bulls entered the final as one of the competition's most successful clubs. They had already won the Champions League three times and had never previously lost a Champions League final.

The final, however, quickly moved in Slappers' direction. Slappers took control almost immediately and built a remarkable 3–0 lead within the opening three minutes.

Rather than allowing Angry Bulls back into the match, Slappers continued to extend the advantage. Marble scored all seven Slappers goals as the new lineup produced one of the most dominant performances of the tournament.

The Grand Final ended 7–2, giving Slappers its first HaxBall Champions League championship and handing Angry Bulls the first final defeat in its history.

🎥 Final Record: Slappers vs. Angry Bulls — HaxBall Champions League #21 Grand Final

For Slappers, the victory represented the greatest achievement in the club's history. A team that had begun with a distinctly Italian identity had reached the top of European HaxBall with an international lineup assembled specifically for that campaign. For Misaj, the championship carried an additional personal significance. Following the events of the previous season and the revival of Angry Bulls, he had returned to Slappers with the specific intention of building a team capable of defeating his former club. The two sides ultimately meeting in the Grand Final, and Slappers winning it, brought that story to its conclusion.

Feed Me Success

Slappers' success during this period was not limited to the Champions League. In Feed Me Season 28, the club also won the Feed Me Cup, adding another major trophy to one of the most successful periods in its history.

The results across different competitions helped transform Slappers from a long-established Italian club into a team associated with major international success.

Legacy

Slappers' history can be divided into two distinct identities. Its roots belong to the Italian HaxBall scene, with founders Enzo and Pazzo establishing the club and its original Italian core.

Its most successful period came much later. Following the events surrounding Angry Bulls in HCL #20, Enzo gave Misaj the freedom to assemble a new lineup, resulting in the Slappers team of NORBIGO, Misaj, Vichoexe and Marble.

That lineup delivered the club's defining achievement by winning HaxBall Champions League #21, defeating the reigning champions Smokin' Aces on the way to the final before producing a dominant 7–2 victory over Angry Bulls.

The same general era also included the club's Feed Me Cup victory in Season 28 and the establishment of two significant individual club records: Misaj's 72 assists and Necro's 88 goals.

From its Italian beginnings to its eventual European championship, Slappers developed into a club whose history connected different generations, nationalities and competitive eras of HaxBall.
