Omertà was a competitive HaxBall team best remembered for winning HaxBall Champions League #14.

The championship lineup consisted of Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba. In the Grand Final, Omertà defeated Klub Emeryta 3–2 in Golden Goal.

The championship was defined by a dramatic final in which Omertà recovered from an early deficit, took the lead through two goals from Mamba, and ultimately secured the trophy when Deliric scored the decisive Golden Goal.

Overview

Omertà's place in HaxBall history is primarily defined by its championship run in HaxBall Champions League #14.

The team reached the decisive stages of the competition with a lineup of Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

Their championship was secured against Klub Emeryta in a closely contested final that required Golden Goal to determine the champion.

HaxBall Champions League

Omertà became HaxBall Champions League winners in Edition #14, earning the championship after one of the closest finals of the tournament's Flash-era period.

HaxBall Champions League #14: Golden Goal Glory

Omertà reached the HaxBall Champions League #14 Grand Final with Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

Their opponents were Klub Emeryta, whose starting lineup featured kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo.

Klub Emeryta struck first late in the opening half. DrG found the breakthrough and gave Omertà a 1–0 deficit.

Omertà responded early in the second half. A loose rebound around the Klub Emeryta goal fell to Mamba, who finished into the corner to equalize at 1–1.

Shortly afterwards, Omertà applied pressure around the opposing goal and another loose ball emerged. Mamba reacted first again, scoring his second goal of the final and turning the match around to give Omertà a 2–1 lead.

Klub Emeryta answered quickly. A misplaced ball from Pringles was intercepted, allowing Liebe to convert the opportunity and bring the score back to 2–2.

Neither side was able to decide the championship during the remaining period of the match. The final eventually progressed into Golden Goal.

The decisive moment came through Deliric. After a physical battle with DrG, Deliric managed to win the situation and finish the opportunity.

The goal immediately ended the final. Omertà had completed a 3–2 victory and secured its HaxBall Champions League championship.

Historical Notes

The championship carried different significance for several members of the winning lineup.

For Pringles, the victory represented his first HaxBall Champions League championship.

For aguero10, the title added another chapter to an already historic European career. Having previously won the competition twice with Nerdlucks and once with Hellhounds, the Omertà championship became his fourth HaxBall Champions League title.

Mamba's two goals provided the turnaround after Omertà fell behind, while Deliric ultimately delivered the championship-winning moment in Golden Goal.

Legacy

Omertà's legacy is centered around its HaxBall Champions League #14 championship.

The championship lineup of Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba secured the title through a final that required several changes of momentum and ultimately Golden Goal.

Mamba's two-goal performance turned an early deficit into a lead, while Deliric scored the final and most important goal of the team's campaign.

The victory also connected Omertà to several major figures of its era, particularly aguero10, whose fourth Champions League title further strengthened one of the most successful European records of the period.

With its dramatic 3–2 Golden Goal victory over Klub Emeryta, Omertà permanently secured its place among the championship teams of HaxBall Champions League history.
