Deathwish was a Turkish competitive HaxBall team whose recorded history is centered on a single season, HaxBall Champions League #20. The team reached the final in that edition and is mainly remembered for the controversial semi-final against Angry Bulls and the final that followed against Smokin' Aces.

The team was captained by Turya. Its HCL #20 squad also included Müllewa, Jéal, Abduction and Splat.

Most of the team's surviving competitive record comes from that HCL #20 campaign.

Overview

Deathwish was a short-lived Turkish team whose recorded competitive presence is centered on HaxBall Champions League #20. The team reached the final in that edition, but there is no comparable multi-season record attached to the club in this archive.

Captain Turya led the team, while former Angry Bulls players Müllewa and Abduction later faced their former club in the semi-final.

Jéal also became one of the most important figures of the run, particularly in the final, while Splat played in the lineup that reached the championship match.

HaxBall Champions League #20

HaxBall Champions League #20 is the main recorded tournament in Deathwish's history. The team reached the final after a semi-final against Angry Bulls that became controversial because of a fair-play dispute.

Semi-Final Against Angry Bulls

In the semi-final, Deathwish faced Angry Bulls. The matchup already carried an unusual personal dimension because Deathwish included former Angry Bulls players Müllewa and Abduction, while Jéal was one of Misaj's closest friends at the time.

The decisive controversy began when Angry Bulls goalkeeper Liéds experienced a serious ping problem during play, contributing to a goal being conceded. Angry Bulls expected Deathwish to respond with fair play, but the match continued.

Within Deathwish, Abduction wanted the team to respond with fair play, but the other players did not agree and play continued. The incident turned an already important semi-final into one of the most controversial matches of the edition.

Despite Misaj producing a major individual performance of 3 goals and 1 assist, Deathwish eliminated Angry Bulls and advanced to the HCL #20 final.

The consequences extended beyond the semi-final itself. Tensions between Misaj and several Deathwish players increased after the match, and Angry Bulls subsequently shut down. The incident later became an important part of the chain of events that led into the following Champions League edition.

Final Against Smokin' Aces

Deathwish entered the final against Smokin' Aces with a lineup of Abduction, Müllewa, Jéal and Splat. Smokin' Aces fielded vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha.

Smokin' Aces took the lead through xSha, but Deathwish responded early in the second half. A move involving Müllewa created a loose ball for Jéal, who reacted quickly and scored to make it 1–1.

After Smokin' Aces regained the lead at 2–1, Deathwish answered again. Müllewa led the transition and found Splat, who finished the move to level the final at 2–2.

The match moved into extra time, where Deathwish struck first. Müllewa produced an excellent pass for Jéal, who calmly finished the opportunity to put Deathwish 3–2 ahead. At that moment, the team stood on the edge of its first Champions League title.

The final then changed direction dramatically. During the second period of extra time, a physical spam battle produced a loose ball toward Jéal. While attempting to deal with it defensively, he accidentally sent the ball into his own net, bringing the score back to 3–3.

With the championship now decided by Golden Goal, xSha found himself one-on-one with Abduction. He converted the chance to complete his hat-trick and give Smokin' Aces a 4–3 victory.

Deathwish had recovered from two separate deficits and briefly led in extra time before ultimately losing the final through Golden Goal.

Final Lineup (#20): Abduction • Müllewa • Jéal • Splat

Legacy

Deathwish had a brief competitive existence and is documented mainly because of its HaxBall Champions League #20 campaign.

The semi-final controversy against Angry Bulls and the 4–3 Golden Goal defeat to Smokin' Aces in the final are the principal recorded events associated with the team.
