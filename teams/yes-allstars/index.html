YeS AllStars was one of the leading teams of the Flash era in competitive HaxBall. The team is particularly remembered for reaching the final of HaxBall Champions League #11, with a lineup featuring Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Their defining appearance came against Angry Bulls in the HCL #11 final. YeS AllStars opened the scoring and controlled possession for periods of the match, but Angry Bulls became increasingly dangerous in attack and eventually won 5–2.

Overview

YeS AllStars established itself as one of the prominent teams of the Flash-era competitive scene, with its strongest recorded Champions League run coming in HaxBall Champions League #11. The four-player lineup combined the aggressive goalkeeping of Caccapupu with Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Among the lineup, Caccapupu was one of the most recognizable Flash-era goalkeepers and was known for an aggressive, high-pressure approach. Chro and DrG also became directly involved in one of the defining moments of the final when they combined for the opening goal.

HaxBall Champions League #11

YeS AllStars reached the championship match with a final lineup of Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, represented by Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Final Against Angry Bulls

YeS AllStars made the stronger start. DrG found Chro with a pass, and Chro finished cleanly into the corner to give YeS AllStars an early 1–0 lead.

Angry Bulls responded through Müllewa, who combined with Linas before finishing the return pass to make it 1–1.

For periods of the final, YeS AllStars saw more of the ball, but Angry Bulls consistently looked more dangerous when reaching the attacking end. That difference became increasingly important as the match progressed.

In the second half, the connection between ES Pierre and Müllewa shifted the match. Müllewa scored to make it 2–1, before Pierre finished another move to extend the lead to 3–1.

A mistake from Caccapupu then gave Müllewa the chance to complete his hat-trick and make the score 4–1.

YeS AllStars still responded. Fritz found Chro, who scored his second goal of the final to reduce the deficit to 4–2.

The final goal came from another direct battle between Müllewa and Caccapupu. After a spamming duel between the two, the ball eventually went in off Caccapupu for an own goal, completing a 5–2 victory for Angry Bulls.

The result left YeS AllStars as HaxBall Champions League #11 runners-up, while Angry Bulls secured the first Champions League title in their history.

Legacy

YeS AllStars is remembered as one of the notable teams of the Flash era, with its HCL #11 final run standing as the clearest recorded example of the team's competitive level.

The team's final lineup also brought together several recognizable Flash-era players. Caccapupu's aggressive goalkeeping, DrG and Chro's opening combination, and Chro's two-goal performance ensured that YeS AllStars remained competitive even as Angry Bulls gradually took control of the match.

Although the campaign ended without a championship, the HCL #11 runner-up finish remains the defining recorded achievement of YeS AllStars.
