Wicked Guys is a Turkish HaxBall team founded by twin brothers Buub and Bullas. The club remained active in the competitive scene as of 2026.

The greatest achievement in the club's history came in HaxBall Champions League #24, when Wicked Guys won the competition with a championship lineup of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Overview

Wicked Guys competed as part of the Turkish HaxBall scene and remained active through 2026.

While several players represented the club across different periods, its most important competitive success came during HaxBall Champions League #24.

HaxBall Champions League #24

The Club's First Championship

Wicked Guys reached the final of HaxBall Champions League #24 with Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Their opponents were Good Guys, whose final lineup consisted of gggggk, B ull, William and Torment.

The decisive moment arrived during the opening minutes of the first half. Taziek found space for a shot and placed the ball into the corner, giving Wicked Guys an early 1–0 lead.

Following the goal, the match remained closely contested. Neither team was able to establish complete control, and the narrow scoreline kept the championship open throughout the final.

Wicked Guys protected their advantage and completed the difficult final with a 1–0 victory, securing the club's first HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Roster (#24): Wiagoal • Vichoexe • Taziek • Makenzie
