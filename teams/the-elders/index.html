The Elders was a competitive HaxBall team best remembered for winning HaxBall Champions League #18.

The championship lineup consisted of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak. In the Grand Final, The Elders defeated Seeders 3–2 after a dramatic comeback that was ultimately decided by Golden Goal.

The final became the defining match in The Elders' history, with the team recovering from two separate deficits before SAKY scored the championship-winning goal only 15 seconds into Golden Goal.

Overview

The Elders' place in competitive HaxBall history is primarily defined by the club's championship run in HaxBall Champions League #18.

The team entered the decisive stages of the tournament with a lineup of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak, eventually reaching the Grand Final against Seeders.

Their championship was secured through a final in which The Elders repeatedly found themselves under pressure. Despite going behind twice after regulation time, the team responded on both occasions before completing the comeback in Golden Goal.

HaxBall Champions League

The Elders became HaxBall Champions League winners in Edition #18, securing the only Champions League title currently recorded in the club's history.

HaxBall Champions League #18: Fifteen Seconds to Glory

The Elders reached the HaxBall Champions League #18 Grand Final with LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak, where they faced Seeders.

The match remained scoreless throughout regular time, with neither team able to find the breakthrough. The championship therefore moved into extra time at 0–0.

Seeders struck first. Marble created the opening for Yannex, who converted the opportunity to put The Elders 1–0 behind.

The Elders responded through Vak. From a corner-spam situation, Vak found the equalizer and brought the score back to 1–1.

Seeders then regained the advantage. Pressure around the corner produced a deflection that carried the ball into the net, restoring their lead at 2–1 and placing The Elders behind for a second time.

With less than two minutes remaining, The Elders produced another response. Vak became directly involved again, providing the assist for SAKY, who scored the late 2–2 equalizer and forced the championship into Golden Goal.

The deciding period lasted only seconds. Just 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY scored again, completing the comeback and giving The Elders a dramatic 3–2 victory.

Vak had contributed directly to two of the team's three goals, scoring the first equalizer and assisting the second. SAKY then delivered both the late 2–2 goal and the championship-winning Golden Goal.

Legacy

The Elders' legacy is centered around its HaxBall Champions League #18 championship and one of the most dramatic finals of that edition.

The championship lineup of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak showed considerable resilience in the final, recovering after falling behind twice in extra time.

Vak played a central role in keeping the comeback alive with a goal and an assist, while SAKY became the decisive figure in the closing stages by scoring both the late equalizer and the Golden Goal winner.

The image most closely associated with The Elders' championship remains the final moment itself: after a long match that had remained goalless throughout regulation, the title was ultimately decided only 15 seconds into Golden Goal.

That 3–2 victory over Seeders permanently secured The Elders a place among the HaxBall Champions League's championship teams.
