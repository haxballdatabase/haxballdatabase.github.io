Antartic Lions was a HaxBall team active during the Flash era, best known for reaching consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals in Editions #8 and #9.

The club won HaxBall Champions League #8 before returning to the championship match one edition later in Edition #9, where it finished as runner-up to Stiff Wind.

The two consecutive final appearances gave Antartic Lions one of the stronger short-term European records of its period, with a championship followed immediately by another run to the final.

Overview

Antartic Lions' documented competitive peak came across two consecutive HaxBall Champions League editions during the Flash era.

The club first became European champion in Edition #8 with a lineup built around tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer, with Yannex later entering from the bench.

One edition later, Antartic Lions reached the final again. By that point tonnnek had moved to Stiff Wind, creating the unusual situation in which the goalkeeper from Antartic Lions' championship lineup faced his former club in the next HaxBall Champions League final.

HaxBall Champions League History

Antartic Lions reached two consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals, recording one championship and one runner-up finish.

The club won Edition #8 and then returned immediately in Edition #9. Its attempt to defend the championship ended with a narrow 2–1 defeat against Stiff Wind.

HaxBall Champions League #8: Champions

Antartic Lions won HaxBall Champions League #8, securing the first documented HaxBall Champions League title in the club's history.

The championship lineup began with tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer. Yannex later entered the match from the bench.

The surviving HaxBall Database archive preserves the championship result and lineup, but the final opponent is not currently documented. A replay of the Edition #8 final has also not been archived.

HaxBall Champions League #9: A Second Consecutive Final

Antartic Lions returned to the final in HaxBall Champions League #9, attempting to defend the championship it had won one edition earlier.

The opponent was Stiff Wind, whose final lineup consisted of tonnnek, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Catrice.

The final carried an unusual connection between the two clubs. tonnnek had been the goalkeeper in Antartic Lions' Edition #8 championship lineup before moving to Stiff Wind and facing his former team in the very next final.

Stiff Wind opened the scoring through Catrice. Antartic Lions responded when YaaDon created an opportunity for Meyer, who converted the equalizer.

Stiff Wind later regained the lead and moved ahead 2–1. During the closing stages, R9 remained deep and concentrated on protecting Stiff Wind's advantage, limiting Antartic Lions' opportunities to find another equalizer.

The score remained unchanged, leaving Antartic Lions with a 2–1 defeat and a runner-up finish one edition after winning the competition.

Legacy

Antartic Lions' historical record is defined by its consecutive HaxBall Champions League final appearances during the Flash era.

Winning Edition #8 gave the club a major European championship, while reaching the final again in Edition #9 demonstrated that the title was followed by another sustained tournament run rather than an isolated result.

The transition between the two finals also created one of the more unusual links between consecutive editions: tonnnek won the championship with Antartic Lions in #8, then moved to Stiff Wind and defeated his former club in the #9 final.

With one championship and one runner-up finish from consecutive editions, Antartic Lions remains a notable Flash-era team in the HaxBall Champions League archive.
