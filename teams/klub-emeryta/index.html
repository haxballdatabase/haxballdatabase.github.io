Klub Emeryta was a successful European HaxBall team that competed at the highest level across both domestic league competition and the HaxBall Champions League.

The club enjoyed considerable success in the Feed Me League, winning Division 1 in Seasons 19 and 22. Those championships demonstrated Klub Emeryta's ability to perform consistently across full league campaigns.

Klub Emeryta also became a regular presence on the European stage, reaching the HaxBall Champions League final three times in Editions #14, #16 and #17. The club ultimately became European champion in Edition #17.

Across those campaigns, Klub Emeryta featured several high-level players from different generations, including kojo, aguero10, ANDREA, Liebe, DrG, SAKY and xSha. Its ability to rebuild and remain competitive with changing lineups became an important part of the club's history.

Overview

Klub Emeryta established itself as a regular presence near the top of competitive HaxBall, combining domestic success with repeated appearances at the highest level of European competition.

Its two Feed Me Division 1 championships demonstrated the team's strength over longer league campaigns, while three HaxBall Champions League finals confirmed its ability to compete with Europe's strongest teams.

The club's European record developed an unusual pattern. Klub Emeryta reached the final in Editions #14, #16 and #17, and remarkably, all three finals finished 3–2 after Golden Goal.

Those appearances came with significantly different lineups. Players such as Liebe, DrG, aguero10, ANDREA, Per, Ter, SAKY and xSha represented different versions of the team, while kojo provided continuity by appearing in all three HaxBall Champions League finals.

Feed Me League

Klub Emeryta was also a successful and well-known team in the Feed Me League, one of the major domestic competitions of the period.

The club won Division 1 twice, becoming champions in Season 19 and again in Season 22.

Klub Emeryta also reached the Feed Me Cup final three times, finishing as runners-up on each occasion. Together with its two Division 1 championships, those repeated deep cup runs underlined the club's regular presence near the top of Feed Me competition.

HaxBall Champions League

Klub Emeryta reached three HaxBall Champions League finals across four editions, appearing in #14, #16 and #17.

The club finished as runner-up in its first two appearances before finally winning the competition in Edition #17.

HaxBall Champions League #14

Klub Emeryta reached the first of its three HaxBall Champions League finals in Edition #14, facing Omertà.

The Klub Emeryta lineup featured kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, with ANDREA also entering the match during the second half. Omertà fielded Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

Klub Emeryta struck first late in the opening half. DrG found the breakthrough and gave the team a 1–0 lead.

Omertà responded early in the second half. A loose rebound fell to Mamba, who finished into the corner to make it 1–1.

Shortly afterwards, pressure around the Klub Emeryta goal created another loose ball. Mamba reacted first again and scored his second goal, turning the final around and putting Omertà 2–1 ahead.

Klub Emeryta responded quickly. Following an intercepted pass after a mistake from Pringles, Liebe converted the opportunity to level the final at 2–2.

The match remained tied through the main period of extra time and eventually moved into Golden Goal.

There, a physical battle between DrG and Deliric produced the decisive moment. Deliric managed to finish the opportunity, giving Omertà a 3–2 victory and leaving Klub Emeryta narrowly short of the championship.

It was Klub Emeryta's first appearance in a HaxBall Champions League final and the beginning of a European run that would continue across the following editions.

Final Lineup (#14): kojo • Liebe • DrG • Echo
Second-half substitute: ANDREA

HaxBall Champions League #16

Two editions later, Klub Emeryta returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with a very different lineup consisting of Per, kojo, ANDREA and aguero10.

Their opponents were Stiff Wind, whose lineup featured PiperSalvia, ROCKY BALBOA, Altidore and Catrice.

Klub Emeryta made an excellent start. In the sixth minute of the first half, ANDREA found aguero10, who placed the ball into the corner to open the scoring.

A mistake from Stiff Wind goalkeeper PiperSalvia soon gave aguero10 another opportunity. He punished the error and scored his second goal, putting Klub Emeryta into a commanding 2–0 lead.

For much of the opening period, the final appeared to be firmly under Klub Emeryta's control. That changed dramatically near the end of the first half.

Altidore found Catrice, who finished to reduce the score to 2–1. Before Klub Emeryta could settle again, Catrice returned the favour and assisted Altidore for the equalizer.

Stiff Wind had scored twice in less than a minute, turning a 2–0 deficit into a 2–2 draw and completely changing the momentum of the final.

The second half produced no further goals, sending the match into Golden Goal.

There, Catrice won the decisive physical battle and scored the final goal of the match. Stiff Wind completed the comeback with a 3–2 victory, leaving Klub Emeryta one goal away from the championship for the second time.

The defeat was particularly difficult because of how strongly Klub Emeryta had started. A two-goal advantage had disappeared in less than a minute before the team suffered another narrow final loss.

Final Lineup (#16): Per • kojo • ANDREA • aguero10

HaxBall Champions League #17: Redemption

One edition after the Golden Goal defeat to Stiff Wind, Klub Emeryta returned to the final for the second consecutive season. It was also the club's third HaxBall Champions League final in four editions.

Once again, the team had changed considerably. kojo remained from the previous campaigns, but the new lineup around him featured Ter and the rapidly rising attacking duo of SAKY and xSha.

Klub Emeryta entered the final as the underdog against BLACKOUT, a team built around the established Seeders core. SAKY and xSha had been competing in Division 2 only a few seasons earlier, while their opponents already possessed extensive experience at the highest level.

BLACKOUT controlled much of the early match through its possession-heavy approach. That control eventually produced the opening goal when Isco found Yannex, who finished the move to give BLACKOUT a 1–0 lead.

Klub Emeryta responded in a very different way. Rather than trying to match BLACKOUT's possession game, they punished them on the counter. SAKY found xSha, who converted the opportunity to bring the final level at 1–1.

SAKY then produced another major moment. Using his strength in a corner-spam situation, he forced the ball through and gave Klub Emeryta a 2–1 lead.

BLACKOUT refused to disappear. With less than a minute remaining, aguero10 found Isco, who scored the equalizer and sent the final into Golden Goal.

For the third time in three HaxBall Champions League finals, Klub Emeryta found itself entering Golden Goal at 2–2. The previous two occasions had ended in defeat. This time, the club finally found the winning goal.

Klub Emeryta broke forward quickly and xSha found himself in the type of one-on-one attacking situation that would later become closely associated with his career. He converted the opportunity and scored the Golden Goal.

The finish completed a 3–2 victory and made Klub Emeryta HaxBall Champions League champions.

After two previous Golden Goal final defeats, Klub Emeryta had returned once again with a largely rebuilt and less-favoured lineup and finally completed its redemption.

For kojo, the victory carried an additional layer of meaning. He had appeared in all three Klub Emeryta finals, suffering defeats in Editions #14 and #16 before finally lifting the trophy in Edition #17.

The championship also became an early landmark for SAKY and xSha. Both had been Division 2 players only a few seasons earlier, yet they had now played central roles in defeating one of the strongest and most established teams of the period.

Championship Lineup (#17): kojo • Ter • SAKY • xSha

Legacy

Klub Emeryta is remembered as a successful club of its competitive period, with achievements across both the Feed Me League and the HaxBall Champions League.

Its Division 1 championships in Feed Me Seasons 19 and 22 established a strong domestic record, while three HaxBall Champions League final appearances demonstrated the club's ability to repeatedly challenge at the highest European level.

The European finals produced a remarkable sequence: all three finished 3–2 after Golden Goal. Klub Emeryta lost the first two against Omertà and Stiff Wind before xSha's Golden Goal against BLACKOUT finally delivered the championship in Edition #17.

kojo connected all three final teams, while the players around him changed considerably across the different campaigns. That continuity through multiple versions of the squad became an important part of Klub Emeryta's European story.

With two Feed Me Division 1 championships, three HaxBall Champions League finals and a European title in Edition #17, Klub Emeryta built a strong record across multiple forms of competitive HaxBall.
