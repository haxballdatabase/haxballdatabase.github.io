Angry Bulls is one of the most successful and historically significant teams in competitive HaxBall.

The club won the HaxBall Champions League three times, with its championships coming in Edition 11, 15 and 19. Across different generations of competitive HaxBall, Angry Bulls developed a reputation for strong team play, competitive mentality and success on the biggest stage.

Two of the most important figures in the club's history were ES Pierre and Müllewa. Pierre served as captain during some of Angry Bulls' most successful periods and became closely associated with the club's leadership and organization, while Müllewa established himself as one of the great strikers of his generation and an Angry Bulls legend.

Angry Bulls' history is also closely connected with players such as Abduction, Linas, Liéds, Misaj, Necro and nova, with different generations contributing to the club's long Champions League history.

Overview

Angry Bulls built much of its identity around a core of players who remained connected to the club across different periods. At the centre of the early years were captain ES Pierre and striker Müllewa, two players whose partnership became an important part of the team's first major successes.

Pierre and Müllewa consistently understood each other well on the pitch. Pierre's organized and team-oriented approach complemented Müllewa's instinctive attacking game. Their chemistry was particularly visible during the club's early championship years, when combinations between the two regularly created some of Angry Bulls' most dangerous attacks.

Müllewa in particular became an Angry Bulls legend. Known for his ability to score from unusual situations and provide goals without always needing perfectly constructed chances, he gave Angry Bulls a constant attacking threat and played a major role in the club's first two HaxBall Champions League titles.

Later generations added players such as Misaj, Liéds and Necro, allowing Angry Bulls to remain competitive across multiple eras and eventually reach the top of the competition's all-time title standings.

HaxBall Champions League

Angry Bulls' history is particularly closely connected with the HaxBall Champions League. The club's three championships came across different periods of the competition, establishing Angry Bulls as one of its most successful teams.

HaxBall Champions League #11: The First Title

HaxBall Champions League #11 marked the beginning of Angry Bulls' Champions League legacy. In the final, they faced Yes Allstars, whose lineup consisted of Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Yes Allstars struck first. DrG found Chro with a pass, and Chro placed the ball cleanly into the corner to give his team a 1–0 lead.

Angry Bulls responded through Müllewa. After an excellent exchange between Müllewa and Linas, Linas played a through ball back into his path. Müllewa finished the move perfectly to make it 1–1. The attack had effectively been started and finished by Müllewa himself.

The match developed an interesting rhythm. Yes Allstars saw more of the ball for periods of the game, but Angry Bulls looked more dangerous whenever they reached the attacking end. They made far better use of their opportunities, and the understanding between ES Pierre and Müllewa became increasingly visible as the match developed.

That connection produced another goal in the second half. Pierre and Müllewa combined in attack, and Müllewa once again found the finish to put Angry Bulls 2–1 ahead.

Only a short time later, the roles were reversed. Müllewa sent the ball into the danger area and ES Pierre finished the move, extending the lead to 3–1. The two goals were a clear example of the understanding the pair had developed on the pitch.

Yes Allstars then began to lose control of the match. A mistake from Caccapupu gave Müllewa another opportunity, and the striker punished the error to score his third goal of the final and make it 4–1.

Yes Allstars managed to pull one back when Fritz found Chro, who scored his second goal of the match to reduce the deficit to 4–2. By that stage, however, there was little time remaining.

The final goal came following a spamming battle between Müllewa and Caccapupu. The ball eventually went in off Caccapupu for an own goal, setting the final score at 5–2.

The championship lineup consisted of Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa. Müllewa finished the final with a hat-trick and was involved in several of Angry Bulls' most dangerous attacks, while his understanding with Pierre became one of the clearest features of the match.

The victory gave Angry Bulls their first HaxBall Champions League title.

HaxBall Champions League #15: Return to Glory

At HaxBall Champions League #15, Angry Bulls returned to Champions League glory after a four-edition gap.

Four editions after their first title, Angry Bulls brought Misaj into the team. His arrival added another major player to the lineup, while ES Pierre continued to lead the side as captain and Müllewa remained one of its main attacking threats.

In the final, Angry Bulls faced fellow Turkish side Skins. The connection between Misaj and Müllewa became one of the key features of the match. Misaj provided two assists, while Müllewa scored two goals. His second arrived shortly after the first, quickly giving Angry Bulls a 2–0 lead.

Angry Bulls eventually won the match 3–1, securing their second HaxBall Champions League title. For Müllewa, the victory made him a two-time champion, while Misaj won the competition for the first time.

The championship lineup was Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Angry Bulls also achieved another important success with the same lineup, winning ESL 2017.

HaxBall Champions League #19: Reaching the Summit

HaxBall Champions League #19 became another major moment for Angry Bulls.

After a period away from the game, Angry Bulls returned to the Champions League and once again managed to reach the final.

In the semi-final, Angry Bulls defeated Trailer Park Penguins (TPP), the former name of Smokin' Aces, to secure their place in the final.

The victory set up a final against Stiff Wind, who entered the match holding the all-time record with three Champions League titles. Once again, Angry Bulls faced another Turkish team in the final. Angry Bulls were attempting to win their third trophy and equal Stiff Wind's record.

The two clubs had developed into major rivals, making the final a meeting between two of the competition's strongest teams.

Angry Bulls had originally expected Pringles to be available for the final, but he was unable to attend. As a result, reserve player nova stepped into goal. Despite the late change, nova produced a good performance in the final. He also had a good relationship with Misaj, who helped give him confidence ahead of the match.

Angry Bulls initially fell 1–0 behind.

During the match, Misaj produced one of the key moments of the comeback. Using his trademark "X-fake", he created an opening and provided the assist for Necro, who scored the equalizer.

The match remained closely contested and eventually went into extra time.

In extra time, Misaj scored the winning goal to complete the comeback and secure the championship for Angry Bulls.

The victory gave Angry Bulls their third HaxBall Champions League title and brought the club level with Stiff Wind at the top of the competition's all-time championship standings.

HaxBall Champions League #20: The Turning Point

HaxBall Champions League #20 marked a major turning point in the history of Angry Bulls.

During the semi-final against Deathwish, Angry Bulls goalkeeper Liéds experienced a ping problem which resulted in a goal being conceded. Angry Bulls expected their opponents to respond with fair play, but Deathwish continued the match.

The situation became particularly important because the Deathwish lineup included former Angry Bulls players Müllewa and Abduction, as well as Jéal, one of Misaj's closest friends.

Despite Misaj producing a performance of 3 goals and 1 assist, Angry Bulls lost the semi-final.

Following the match, Misaj created and edited a video concerning the incident. Tensions between Misaj and several Deathwish players increased afterwards, and Angry Bulls subsequently shut down.

Approximately one to two months later, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction. Together, they decided to bring Angry Bulls back and rebuild the team for another campaign.

By that point, Misaj's relationship with several figures around the revived team, particularly Müllewa, had already deteriorated badly. Seeing Pierre and Müllewa reunite and restore Angry Bulls widened that divide even further.

Only after Angry Bulls had been revived did Misaj take control of Slappers and begin building a team of his own. The purpose behind the project was personal and direct: Misaj wanted to defeat Angry Bulls.

What had started with the fallout of the previous season had now become a genuine rivalry, with the former teammates moving forward on opposite sides.

HaxBall Champions League #21: A Twist of Fate

HaxBall Champions League #21 took the rivalry in a direction that neither side could have guaranteed when the tournament began.

ES Pierre, Müllewa and Abduction had already reunited and brought Angry Bulls back. Their decision to rebuild the club became the final spark for Misaj, whose relationship with Müllewa had already collapsed after the previous season.

Misaj then took control of Slappers and assembled a new team with Angry Bulls specifically in his sights. His mission was clear: he wanted to beat the revived Angry Bulls.

There was no guarantee, however, that the two teams would ever actually meet. Both still had to make their way through the HaxBall Champions League. Slappers reached the final after defeating Smokin' Aces, the reigning champions, while Angry Bulls advanced through the other side of the tournament.

The fact that the two teams eventually met in the final felt almost like a twist of fate. Only one season earlier, Misaj had still been part of Angry Bulls' recent history. Now Pierre and Müllewa had restored the club, while Misaj had built another team specifically to bring it down.

What had begun as a breakdown between former teammates had somehow become a HaxBall Champions League final.

On one side stood the revived Angry Bulls, once again built around ES Pierre, Müllewa and Abduction. On the other stood Misaj and the Slappers team created after the split.

Angry Bulls entered the final with an imposing record. The club had already won the HaxBall Champions League three times and had never previously lost a final.

Misaj had once been part of Angry Bulls' championship history. By HaxBall Champions League #21, he had become the figure standing directly against it.

Slappers immediately took control of the match. They raced into a 3–0 lead within the opening three minutes, putting Angry Bulls under enormous pressure almost from the start.

The match eventually ended 7–2 in favour of Slappers, handing Angry Bulls their first-ever defeat in a HaxBall Champions League final.

For Misaj, the victory completed the revenge story that had begun after the previous season. Pierre and Müllewa had reunited and revived Angry Bulls; Misaj had answered by building Slappers with the purpose of defeating them. Against the odds, the two paths had eventually led to the same final.

The result turned HaxBall Champions League #21 into far more than another championship match. Former teammates had separated, built opposing sides and eventually found themselves facing each other in the biggest match of the competition.

Legacy

Angry Bulls' legacy is built around its three HaxBall Champions League titles and the players who represented the club across several different periods.

Its championships in Edition 11, 15 and 19 came with different lineups, allowing Angry Bulls to remain successful while the roster changed over time.

ES Pierre and Müllewa became two of the central figures of the club's early identity. Pierre's leadership helped organize and hold the team together, while Müllewa's goals made him one of Angry Bulls' most important attacking players and ultimately an Angry Bulls legend.

Their understanding on the pitch was particularly visible in the HaxBall Champions League #11 final. Müllewa scored three times, Pierre also found the net, and several of Angry Bulls' most dangerous attacks came through combinations involving the pair.

Around them, players such as Abduction, Linas and later Liéds helped form different successful versions of the team.

The club's history later became closely connected with Misaj. His involvement in the #15 and #19 championship teams made him an important part of Angry Bulls' success, but the events surrounding #20 changed that relationship completely.

After Angry Bulls shut down, Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction and brought the club back. Misaj, whose relationship with Müllewa had already deteriorated, responded by building Slappers with the intention of defeating the revived team.

Their eventual meeting in the HaxBall Champions League #21 final became one of the strangest turns in the club's history: a former Angry Bulls champion had created a new team to defeat the club, and the two sides ultimately met in the final itself.

Angry Bulls' third Champions League title was also historically important because it brought the club level with Stiff Wind at the top of the all-time championship standings at the time.

With three championships, several generations of notable players and a history filled with major finals, partnerships and rivalries, Angry Bulls remains one of the most important clubs in HaxBall Champions League history.
