THE COMPLETE COMPETITIVE DATABASE & ARCHIVE

Welcome to HaxBall Database, a comprehensive competitive database and archive dedicated to documenting the players, teams, tournaments, statistics, records and stories that shaped European HaxBall.

From detailed player careers and championship lineups to historic finals, rankings, records and major rivalries, this platform is built to serve as a permanent reference point for HaxBall's competitive scene.

⭐ Built & Verified by Elite Players

HaxBall Database is curated using direct input, historical archives and first-hand knowledge from elite players across multiple generations of competitive HaxBall. Their experience helps preserve player achievements, match details, team histories and important competitive moments with greater accuracy and context.

⚖️ Objective Database & Records

HaxBall Database operates as an objective, evidence-based database and historical archive.

Player careers, championship lineups, tournament placements, match results and statistical records are documented using available replays, competition records and historical sources. The goal is to preserve an accurate and unbiased record of competitive HaxBall.

Preserving records, statistics and history without distortion.

📊 Stats, Rosters & Stories

Statistics preserve the record, while context explains how those records were created.

HaxBall Database connects information such as HaxBall Champions League titles, goals, assists, clean sheets, final appearances and championship squads with the rivalries, tactical changes and major matches behind them.

Whether documenting a player's career or reconstructing the history of a championship team, each record is preserved with its competitive context.

🏛️ The HaxBall Competitive Archive

Across more than a decade of competitive play, thousands of players have competed across different teams, leagues and tournaments, leaving behind trophies, statistics and memorable performances.

By organizing these records into a searchable database, HaxBall Database aims to keep player achievements, team histories, championship squads, tournament results and major competitive moments permanently accessible to the community.

ℹ️ About HaxBall

THE GAME BEHIND THE RECORDS

First released in December 2010, HaxBall was originally implemented using the Flash Player and used RTMFP to create peer-to-peer connections in a way similar to WebRTC.

Following the discontinuation of Flash Player, the game successfully transitioned to HTML5, continuing its legacy as one of the most popular browser-based competitive multiplayer games.
