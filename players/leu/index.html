Leu is a HaxBall player regarded as one of the best DMs of his era and one of the central figures in the history of Pas De Nom.

As the captain of Pas De Nom, Leu became closely associated with one of the most recognizable Turkish teams of the Flash era. His entire known club career was spent with Pas De Nom, making his name inseparable from the team's competitive history.

Leu reached two HaxBall Champions League finals with the club. He captained Pas De Nom to the championship in Edition #7, defeating rivals Angels of Death 4–1, before the team reached another final five editions later in Edition #12, finishing runner-up to Skins.

His career was also closely connected with Tino. Both players spent their known club careers exclusively with Pas De Nom and remained part of the team's core across the two major European runs that defined the club's history.

Biography

Leu established himself as one of the leading DMs of the middle years of the Flash era and built his entire known club career around Pas De Nom.

Rather than moving between several major teams, Leu remained associated with one club throughout his competitive career. This made him one of the players most closely identified with Pas De Nom and its distinctive place in Flash-era HaxBall.

His importance to the team went beyond simply occupying the DM position. Leu served as captain and was part of the core that helped Pas De Nom become a HaxBall Champions League champion and reach another final five editions later.

During his active years, Leu was regarded as one of the strongest DMs of his era, competing during a period that contained several highly regarded players in the position.

Pas De Nom

Leu's career is almost impossible to separate from the history of Pas De Nom.

The club became one of the most recognizable teams of its period, combining strong individual players with a distinctive tactical identity. Pas De Nom became particularly associated with Dropball Jukes, integrating the technique into its collective attacking play during the Flash era.

Leu occupied the DM position within this structure and served as the team's captain. Rather than being remembered through several different club spells, his competitive identity remained centered on Pas De Nom from beginning to end.

The same was true of Tino, one of the most talented attacking players of the era. Leu and Tino both spent their known club careers exclusively with Pas De Nom, becoming two of the names most consistently associated with the team.

Their continuity became particularly visible in Europe. Both were present during the club's championship run in Edition #7 and remained part of the team when Pas De Nom reached another HaxBall Champions League final in Edition #12.

Captaincy

Leu served as the captain of Pas De Nom during the club's competitive years.

His captaincy placed him at the center of a team containing several notable players, including Tino, Kranjcar, Guti.Haz and others who appeared across Pas De Nom's major European campaigns.

The role matched his position on the pitch. As a DM, Leu operated in an area of the game that connected the defensive and attacking parts of the team, while his position as captain gave him an equally central place within the identity of Pas De Nom itself.

The club's two HaxBall Champions League finals ultimately became the clearest competitive record of his period as captain: a championship in Edition #7 followed by a runner-up finish in Edition #12.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Leu reached the HaxBall Champions League final twice, both times with Pas De Nom.

His first final ended with the club's greatest achievement, a 4–1 victory over Angels of Death in Edition #7.

Five editions later, Pas De Nom reached the HaxBall Champions League final again in Edition #12, with Leu still part of the team's core. Their second championship match ended differently, as Skins won 4–1 and prevented Pas De Nom from securing a second European title.

HaxBall Champions League #7: The Rivalry Decided

Pas De Nom reached the final of Edition #7 against Angels of Death, one of the team's major Turkish rivals.

The final represented the biggest meeting between two clubs whose rivalry had become one of the notable conflicts of Turkish HaxBall during the period. With the HaxBall Champions League championship at stake, the match offered both sides an opportunity to settle that competition on the largest stage available to them.

The match carried additional tension because of the circumstances surrounding the host selection. Seven of the eight players involved were Turkish, while one was Portuguese. Pas De Nom requested that the game be played on a Turkish host, but Angels of Death rejected the proposal, with administrators eventually ruling that the final would take place on a European host.

Pas De Nom also had to adjust its expected lineup. Kranjcar had played during the earlier stages of the campaign but was unavailable for the final, leading to Adékyz joining the team. Tino, normally a striker, was moved into midfield.

The final lineup consisted of Guti.Haz, Leu, Tino and Adékyz.

Pas De Nom established a 2–0 lead before Angels of Death responded through Dark to make the score 2–1.

Leu then became directly involved in one of the decisive moves of the final. After a battle with R9, he recovered the ball and found Tino in midfield. Tino immediately moved the play forward to Adékyz, who completed his hat-trick and restored Pas De Nom's two-goal advantage at 3–1.

The team later added a fourth through Tino, completing a convincing 4–1 victory.

The result gave Pas De Nom its first HaxBall Champions League championship and provided the club with its defining victory over Angels of Death. For Leu, it represented the peak of his career as Pas De Nom's captain and DM.

Championship Lineup (#7): Guti.Haz • Leu • Tino • Adékyz

HaxBall Champions League #12: One Step from a Second Crown

Five editions after winning the competition, Pas De Nom reached the HaxBall Champions League final again.

The Edition #12 lineup consisted of tonnnek, Leu, Kranjcar and Tino. Leu and Tino remained important figures from the earlier championship side, while Kranjcar was available for the final after having missed the Edition #7 championship match.

Their opponent was another Turkish team, Skins, who fielded Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Skins took control of the match through Catrice, who scored all four of his team's goals. Pas De Nom managed to pull one goal back during the second half, but could not complete a comeback.

Catrice scored his fourth goal in the final seconds, immediately ending the match and completing a 4–1 victory for Skins.

The result left Leu and Pas De Nom one victory short of becoming two-time HaxBall Champions League champions.

Final Lineup (#12): tonnnek • Leu • Kranjcar • Tino

Playing Style

Leu played primarily as a DM and was regarded as one of the best players in the position during his era.

His role placed him at the center of Pas De Nom's structure, operating behind the team's more attacking players while remaining involved in the development of possession.

One surviving example of this role can be seen in the Edition #7 final. After competing with R9 for possession, Leu moved the ball into Tino in midfield, beginning the sequence that produced Pas De Nom's decisive third goal.

He also played within a Pas De Nom side known for an unconventional tactical identity, particularly its use of Dropball Jukes. The team's willingness to use unusual techniques became one of the characteristics that separated it from many other Flash-era sides.

Unusual Controls

One of the more unusual details of Leu's playing career was his choice of movement controls.

During an era when the large majority of competitive HaxBall players used the arrow keys for movement, Leu instead played using W, A, S and D.

The difference had no direct connection to his role as a DM, but it became a distinctive personal detail in an era when control setups among high-level players were generally much more standardized.

Legacy

Leu is remembered as one of the leading DMs of his era and one of the players most closely associated with Pas De Nom.

Unlike many prominent players whose careers passed through several major clubs, Leu's known competitive career remained entirely connected to one team. Together with Tino, he became part of a small core whose identity was inseparable from Pas De Nom.

As captain, he led the club through the most important period in its history. Pas De Nom won HaxBall Champions League #7, defeating major rival Angels of Death 4–1, and reached another final in Edition #12 five editions later.

His place within the team was also linked to Pas De Nom's unusual tactical character. The club became known for experimenting with techniques such as Dropball Jukes, while Leu himself stood out even in something as fundamental as his controls, competing at the highest level using WASD rather than the arrow-key setup used by most players of the era.

His championship, captaincy, two European finals and standing as one of the strongest DMs of his era made Leu one of the central names in the history of Pas De Nom and an important player of the middle Flash period.
