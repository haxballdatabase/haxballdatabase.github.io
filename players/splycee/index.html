Biography

Splycee is a Turkish player who primarily played as a striker. He was also used as a DM, giving him the ability to contribute in both attacking and deeper roles.

His clearest documented major tournament appearance came with FEAR in HaxBall Champions League #22.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #22

Splycee was part of the FEAR squad that reached the HaxBall Champions League #22 final.

FEAR lost the final, leaving Splycee and the team as HaxBall Champions League #22 runners-up.
