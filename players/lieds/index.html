Liéds is a Turkish HaxBall goalkeeper regarded as one of the greatest goalkeepers and one of the most successful players in HaxBall history, particularly during the Flash era. His strongest period came before the transition to HTML5, when he established himself among the leading goalkeepers of his generation while collecting major titles across both club and international competition.

Liéds won the HaxBall Champions League three times, remarkably doing so with three different teams: Skins in #12, Stiff Wind in #13 and Angry Bulls in #15. With three championships, Liéds holds the all-time record for the most HaxBall Champions League titles won by a goalkeeper.

His success extended far beyond club competition. Liéds also won four national-team championships with Turkey: the 2016 Nations Cup, 2017 Nations Cup, ESL 2017 and 2019 Nations Cup. Across his documented HaxBall Champions League and national-team record, he collected seven major championships.

With four national-team titles, Liéds holds the second-highest national-team championship total in HaxBall history, behind only Misaj. His achievements therefore stand out not only among goalkeepers, but among players across all positions.

The transition to HTML5 represented a very different period of his club career. Liéds was unable to reproduce the individual level and sustained club success that had defined him on Flash, although he still added one major achievement in the new version of the game by winning the 2019 Nations Cup with Turkey.

Biography

Liéds established his reputation during the Flash era, where he became one of the leading goalkeepers in European HaxBall.

Unlike players whose major success became tied to one particular lineup, Liéds achieved at the highest level with several different teams. His HaxBall Champions League championships came with Skins, Stiff Wind and Angry Bulls, giving his career an unusual level of variety at the top of European competition.

His most successful club period came between HaxBall Champions League Editions #12 and #15. Across those four editions, Liéds won the competition three times and became part of championship lineups containing several of the most recognizable players of the Flash era.

Those three victories also established an all-time positional record. No goalkeeper has won more HaxBall Champions League titles than Liéds, whose three championships remain the highest total achieved by a player in the position.

He became particularly associated with both Stiff Wind and Angry Bulls, two teams that would later stand among the most successful clubs in HaxBall Champions League history.

Liéds' achievements were exceptional not only for a goalkeeper, but also when compared with players across all positions. Alongside his three HaxBall Champions League titles, he accumulated four championships with the Turkish national team.

That combination of European and international success made Liéds one of the most decorated and successful players of his generation.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Liéds won three HaxBall Champions League championships during the Flash era.

His titles came with three separate teams: Skins in #12, Stiff Wind in #13 and Angry Bulls in #15. Winning with different lineups became one of the strongest features of his European record.

The sequence also demonstrated the level of success Liéds maintained during his prime. After winning with Skins, he immediately followed it with another championship at Stiff Wind before later adding a third title with Angry Bulls.

His three championships gave Liéds a unique place in the competition's history: no goalkeeper has won more HaxBall Champions League titles. He remains the most successful goalkeeper in the competition by championship count.

The fact that each of those championships came with a different club further strengthened the record, showing that his European success was not tied to one dominant team.

HaxBall Champions League #12: Champion with Skins

Liéds won his first HaxBall Champions League title with Skins in Edition #12.

Skins reached the final with a lineup consisting of Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice, with Liéds occupying the goalkeeper position.

Their opponents were Pas De Nom, who fielded Tonnnek, Leu, Kranjcar and Tino.

Skins took control of the final and eventually secured a convincing 4–1 victory. Catrice scored all four Skins goals as the team claimed the championship.

The victory gave Liéds his first HaxBall Champions League title and began the most successful stretch of his European career.

Championship Lineup (#12): Liéds • Gilbert • Elysion • Catrice

HaxBall Champions League #13: Back-to-Back Champion

One edition later, Liéds moved into the championship lineup of Stiff Wind.

The team lined up with Liéds at GK, Liebe at DM, ROCKY BALBOA at AM and Altidore at ST.

Stiff Wind survived a difficult semi-final against Gladiators, winning 1–0 after Altidore converted the decisive opportunity.

In the final, Stiff Wind faced Lux Aeterna. The team won 3–1 to capture its second HaxBall Champions League championship.

For Liéds, the result meant back-to-back HaxBall Champions League titles with two different teams.

Championship Lineup (#13): Liéds • Liebe • ROCKY BALBOA • Altidore

HaxBall Champions League #15: A Third Team, A Third Title

Liéds added his third HaxBall Champions League championship in Edition #15, this time representing Angry Bulls.

The championship lineup consisted of Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

In an interesting connection to Liéds' earlier career, Angry Bulls faced his previous championship team Skins in the final.

Angry Bulls won the match 3–1, giving the club its second HaxBall Champions League championship and Liéds his third individual title.

The achievement meant that Liéds had now become HaxBall Champions League champion with Skins, Stiff Wind and Angry Bulls.

The third championship also moved Liéds into outright record territory among goalkeepers, giving him the highest HaxBall Champions League title total ever achieved by a GK.

The combination of Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa also appeared together for Turkey in ESL 2017, where the group added another major championship to Liéds' career.

Championship Lineup (#15): Liéds • Misaj • ES Pierre • Müllewa

HTML5 Era

The transition from Flash to the renewed HTML5 version of HaxBall marked a clear change in Liéds' career.

While he had been one of the leading goalkeepers of the Flash era, Liéds never fully adapted to the new version of the game and was unable to reproduce the same individual level and club success that had defined his prime.

He nevertheless remained involved in high-level competition. In HaxBall Champions League #20, Liéds returned as Angry Bulls' goalkeeper during the club's run to the semi-finals.

During the semi-final against Deathwish, Liéds suffered a serious ping problem that resulted in a goal being conceded. The incident became part of a wider fair-play controversy surrounding the match, which Angry Bulls ultimately lost.

Despite continuing to compete, Liéds did not add another major club championship during the HTML5 period.

His club decline after Flash did not completely end his success, however. The 2019 Nations Cup provided one final major championship and demonstrated that Liéds could still contribute to a title-winning lineup in the HTML5 era.

International Career

Liéds represented Turkey extensively in international competition and built one of the most successful national-team careers in HaxBall history.

Across different periods of the game, Liéds won four national-team championships. This total places him second only to Misaj in national-team titles.

The four championships also showed unusual longevity at international level. Liéds was part of title-winning Turkish teams across multiple years and remained involved long enough to win major international tournaments during both the Flash and HTML5 periods.

Nations Cup 2016: The First International Crown

Liéds won his first major national-team championship with Turkey in the 2016 Nations Cup.

The championship lineup consisted of Liéds, Linas, Liebe and Müllewa, with Liéds occupying his usual goalkeeper position.

2016 Nations Cup Championship Lineup: Liéds • Linas • Liebe • Müllewa

Nations Cup 2017: Back-to-Back International Success

Liéds added another championship with Turkey in the 2017 Nations Cup, extending his international success into a second consecutive Nations Cup campaign.

The Turkish championship squad included Liéds, Gilbert, Vanessa, ANDREA and Misaj.

2017 Nations Cup Championship Lineup: Liéds • Gilbert • Vanessa • ANDREA • Misaj

ESL 2017: A Third National-Team Championship

Liéds continued his successful international run in ESL 2017.

Turkey fielded a lineup of Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa, bringing together the same four players who also formed Angry Bulls' HaxBall Champions League #15 championship lineup.

In the final, Turkey defeated Italy 2–1, giving Liéds his third national-team championship.

ESL 2017 Championship Lineup: Liéds • Misaj • ES Pierre • Müllewa

Nations Cup 2019: HTML5 Success

Liéds' fourth national-team championship, and his most important success of the HTML5 era, came in the 2019 Nations Cup, where he played as Turkey's goalkeeper alongside Misaj, Necro and Marble.

Turkey faced Portugal in the final and found itself 2–0 behind. The team recovered dramatically, forcing the match into extra time before completing a remarkable 5–2 comeback victory.

2019 Nations Cup Final Lineup: Liéds • Misaj • Necro • Marble

The championship gave Liéds his fourth national-team title and became the major exception to the decline in club success that followed his transition from Flash to HTML5.

His international record became one of the strongest parts of his overall legacy. Four national-team championships across different periods of competitive HaxBall gave Liéds a level of international success surpassed only by Misaj.

Playing Style

Liéds played primarily as a goalkeeper and built his historical reputation through his performances in the position during the Flash era.

His record demonstrates his ability to function successfully behind very different championship lineups. Within a short period, he won European titles with three different teams, each containing a different defensive and attacking structure.

This success became an important part of his standing among Flash-era goalkeepers. Rather than being associated with only one dominant squad, Liéds repeatedly found himself in championship-level teams and contributed from the goalkeeper position.

The HTML5 transition, however, did not suit him to the same extent. The contrast between his dominance during Flash and his reduced impact after the game's transition became one of the clearest dividing lines in his career.

Legacy

Liéds' legacy is built primarily around his Flash-era peak, when he established himself among the greatest goalkeepers in HaxBall history.

Winning three HaxBall Champions League titles with three different teams gave his career a particularly strong European record. The championships with Skins, Stiff Wind and Angry Bulls demonstrated that his success was not dependent on a single squad or club.

Liéds remains the most successful goalkeeper in HaxBall Champions League history by championship count. His three titles are the highest total ever achieved by a goalkeeper, and the fact that all three came with different clubs makes the record particularly notable.

His success was not limited to club competition. With Turkey, Liéds won four major national-team championships: the Nations Cup in 2016, the Nations Cup in 2017, ESL 2017 and the Nations Cup in 2019.

Those four international championships give Liéds the second-highest national-team title total in HaxBall history, behind only Misaj. His record therefore combines historic success at both club and national-team level.

The move to HTML5 prevented Liéds from sustaining the same individual level and club dominance that had characterized his Flash-era prime. He nevertheless remained capable of contributing at the highest level, with the 2019 Nations Cup championship providing one final major title in the newer version of the game.

Across his HaxBall Champions League and national-team career, Liéds collected seven major championships. Few players combined European and international success to the same extent.

With the all-time HaxBall Champions League goalkeeper title record, four national-team championships and major trophies across both Flash and HTML5, Liéds' achievements place him not only among the greatest goalkeepers in HaxBall history, but also among the most successful players the game has produced regardless of position.
