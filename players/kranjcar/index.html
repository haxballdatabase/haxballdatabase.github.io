Kranjcar is a Turkish HaxBall player from the Flash era, widely regarded as one of the best players of his era and remembered as one of the most recognizable and important figures in the early history of HaxBall.

A talented attacking player capable of operating as both an AM and striker, Kranjcar proved himself across several major teams and tournaments. He competed alongside many of the strongest players of his generation, became a HaxBall Champions League winner and also achieved international success with Turkey.

His place in HaxBall history, however, extends far beyond his achievements on the pitch. During a period when the game was rapidly growing in popularity, Kranjcar became one of the most recognizable personalities surrounding HaxBall through the movies and team videos he produced.

His videos helped introduce competitive HaxBall, its players and its teams to a wider audience. At the height of the Flash era, even people with only limited knowledge of the competitive scene could recognize the Kranjcar name, giving him a level of visibility reached by relatively few players of the period.

Kranjcar therefore occupied an unusual position within the game's history. He was both a proven high-level player and a cultural figure whose work helped shape how competitive HaxBall was presented, watched and remembered.

At club level, he became a HaxBall Champions League champion with Zebras in Edition #5, played a particularly active role during his time with Pas De Nom, and maintained a close relationship with Silas Beavers.

He played an important role in Pas De Nom's run to the HaxBall Champions League #7 final before being unable to take part in the championship match itself. Five editions later, he returned to the final with Pas De Nom in Edition #12. Internationally, he reached the ESL CC 2014 final with Turkey and later became an ESL 2015 champion.

Biography

Kranjcar competed during the Flash era and became part of several important teams from the period.

An attacking player capable of playing as both an AM and striker, he possessed considerable individual ability and reached a level that allowed him to compete successfully alongside many of the strongest names of his generation. His career included appearances for Silas Beavers, Zebras and Pas De Nom, as well as international competition with Turkey.

Although he was not primarily remembered as one of the absolute dominant players of his generation, Kranjcar repeatedly demonstrated his quality in high-level competition and remained involved with some of the most important teams and tournaments of the Flash era.

What ultimately made his career unusual was the combination of that competitive ability with an influence that reached far beyond official matches.

Silas Beavers

Kranjcar maintained a particularly close connection with Silas Beavers, one of the defining teams of the early competitive period.

Within the Silas squad itself, Kranjcar was more often used as a substitute or rotational player rather than as a permanent member of the primary lineup.

This role did not define his wider level or importance as a player. With Pas De Nom, Kranjcar occupied a considerably more active role and became one of the names most closely associated with the team.

His involvement with Pas De Nom, including his important contribution during the club's HaxBall Champions League #7 campaign, demonstrated that the more limited role he held within Silas Beavers was not representative of his overall playing ability.

Kranjcar's relationship with Silas also extended far beyond match participation. He became closely connected to the public image and memory of the team through the videos he produced about Silas Beavers.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Kranjcar was involved in several important HaxBall Champions League campaigns during the Flash era.

He became a champion with Zebras in Edition #5, played an important role in Pas De Nom's journey to the Edition #7 final, and later returned to the championship match with Pas De Nom in Edition #12.

These campaigns connected Kranjcar with several of the most significant teams and players of early competitive HaxBall.

HaxBall Champions League #5: Champion with Zebras

Kranjcar became a HaxBall Champions League champion in Edition #5 as part of Zebras.

The championship became one of the major achievements of his playing career and placed him in a successful Zebras side featuring several important names of the period.

The victory also completed Dexter's third consecutive HaxBall Champions League championship.

HaxBall Champions League #7: The Final He Missed

Kranjcar became one of the players most closely associated with Pas De Nom, where he held a much more active role than during his time with Silas Beavers.

During HaxBall Champions League #7, Kranjcar played throughout the earlier stages of Pas De Nom's campaign and made an important contribution to the team's run toward the championship match.

However, Kranjcar was unable to make the final against Angels of Death. His absence forced Pas De Nom to change its expected lineup, with Adékyz stepping in as his replacement and Tino moving from striker into midfield.

Despite being unable to play, Kranjcar remained closely behind his teammates and strongly supported Pas De Nom around the championship match.

Adékyz proved to be an exceptional replacement. He scored twice as Pas De Nom established a 2–0 lead, before Dark pulled one back for Angels of Death.

Pas De Nom then restored its two-goal advantage. Leu found Tino in midfield, who immediately created the opportunity for Adékyz to complete his hat-trick and make the score 3–1.

Adékyz then turned provider on the counter-attack, assisting Tino for the final goal of a convincing 4–1 victory.

Although Kranjcar was not part of the lineup that played the final and is therefore not listed among his HaxBall Champions League titles on this page, his contribution during Pas De Nom's road to the final remained an important part of the club's championship campaign.

Final Lineup: Guti.Haz • Leu • Tino • Adékyz
Final: Pas De Nom 4–1 Angels of Death
Adékyz: 3 Goals • 1 Assist

HaxBall Champions League #12: Return to the Final

Five editions later, Kranjcar returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Pas De Nom.

The final lineup consisted of tonnnek, Leu, Kranjcar and Tino.

Their opponent was Skins, another Turkish team, who lined up with Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Skins took control of the match through a four-goal performance from Catrice and eventually won 4–1.

The result left Kranjcar and Pas De Nom as HaxBall Champions League #12 runners-up.

Final Lineup (#12): tonnnek • Leu • Kranjcar • Tino
Final: Pas De Nom 1–4 Skins
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

Kranjcar also represented Turkey at international level during the Flash era.

His national-team career included appearances in major international competition, reaching the ESL CC 2014 final before later becoming an ESL 2015 champion with Turkey.

ESL CC 2014 Final

Kranjcar represented Turkey in the ESL CC 2014 final against the Czech Republic.

Turkey lined up with TinerMania, R9, Overkill and Kranjcar, while the Czech Republic fielded KEANU, Roman, Forsbi and CvRk.

The Czech Republic won the final 3–0, leaving Kranjcar and Turkey as ESL CC 2014 runners-up.

Turkey: TinerMania • R9 • Overkill • Kranjcar
Czech Republic: KEANU • Roman • Forsbi • CvRk
Final: Turkey 0–3 Czech Republic

HaxBall Movies and Cultural Impact

Beyond his competitive career, Kranjcar became one of the most recognizable figures connected with HaxBall's early video culture.

During HaxBall's rapid rise in popularity, he produced HaxBall movies and team videos that presented the game far beyond the format of ordinary match recordings and highlight compilations.

These videos showcased players, teams and the atmosphere surrounding competitive HaxBall to audiences who did not necessarily follow organized leagues. His work became one of the major reasons Kranjcar's name was recognizable even outside the core competitive community.

Three movies in particular remain closely associated with this part of his legacy: Kranjcar HaxBall Movie, Pas De Nom HaxBall 2015 and Farewell Silas Beavers.

🎥 Kranjcar HaxBall Movie • Most-viewed HaxBall movie on YouTube

Pas De Nom HaxBall 2015

Kranjcar's connection with Pas De Nom was not limited to his playing career. He also produced Pas De Nom HaxBall 2015, a team movie dedicated to one of the most distinctive teams of the Flash era.

The movie presented Pas De Nom through a polished combination of gameplay, editing and team identity, capturing many of the characteristics that had made the club recognizable within competitive HaxBall.

Its production quality and presentation helped it stand apart from conventional highlight compilations. Alongside Kranjcar's later Silas work, it became one of the defining examples of his contribution to HaxBall's movie culture.

Farewell Silas Beavers

Kranjcar later produced his final movie dedicated to Silas Beavers, another team with which he maintained a particularly close relationship.

The Farewell Silas Beavers movie represented an unusually advanced team video for its period. Its editing, presentation and overall production went far beyond what was normally associated with HaxBall community videos at the time.

Rather than functioning simply as a collection of match highlights, the movie captured the identity, players and atmosphere surrounding Silas Beavers and turned the team's history into a more complete visual presentation.

Its production remained impressive long after its original release, and the movie continued to be revisited years later. Together with Pas De Nom HaxBall 2015, it remains one of the strongest examples of Flash-era HaxBall movie production.

Legacy

Kranjcar's place in HaxBall history cannot be measured only through championships or individual playing ability.

He was a talented attacking player who competed for important teams, became a HaxBall Champions League champion and represented Turkey in multiple major international finals, including the ESL CC 2014 final and the successful ESL 2015 campaign.

His playing career alone was enough to establish him as one of the best and most notable players of the Flash era. His particularly active role with Pas De Nom, including his contribution to the club's Edition #7 run before missing the final, further demonstrated his importance at high competitive level.

What separated Kranjcar from many players of comparable competitive level was the reach of his influence outside official matches.

Through his HaxBall movies, he helped present competitive HaxBall to a wider audience during one of the most important periods in the game's growth. His videos contributed to the visibility of individual players, teams and the competitive scene itself.

Pas De Nom HaxBall 2015 and Farewell Silas Beavers became particularly enduring examples of this influence, while his own HaxBall movie remains one of the most widely viewed videos associated with the game.

The combination of his playing ability, competitive achievements, enormous visibility and influence on HaxBall's video culture made Kranjcar one of the most recognizable and distinctive figures in the game's early history.
