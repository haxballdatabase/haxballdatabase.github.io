SeRGeN is a Turkish HaxBall player from the early Flash era whose primary positions were DM and GK.

A highly versatile player, SeRGeN was capable of playing in virtually any position on the pitch when required. Although DM and GK were his main roles, his ability was not limited to either position.

He became part of Silas Beavers during one of the club's most successful periods and won HaxBall Champions League #4.

Silas Beavers

SeRGeN was one of the players associated with Silas Beavers during the early Flash era.

His versatility allowed him to fill different roles depending on the needs of the team. While his primary positions were DM and GK, he was capable of playing across the pitch rather than being restricted to a single role.

He was part of the Silas Beavers lineup that won HaxBall Champions League #4, completing the club's back-to-back championship run after its earlier victory in Edition #3.

HaxBall Champions League #4

SeRGeN became a HaxBall Champions League winner with Silas Beavers in Edition #4.

The championship lineup consisted of SeRGeN, Dexter, Иukroyš and Clinical Striker.

The victory gave Silas Beavers its second consecutive HaxBall Champions League title and established the team as one of the defining sides of the early competition.

Playing Role and Versatility

SeRGeN's primary positions were DM and GK, but positional versatility was one of the clearest characteristics of his profile.

He was capable of being placed in any position on the pitch and adapting to the role required from him. This made him considerably more flexible than a player tied exclusively to one position.

DM and GK remained his main roles, but his overall game allowed him to contribute outside those positions whenever necessary.
