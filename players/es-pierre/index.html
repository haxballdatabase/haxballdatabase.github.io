ES Pierre is regarded as one of the finest AMs of his generation, establishing himself as one of the leading midfielders of his era through intelligent positioning, defensive awareness and consistent team play.

He is best known as the long-time captain of Angry Bulls and one of the central figures behind the club's success across multiple generations.

Playing primarily as an AM, ES Pierre built his reputation in a way that differed from many players in the position. His game was not based on flashy technique or large attacking numbers. Instead, his greatest strengths were positioning, defensive intelligence, timing and an understanding of where he needed to be on the pitch.

ES Pierre won the HaxBall Champions League three times with Angry Bulls, becoming champion in Editions #11, #15 and #19. He later returned to the final in Edition #21, giving him a career record of four HaxBall Champions League finals, three championships and one runner-up finish.

Beyond his individual playing style, ES Pierre became closely associated with the identity of Angry Bulls itself. He remained an important figure as different generations of players passed through the team, and even played a central role in reviving the club after it had temporarily shut down.

Biography

ES Pierre became one of the players most closely associated with Angry Bulls, serving as captain during some of the most successful periods in the club's history.

Unlike many players whose careers were tied to one particular lineup, ES Pierre remained part of Angry Bulls through several different generations. The players around him changed considerably, but his leadership and organized approach remained part of the club's identity.

During the club's earlier years, ES Pierre developed a particularly effective partnership with Müllewa. ES Pierre's structured and team-oriented style complemented Müllewa's instinctive attacking game, and the understanding between the two became an important part of Angry Bulls' first major successes.

Later lineups brought players such as Misaj, Liéds, Necro and nova into the team. ES Pierre remained part of the squad as Angry Bulls continued to evolve, eventually becoming a three-time HaxBall Champions League champion.

His importance to Angry Bulls also extended beyond individual matches. After the club temporarily shut down following HaxBall Champions League #20, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction and helped bring the team back for another campaign.

HaxBall Champions League Career

ES Pierre reached four HaxBall Champions League finals with Angry Bulls, winning three of them. His championships came across Editions #11, #15 and #19, with a final runner-up appearance in Edition #21.

The span between his first and final appearance also reflected his longevity within the club. ES Pierre was present during different versions of Angry Bulls rather than succeeding with only one fixed group of teammates.

HaxBall Champions League #11: His First Title

ES Pierre's first HaxBall Champions League championship came in Edition #11, when Angry Bulls faced Yes Allstars in the final.

The Angry Bulls lineup consisted of Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa, while Yes Allstars featured Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Yes Allstars opened the scoring through Chro, but Angry Bulls responded when Müllewa combined with Linas and finished the move to make it 1–1.

As the match progressed, ES Pierre's understanding with Müllewa became increasingly important. The pair combined in attack before Müllewa scored again to give Angry Bulls a 2–1 lead.

Only a short time later, the roles were reversed. Müllewa played the ball into ES Pierre, who finished the opportunity to extend the lead to 3–1.

Angry Bulls eventually won the final 5–2, securing ES Pierre's first HaxBall Champions League title.

The match also provided an early example of the chemistry ES Pierre had developed with Müllewa. Their combination directly contributed to two consecutive goals during the period in which Angry Bulls took control of the final.

Championship Lineup (#11): Abduction • Linas • ES Pierre • Müllewa

HaxBall Champions League #15: Back on Top

Four editions later, ES Pierre returned to the HaxBall Champions League final as captain of another Angry Bulls lineup.

The team now consisted of Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa. Misaj's arrival added another major player to the squad, while ES Pierre continued to provide leadership and Müllewa remained one of the team's main attacking threats.

Angry Bulls faced fellow Turkish side Skins in the final. The attacking partnership between Misaj and Müllewa played a major role, with Misaj providing two assists and Müllewa scoring twice.

Angry Bulls completed a 3–1 victory, making ES Pierre a two-time HaxBall Champions League champion.

The same Angry Bulls lineup also achieved another important success by winning ESL 2017.

Championship Lineup (#15): Liéds • Misaj • ES Pierre • Müllewa

HaxBall Champions League #19: Three-Time Champion

ES Pierre later returned to the top of the competition in HaxBall Champions League #19, this time with another substantially changed Angry Bulls lineup.

The championship lineup consisted of nova, Misaj, ES Pierre and Necro, meaning ES Pierre was once again competing alongside a very different group of players from those involved in his previous two titles.

Angry Bulls defeated Trailer Park Penguins in the semi-final before meeting Stiff Wind in the final.

Stiff Wind had already won three HaxBall Champions League championships. Angry Bulls were attempting to win their third title and draw level with them.

Angry Bulls fell 1–0 behind, but Misaj created the equalizer for Necro using his trademark X-fake before scoring the winning goal himself in extra time.

The comeback gave ES Pierre his third HaxBall Champions League title, making him a three-time champion in the competition.

Reaching three titles marked one of the biggest milestones of ES Pierre's career. Each championship had also come with a noticeably different Angry Bulls lineup, underlining his longevity and his ability to remain part of championship-winning teams across multiple generations.

Championship Lineup (#19): nova • Misaj • ES Pierre • Necro

HaxBall Champions League #21: Brothers to Rivals

ES Pierre's fourth HaxBall Champions League final followed a conflict that had begun one edition earlier and changed the relationship between several of Angry Bulls' most important figures.

During HaxBall Champions League #20, Angry Bulls were eliminated by Deathwish in a highly controversial semi-final. Deathwish included former Angry Bulls players Müllewa, Abduction and Jéal, and tensions escalated after Angry Bulls expected fair play following a severe connection problem for Liéds but did not receive it.

The fallout eventually contributed to Angry Bulls shutting down. For Misaj, the dispute became particularly personal, with Müllewa at the centre of the conflict.

Approximately one to two months later, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction and revived Angry Bulls. It was not simply the return of the club that created the new rivalry. The decisive point for Misaj was that ES Pierre had chosen to rebuild Angry Bulls alongside Müllewa, the player at the centre of the dispute that had contributed to the previous team's collapse.

Misaj had previously been one of Angry Bulls' most important players and had shared two HaxBall Champions League championships with ES Pierre. The two had once stood together at the heart of the club's success, but the events surrounding HaxBall Champions League #20 and the subsequent reunion between ES Pierre and Müllewa placed them on opposite sides.

Misaj took control of Slappers and built a new squad with Angry Bulls firmly in his sights. A player who had once been central to Angry Bulls was now becoming its fiercest rival.

The two sides eventually met in the HaxBall Champions League #21 final. Angry Bulls were chasing a fourth championship, while ES Pierre had the opportunity to become a four-time HaxBall Champions League winner. For Misaj, the final carried a very different motivation. After ES Pierre had reunited with Müllewa and revived Angry Bulls, Misaj viewed his former teammates as his greatest rivals and wanted revenge.

The final quickly turned into a dominant performance from Slappers. Misaj's side raced into a 3–0 lead within the opening three minutes and never allowed Angry Bulls to recover.

Marble delivered an extraordinary individual performance, scoring all seven of Slappers' goals, while Misaj played a central role in Slappers' attacking control and in creating the situations from which Marble repeatedly found the finish.

As the scale of the defeat became clear, the match commentator summed up Angry Bulls' collapse with a line that captured the moment: “Masters of Champions League become, Masters of Disasters.”

Slappers eventually completed a dominant 7–2 victory, denying Angry Bulls and ES Pierre a fourth HaxBall Champions League championship.

With the victory, Misaj had completed the mission he had set for himself: defeating the rebuilt Angry Bulls on the biggest stage. Once one of the club's most important players and one of ES Pierre's closest teammates, Misaj had now become their fiercest rival and decisively beaten them in the final.

For ES Pierre, the defeat brought the first runner-up finish of his four HaxBall Champions League finals and ended Angry Bulls' previously perfect record in championship matches.

Playing Style

ES Pierre played primarily as an AM, but his game was not built around flashy technical ability or attacking statistics.

Unlike many attacking midfielders who built their reputation through goals and assists, ES Pierre's influence was heavily connected to defensive intelligence. His ability to read the play, anticipate danger and provide cover for teammates made him difficult for opposing teams to play through.

Much of his value came from actions that were less visible than goals or individual dribbles. ES Pierre regularly positioned himself to close passing lanes, interrupt developing attacks and provide support in areas where his team was vulnerable.

Rather than constantly chasing the ball, ES Pierre relied on understanding where the next danger was likely to appear. His positioning often allowed him to move into the correct area before an opponent could fully develop an attack.

This made his understanding of space, timing and defensive responsibility the foundation of his playing style. Despite occupying an advanced midfield position, ES Pierre often contributed heavily to the defensive structure behind Angry Bulls' attacking players.

His style also suited players such as Müllewa particularly well. While Müllewa focused heavily on attacking movement and finishing, ES Pierre provided structure and balance from midfield, allowing the two players to complement each other rather than competing for the same role.

Captaincy and Leadership

ES Pierre's importance to Angry Bulls went beyond his role as an AM. As captain, he became closely associated with the organization and continuity of the club.

He helped organize the team, communicate with teammates and maintain a consistent competitive identity even as the squad changed around him. Angry Bulls' three championship lineups included very different groups of players, yet ES Pierre remained part of each one.

His leadership became especially visible after Angry Bulls shut down following HaxBall Champions League #20. Rather than allowing the club to disappear permanently, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction and helped rebuild the team.

The revived side subsequently reached the HaxBall Champions League #21 final, reinforcing ES Pierre's status as one of the figures most closely connected with the club's long-term identity.

Legacy

ES Pierre is remembered as one of the central figures in Angry Bulls history, combining long-term leadership with success across several different versions of the team.

His three HaxBall Champions League championships came with three noticeably different lineups, illustrating the length of ES Pierre's time at the highest level and his ability to remain relevant as the club evolved.

ES Pierre's playing style also gave him a different profile from many prominent attacking midfielders. His reputation was built less on individual attacking numbers and more on positioning, defensive awareness, reading of space and the ability to provide structure for the players around him.

His partnership with Müllewa became an important part of Angry Bulls' early success, while later championship teams saw ES Pierre play alongside figures such as Misaj and Necro.

Perhaps the clearest indication of his connection to the club came after Angry Bulls temporarily disappeared. ES Pierre helped revive the team and led another generation back to a HaxBall Champions League final.

With three HaxBall Champions League titles, four final appearances, an ESL 2017 championship with Turkey and a long period as Angry Bulls captain, ES Pierre established a legacy based not only on trophies, but also on leadership, positioning and defensive responsibility from midfield.
