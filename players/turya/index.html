Turya is a Turkish HaxBall player. He is best documented as the captain of Deathwish, the Turkish team that reached the final of HaxBall Champions League #20.

Deathwish's recorded competitive history is centered on that single Champions League campaign. As captain, Turya led a squad that also included Müllewa, Jéal, Abduction and Splat.

The campaign ended with Deathwish finishing as HaxBall Champions League #20 runners-up. The surviving archive clearly identifies Turya as the team's captain, while the recorded final lineup itself consisted of Abduction, Müllewa, Jéal and Splat.

Deathwish

Turya served as captain of Deathwish, a short-lived Turkish team whose recorded competitive presence is concentrated in HaxBall Champions League #20.

The squad included Müllewa, Jéal, Abduction and Splat, and the team advanced through the tournament to reach the championship match.

HaxBall Champions League #20

HaxBall Champions League #20 is the central recorded tournament attached to Turya's captaincy of Deathwish. The team eliminated Angry Bulls in the semi-final and advanced to the final against Smokin' Aces.

The semi-final became controversial after Angry Bulls goalkeeper Liéds experienced a serious ping problem during play. Deathwish continued the match and eventually advanced, although the surviving team record does not establish a specific individual decision or action by Turya in that incident.

Deathwish later lost the final 4–3 to Smokin' Aces. The recorded final lineup was Abduction, Müllewa, Jéal and Splat.
