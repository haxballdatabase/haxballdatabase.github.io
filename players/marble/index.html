Marble is widely regarded as one of the best attacking players in HaxBall history, combining elite goalscoring, playmaking and versatility at the highest level. He also holds a strong claim as one of the most statistically dominant figures in Feed Me Haxball League history and a complete offensive force. Highly versatile, Marble was capable of dominating both as an Attacking Midfielder (AM) and as a frontline Striker—the position in which he won both of his HaxBall Champions League titles.

Domestically, Marble established total supremacy, holding the undisputed all-time Division 1 records for both Most Goals (285) and Most Assists (185), alongside an astounding 8 Division 1 titles as the focal point of the iconic Seeders dynasty.

His story reads like an epic tale of heartbreak and ultimate redemption. After orchestrating footballing masterclasses with Seeders—only to suffer back-to-back Golden Goal drama in the Haxball Champions League #17 (competing under BLACKOUT) and Haxball Champions League #18 (Seeders) Grand Finals—Marble transformed his career on the international and European stage. Reaching historic heights in Nations Cup 2017, National Team Cup 2019, Haxball Champions League #21 (Slappers), and Haxball Champions League #23 (Flaviu 1997), Marble shattered his finals curse to conquer the summit of competitive HaxBall.

Biography & Early Career

Marble established himself as a premier attacking force during the golden age of competitive HaxBall. Distinguishing himself through a combination of clinical finishing, intelligent positioning, and playmaking vision, he quickly evolved into one of the most versatile and lethal attacking talents in the game.

Dominating Feed Me: The Seeders Dynasty

In competitive HaxBall lore, Seeders is universally recognized as one of the greatest and most dominant teams ever assembled. While their pedigree resonated internationally, their legend was forged through complete supremacy in the Feed Me Haxball League.

From QuickSwans to the Seeders Embargo

The era began when the core lineup won the Feed Me title playing under the banner of QuickSwans. Following that championship season, the entire roster transitioned together to establish the official Seeders tag. With Isco taking over the captaincy, the iconic lineup featured:

🛡️ Legendary Seeders Roster:
Isco (C) • Yannex • Marble • Pringles • aguero10 • shaxlele

Under Isco's vocal leadership and sharp tactical alignment, Seeders captured back-to-back championships in Seasons 24 and 25. Combined with their initial trophy as QuickSwans, Seeders enforced a brutal 3-season embargo over the Feed Me League, establishing the most impressive period of domestic dominance in FM history.

An Unmatched Legacy

As the primary attacking force throughout Seeders' historic three-season embargo, Marble built an individual record in the Feed Me Haxball League that stands at the absolute summit of domestic competition:

  • Division 1 All-Time Most Goals: MARBLE7 — 285 Goals
  • Division 1 All-Time Most Assists: MARBLE7 — 185 Assists
  • Individual Honours: 2x Top Assister, 1x Top Scorer, 8x Division 1 Champion

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

Marble's international prominence reached new heights during Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, representing the Turkish National Team alongside Misaj, Liebe, and Andrea.

Given the unique mechanics of the tournament's Big Easy map, Turkey deployed a tactical setup that allowed Marble complete offensive freedom upfront as the primary goalscorer. Marble responded with a tournament-defining offensive output:

  • Quarter-Finals (3–1 vs UK): Marble helped outplay a stacked UK squad featuring GQKU, Splat, Flash, and Maddude.
  • Semi-Finals (2–1 vs Russia): With the match tied, Marble struck the decisive second-half winner to propel Turkey into the Grand Final.
  • Grand Final (vs Netherlands): Trailing 1–0 against xSha and Hulk, Turkey regrouped as a unit to stage a complete team comeback, with Marble playing a key supporting role in the attacking line to seal the title.

Crowned Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy Champion, Marble secured a major international trophy and further solidified his reputation as a big-game match-winner on the international stage.

European Stage & Golden Goal Nightmares

While Seeders ruled domestic competition with an iron fist, their campaigns in the HaxBall Champions League (HCL) produced two of the most dramatic and heartbreaking narrative arcs in European history.

HaxBall Champions League #17: The Tragedy

Just as they had done with QuickSwans, Seeders entered HCL #17 using another team’s slot, competing under the name BLACKOUT with their full main roster. They dominated the tournament, showcasing superior possession play and advancing seamlessly to the Grand Final.

In the final, BLACKOUT faced Klub Emeryta. While their opponents featured future world-class talents SAKY and xSha up front—alongside kojo in goal and Ter at DM—both SAKY and xSha were still at the beginning of their careers, having competed in Division 2 just seasons prior.

⚔️ HCL #17 Final Breakdown (BLACKOUT vs Klub Emeryta):

  • Domination: BLACKOUT started with total control, stifling Emeryta through suffocating possession football.
  • 1–0: Captain Isco delivered a precise pass for Yannex to open the scoring.
  • 1–1 & 1–2: Emeryta struck back on a counter-attack as SAKY assisted xSha for the equalizer. SAKY then utilized his signature corner-spam execution to put Emeryta ahead 2–1.
  • 2–2: With under 60 seconds left on the clock, aguero10 found Isco, who scored a heroic equalizer to send the final into extra time!
  • Golden Goal: Known for his 1v1 counter-attacking lethality, xSha caught BLACKOUT on a quick transition to score the winner, leaving Marble and Seeders utterly stunned.

HaxBall Champions League #18: Final Nightmare

Returning in HCL #18 officially under the Seeders banner, Marble and his teammates set out to claim European glory. Along their playoff run, Marble produced a defining moment by scoring a brilliant goal to eliminate HCL titans Stiff Wind 3–1.

🥀 HCL #18 Final: "The 15-Second Golden Goal Nightmare" (Seeders vs The Elders)

In the Grand Final, Seeders faced The Elders (LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY, Vak).

  • Regular Time (0–0): A fast-paced, highly technical battle ended scoreless, with Seeders generating the clearer chances.
  • First OT (1–0): In extra time, Marble delivered a perfect assist for Yannex to break the deadlock!
  • Second OT (1–1): Vak equalized for The Elders with a corner-spam finish.
  • Reclaiming the Lead (2–1): Refusing to yield, Marble created chaos in the box with a fierce corner-spam, forcing the ball off Isco and Yannex into the net to make it 2–1.
  • Late Drama (2–2): With under two minutes left, Vak assisted SAKY for a late equalizer, pushing the match into Golden Goal.
  • 15-Second Heartbreak (2–3): Just 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY scored the winner for The Elders.

Losing two consecutive Champions League Grand Finals in Golden Goal extra time—despite outplaying their opponents for long stretches—remains one of the most tragic narratives in HaxBall history. Nevertheless, Seeders' mesmerizing passing style and Marble’s individual brilliance earned them universal respect as the greatest team never to win HCL during that era.

Breaking the Curse & European Vengeance

Building on his international success, Marble set out to finally conquer the HaxBall Champions League.

Prime Marble (HaxBall Champions League #21)

In HaxBall Champions League #21, Marble joined Slappers alongside Misaj, Norbigo, and Vichoexe on a unified mission for European redemption.

What followed in the Grand Final against defending champions Angry Bulls was the single most dominant individual performance in European HaxBall history. In an astonishing 7–2 blowout victory—the most crushing win ever recorded in an HCL Grand Final—Marble scored ALL 7 GOALS for Slappers.

Unstoppable from start to finish, Marble’s positioning, clinical precision, and relentless offensive movement delivered an all-time record solo performance, shattering his finals curse in unforgettable style to claim his first Champions League trophy.

National Team Cup 2019: The Historic Comeback

On July 30, 2019, Marble represented the Turkish National Team in the National Team Cup 2019, lining up alongside Misaj, Necro, and Liéds in goal. Throughout the tournament, Marble operated as Turkey's primary attacking engine, putting on a masterclass in playmaking and goalscoring efficiency.

In the Quarter-Finals against Romania, Marble put on a complete offensive clinic. He opened the scoring early following a sharp pass from Necro, and went on to orchestrate Turkey's attack by providing 3 assists. Directly involved in all four goals, Marble spearheaded a dominant 4–0 victory to march into the later stages.

In the Grand Final, Turkey faced a powerhouse Portugal roster featuring Pringles and SAKY. Portugal struck twice to build a 2–0 lead, putting Turkey on the brink of defeat. Refusing to lose another major final, Turkey launched an aggressive comeback: Misaj pulled one back to make it 2–1, before Necro scrambled home a last-minute equalizer to force extra time at 2–2.

🔥 Marble's Extra-Time Masterclass (2–0 to 5–2 Comeback):

  • The Go-Ahead Goal (3–2): In extra time, Marble displayed ice-cold instincts upfront, scoring the crucial go-ahead goal to give Turkey their first lead of the final.
  • Putting it Out of Reach (4–2): With Turkey in full control, Necro set up Marble with a sharp pass, and Marble finished ruthlessly to score his second goal of the final, extending Turkey's lead to 4–2 and shattering Portugal's spirit.
  • Championship Sealed (5–2): Turkey closed out a breathtaking 5–2 victory, completing one of the most historic grand final comebacks in competitive HaxBall history.

With a total goal contribution across both the quarter-finals and grand final that defined Turkey's campaign, Marble proved once again that he was an unstoppable big-stage match-winner, adding another major international title to his resume.

HaxBall Champions League #23

Reuniting with Misaj alongside PiperSalvia and SAKY under European power Flaviu 1997 in HCL #23, Marble maintained his peak level of offensive supremacy.

Navigating a grueling tournament bracket—including a tense semifinal clash against aMp's FEAR—Flaviu 1997 captured the HCL #23 title. Marble secured his second Champions League crown, solidifying his place at the absolute summit of international HaxBall.

The Flawless Misaj Partnership

Prior to teaming up with Misaj, Marble had endured two heartbreaking defeats in his first two HCL Grand Finals (HCL #17 as Blackout, HCL #18 as Seeders). However, once paired with Misaj, Marble achieved a flawless 100% win rate in major finals—winning Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, the standard Nations Cup 2017, National Team Cup 2019, Nations Cup 2020, HCL #21, and HCL #23. Misaj’s complete game control and Marble’s unstoppable finishing created one of the most iconic duos in competitive history.

Playing Style

Marble stands out as a highly complete and adaptable attacking player. While he won both of his Champions League titles playing as a clinical frontline finisher, he was equally capable of dictating games from deeper roles as an Attacking Midfielder (AM).

  • Attacking Versatility: Seamlessly transitionable between AM playmaking and frontline goalscoring roles.
  • Elite Playmaking: Holds the Division 1 all-time assist record (185), demonstrating elite vision in tight spaces.
  • Historic Peak Execution: Capable of completely dominating major finals, as proven by his legendary 7-goal performance in the HCL #21 Grand Final and his extra-time double in the 2019 National Team Cup.

Legacy

Marble stands as the ultimate benchmark for modern HaxBall attacking players. By combining unapproachable statistical dominance in the Feed Me Haxball League, surviving the heartbreak of two Golden Goal Champions League finals, delivering an iconic 7-goal solo masterclass in HCL #21, and winning four major international trophies, Marble cemented his legacy as one of the most decorated icons in HaxBall history.
