Biography

Makenzie competed as a striker and became part of the Wicked Guys lineup that achieved the club's first HaxBall Champions League championship.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #24: Champion

Makenzie won the HaxBall Champions League #24 as part of Wicked Guys.

The championship lineup consisted of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Wicked Guys faced Good Guys in the final, whose lineup featured gggggk, B ull, William and Torment.

An early goal from Taziek gave Wicked Guys the lead, and the team held on to secure a 1–0 victory and the championship.

Championship Lineup (#24): Wiagoal • Vichoexe • Taziek • Makenzie
Final: Wicked Guys 1–0 Good Guys
