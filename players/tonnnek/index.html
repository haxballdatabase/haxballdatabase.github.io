tonnnek is a Turkish HaxBall goalkeeper regarded as one of the best goalkeepers of the Flash era. Combining high technical quality with the ability to play aggressively and apply pressure outside his goal, he developed into one of the most complete goalkeepers of his generation.

His competitive record was equally impressive. tonnnek reached three HaxBall Champions League finals, winning consecutive championships with Antartic Lions in #8 and Stiff Wind in #9 before later finishing runner-up with Pas De Nom in #12.

The back-to-back championships became one of the most notable achievements of his career. tonnnek won Edition #8 with Antartic Lions before moving to Stiff Wind and immediately winning again in #9, where his new team defeated his previous championship club in the final.

Unlike many leading players from his generation, tonnnek did not continue into the HTML5 era. He stepped away from competitive HaxBall during the Flash period, leaving his reputation entirely built on his performances in the original version of the game.

Biography

tonnnek established himself during the Flash era as one of the leading goalkeepers of his generation.

His career was notable for both individual quality and major championship success. Rather than being associated with only one successful lineup, tonnnek reached the highest level with Antartic Lions, Stiff Wind and Pas De Nom.

His strongest run came across HaxBall Champions League Editions #8 and #9. tonnnek won the competition in consecutive editions while representing two different teams, first becoming champion with Antartic Lions and then immediately repeating the achievement with Stiff Wind.

Several editions later, he reached another final with Pas De Nom in #12. Although that campaign ended in defeat against Skins, it gave tonnnek a third HaxBall Champions League final and completed a record of two championships from three final appearances.

His entire major competitive career remained within the Flash era. tonnnek eventually stepped away from the game before the transition to HTML5.

HaxBall Champions League Career

tonnnek reached the HaxBall Champions League final three times, recording two championships and one runner-up finish.

His first championship came with Antartic Lions in Edition #8. One edition later, tonnnek changed teams and became the goalkeeper of Stiff Wind, immediately winning the competition again.

The circumstances of the second championship made the achievement particularly memorable. Stiff Wind's opponent in the #9 final was Antartic Lions, meaning tonnnek won his second consecutive title by defeating the club with which he had won the previous edition.

His final recorded appearance on the championship stage came with Pas De Nom in #12, giving him major HaxBall Champions League runs with three different teams during the Flash era.

HaxBall Champions League #8: Champion with Antartic Lions

tonnnek won his first HaxBall Champions League championship in Edition #8 as the goalkeeper of Antartic Lions.

The championship lineup began with tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer, with Yannex later entering the match from the bench.

The victory gave tonnnek his first major European championship and established him as a HaxBall Champions League-winning goalkeeper during the heart of the Flash era.

Championship Lineup (#8): tonnnek • Rq* • CRonaldo • Meyer (Yannex)

HaxBall Champions League #9: Back-to-Back with a New Team

Immediately after his championship with Antartic Lions, tonnnek joined Stiff Wind.

The club had emerged from the rebranding of AoD, with ROCKY BALBOA and R9 forming the core of the new project. tonnnek was brought into the team as its goalkeeper alongside attacker Catrice.

Stiff Wind reached the HaxBall Champions League #9 final with a lineup of tonnnek, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Catrice.

Their opponents were Antartic Lions, tonnnek's championship team from the previous edition.

Stiff Wind opened the scoring through Catrice before Antartic Lions responded when Yaadon created the equalizer for Meyer. Stiff Wind eventually restored its lead and moved ahead 2–1.

During the closing stages, R9 remained deep and concentrated almost entirely on protecting the advantage. With tonnnek behind the defense, Stiff Wind successfully held the score and secured the championship.

The result made tonnnek a back-to-back HaxBall Champions League champion with two different teams, one of the defining achievements of his career.

Championship Lineup (#9): tonnnek • R9 • ROCKY BALBOA • Catrice

HaxBall Champions League #12: A Third Final

tonnnek later returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Pas De Nom in Edition #12.

Pas De Nom fielded tonnnek, Leu, Kranjcar and Tino, bringing together several important names from the early competitive scene.

Their opponents were Skins, whose lineup consisted of Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Skins controlled the final and won 4–1, with Catrice scoring all four of the team's goals.

The defeat prevented tonnnek from winning a third HaxBall Champions League championship, but the appearance gave him a third final with a third different team.

Final Lineup (#12): tonnnek • Leu • Kranjcar • Tino

Playing Style

tonnnek was a goalkeeper whose game combined strong technical quality and aggressive pressure.

Unlike goalkeepers whose effectiveness depended primarily on remaining close to the goal line, tonnnek was capable of playing more proactively. He could move forward to pressure attacking players and reduce their space, giving him another method of defending beyond simply reacting to shots.

His technical level was also an important part of his game. tonnnek was comfortable with the ball for a goalkeeper and possessed the control required to contribute cleanly when possession reached him.

The combination gave him a relatively complete Flash-era goalkeeping profile. He could provide security through traditional saves when required, but was also capable of taking a more aggressive position and becoming directly involved in shutting down attacks before a clean opportunity could develop.

This versatility was one of the qualities that separated tonnnek from more limited goalkeepers of the period and contributed to his reputation as one of the best goalkeepers of the Flash era.

Flash Era and Retirement

tonnnek's competitive career belongs entirely to the Flash era of HaxBall.

His major achievements, including two HaxBall Champions League championships and three final appearances, were completed before the game underwent its later transition to HTML5.

tonnnek eventually stepped away from competitive HaxBall while Flash was still the active version of the game. As a result, unlike several other major goalkeepers of his generation, his career never developed an HTML5 chapter.

His historical reputation is therefore based entirely on what he achieved in the original era, where his combination of technical quality, proactive goalkeeping and championship success made him one of the standout players in his position.

Legacy

tonnnek is remembered as one of the leading goalkeepers of the Flash era, with a career combining a high individual level and a strong championship record.

His two HaxBall Champions League championships made him one of the most successful goalkeepers of his period. Winning those titles in consecutive editions with two different teams added another notable layer to his record.

The #9 championship was particularly symbolic. Only one edition after becoming champion with Antartic Lions, tonnnek stood in goal for Stiff Wind and defeated his previous club in the final to complete the back-to-back run.

His later final with Pas De Nom demonstrated that his success was not limited to a brief two-season period. Across his career, tonnnek reached the HaxBall Champions League final with three different teams, winning two of those appearances.

His playing style also distinguished him from more traditional goalkeepers. The ability to combine aggressive pressure, technical quality and reliable goalkeeping gave tonnnek a broad skill set for the position.

tonnnek left the game before HTML5, meaning his legacy remains inseparable from the Flash era. Within that period, his performances and championship record established him as one of the best and most successful goalkeepers of his generation.
