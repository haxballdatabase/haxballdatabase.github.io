Rene is a Macedonian HaxBall striker from the earliest period of competitive HaxBall, best known as one of the core members of Nerdlucks, the team that won the first two editions of the HaxBall Champions League.

His career is closely connected to the beginning of organized European competition. Rene played in both of Nerdlucks' championship-winning lineups and became one of the first players in HaxBall Champions League history to win the competition twice.

As an attacking player, Rene formed part of a highly successful offensive structure alongside aguero10. Their combination became especially visible in HaxBall Champions League #2, where the pair were directly involved in several of the goals that decided the final.

Rene's most clearly documented individual performance came in that final, where he scored twice and provided an assist in Nerdlucks' 4–1 victory.

At club level, Rene won HaxBall Champions League #1 and #2. The two championships made Nerdlucks the first back-to-back champion in the history of the competition and established the club as HaxBall's first European dynasty.

Rene remained one of the central attacking players across both title-winning editions and played an especially decisive role in the second championship final.

Biography

Rene emerged during the earliest years of organized competitive HaxBall and became one of the attacking players associated with the first successful generation of Nerdlucks.

This period came before many of the structures later associated with European competitive HaxBall had fully developed. The opening editions of the HaxBall Champions League therefore represented an important stage in establishing the first major international club competition.

Rene was present from the beginning. He played in Nerdlucks' inaugural championship lineup and remained an important part of the team when the club returned to defend the title in the following edition.

Across those two campaigns, Rene became part of the first generation of players to win multiple HaxBall Champions League championships.

Nerdlucks

Rene became most closely associated with Nerdlucks, the team that dominated the beginning of the HaxBall Champions League.

The original championship lineup consisted of Fredklop, Nesquik, aguero10 and Rene. Together, the group won HaxBall Champions League #1 and became the first continental champions in the history of the competition.

For Edition #2, Nerdlucks changed its structure by adding GQKU as goalkeeper and moving Fredklop into the DM position. Rene and aguero10 remained in the attacking structure of the team.

The altered lineup successfully defended the championship, giving Nerdlucks consecutive titles and establishing the first dynasty in HaxBall Champions League history.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Rene won the HaxBall Champions League twice, becoming champion in both of the competition's first two editions.

The achievement placed him among the first players in competition history to become a two-time HCL champion and among the first group to win consecutive championships.

While both championships form an important part of his career, Edition #2 provides the clearest surviving record of Rene's direct influence in a major final.

HaxBall Champions League #1: The First Champions

Rene's first HaxBall Champions League championship came in the inaugural edition of the competition.

Nerdlucks competed with a primary lineup of Fredklop, Nesquik, aguero10 and Rene.

The team won the tournament and became the first HaxBall Champions League champion.

For Rene, the championship represented the beginning of a two-edition title run that would immediately place him among the earliest multiple winners of the competition.

HaxBall Champions League #2: A Decisive Final

Nerdlucks returned in HaxBall Champions League #2 attempting to defend its championship.

The lineup changed with the arrival of GQKU, producing a four-player unit of GQKU, Fredklop, aguero10 and Rene.

Final Day: Nerdlucks vs ZaqHaxY

Rene made an immediate impact on the final.

Only 11 seconds into the match, aguero10 created the opening and Rene converted the chance to give Nerdlucks an early lead. The goal became the fastest recorded goal in a HaxBall Champions League final.

Later in the match, Rene again finished an assist from aguero10 to help extend Nerdlucks' advantage.

In the final minute, the partnership reversed roles. Rene supplied the pass and aguero10 scored to complete Nerdlucks' 4–1 victory.

Rene therefore finished the final with two goals and one assist, directly contributing to three of Nerdlucks' four goals as the club secured its second consecutive HaxBall Champions League championship.

Partnership with aguero10

Rene and aguero10 were both members of the Nerdlucks lineups that won HaxBall Champions League #1 and #2.

Their attacking understanding was particularly visible in the second Champions League final. aguero10 assisted both of Rene's goals, including the opening goal after only 11 seconds.

Late in the same match, Rene returned the contribution by assisting aguero10 for Nerdlucks' fourth goal.

The sequence became one of the clearest surviving examples of the attacking combination that helped define Nerdlucks' second championship.

Playing Style and Influence

Rene played as a striker and was part of the attacking structure of Nerdlucks during the club's two championship campaigns.

The surviving record of HaxBall Champions League #2 shows a player capable of both finishing attacks and creating opportunities for teammates. His two goals and one assist in the final demonstrated that his contribution was not limited exclusively to goalscoring.

Rene was also associated within parts of the early HaxBall community with a move that became known as the "Rene trick", another small example of the recognition his name achieved during the game's earlier competitive years.

Because detailed statistical records from the earliest period of competitive HaxBall are limited, broader conclusions about his complete playing style remain difficult to establish from the surviving archive.

Legacy

Rene's place in HaxBall history is closely connected to the beginning of the HaxBall Champions League and the rise of Nerdlucks.

By winning HCL #1 and #2 consecutively, he became one of the first two-time champions in the history of the competition and part of its first back-to-back championship generation.

His most notable documented individual performance came in HaxBall Champions League #2, where he produced two goals and one assist in the final and scored the opening goal after only 11 seconds.

Together with aguero10, Fredklop, Nesquik and GQKU, Rene formed part of the generation that established Nerdlucks as the first dynasty of European competitive HaxBall.

His combination of consecutive championship success and a decisive performance in one of the competition's earliest major finals gives Rene a distinct place within the history of HaxBall's earliest competitive period.
