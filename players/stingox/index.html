Stingox is a HaxBall player from Sweden best known for winning the HaxBall Champions League #6 with Hellhounds.

He was part of the championship lineup alongside Adriano, aguero10 and Ced, helping Hellhounds complete a dominant European campaign that ended with a 4–0 victory over Die Galaktischen in the final.

Biography

Stingox's currently documented competitive history is most closely connected with Hellhounds and the club's successful HaxBall Champions League #6 campaign.

Playing alongside Adriano, aguero10 and Ced, Stingox became part of a lineup that reached the final and delivered one of the more convincing championship victories of the early competition.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Stingox became a HaxBall Champions League champion in Edition #6 with Hellhounds.

HaxBall Champions League #6: Champion with Hellhounds

Hellhounds reached the HaxBall Champions League #6 final with a lineup of Adriano, aguero10, Stingox and Ced.

Their opponents were Die Galaktischen, represented by Caccapupu, iNoob, Nino and Razmo.

Hellhounds controlled the match and eventually completed a dominant 4–0 victory.

The opening goal came after sustained pressure resulted in an own goal from Nino.

Later, aguero10 found Ced with a pass and Ced converted the opportunity to make it 2–0.

Stingox then became directly involved in the third goal. He helped construct the attacking move and set up Ced, who finished into the corner for his second goal of the final.

Hellhounds eventually added a fourth goal and completed the championship match without conceding, giving Stingox his HaxBall Champions League title.

Legacy

Stingox's documented place in competitive HaxBall history is primarily connected with Hellhounds' HaxBall Champions League #6 championship.

He was part of the four-player lineup that defeated Die Galaktischen 4–0 and contributed directly to the final by creating the opportunity for Ced's second goal.

The victory made Stingox one of the Swedish players to win the HaxBall Champions League during the Flash era.
