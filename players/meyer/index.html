Meyer is a HaxBall player from Israel who primarily played as a striker. Competing across both the Flash and HTML5 eras, he became known as one of the leading and talented strikers of his generation.

Meyer is most closely associated with Antartic Lions, with whom he reached consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals. He became a champion in Edition #8 before returning to the final in #9, where he scored Antartic Lions' equalizing goal against Stiff Wind.

His career also included major international success with the Israel HaxBall community. Meyer was part of the Israeli team that won Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Biography

Meyer established himself during the Flash era as one of the notable attacking players of the Israel HaxBall community.

Playing primarily as a striker, he became closely associated with Antartic Lions and was part of the club during one of the strongest periods in its history.

Meyer reached consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals with Antartic Lions in Editions #8 and #9, winning the first before narrowly losing the second.

His career later extended into the HTML5 era, where he remained involved in major competition and added an international championship with Israel.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Meyer's strongest HaxBall Champions League period came with Antartic Lions, reaching the final in consecutive editions and recording one championship and one runner-up finish.

HaxBall Champions League #8: European Champion

Meyer became a HaxBall Champions League champion with Antartic Lions in Edition #8.

The championship lineup began with tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer.

Yannex later entered the match from the bench in place of Meyer, becoming part of the championship-winning squad.

The victory gave Meyer his first HaxBall Champions League championship and established Antartic Lions among the European champions of the Flash era.

Championship Lineup (#8): tonnnek • Rq* • CRonaldo • Meyer

Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #9: Back in the Final

One edition after becoming European champion, Meyer returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Antartic Lions.

Their opponents were Stiff Wind, whose lineup consisted of tonnnek, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Catrice.

The final carried an unusual storyline because tonnnek had been Meyer's teammate during Antartic Lions' championship in Edition #8 before joining Stiff Wind for the following tournament.

Stiff Wind opened the scoring through Catrice.

Antartic Lions responded through a combination between YaaDon and Meyer. YaaDon created the opportunity and Meyer scored the equalizer, bringing the final back level.

Stiff Wind eventually restored its advantage and moved ahead 2–1.

Antartic Lions were unable to find another equalizer, leaving Meyer with a runner-up finish one edition after his championship.

Final: Stiff Wind 2–1 Antartic Lions
Meyer: 1 Goal
Assist: YaaDon
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

Meyer represented Israel in major international competition and became a Nations Cup champion during the HTML5 era.

Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy: International Champion

Meyer was part of the Israeli national team that reached the final of Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Israel lined up with Yannex, P4ER, Unique and Meyer.

Their opponents were Romania, represented by El Drago, aMp, GirlontaR and Hakut.

Israel won the tournament, giving Meyer a major international championship and adding another important title to a career that had begun at the highest level during the Flash era.

Israel: Yannex • P4ER • Unique • Meyer
Romania: El Drago • aMp • GirlontaR • Hakut
Result: 🏆 Winner

From Flash to HTML5

Meyer's competitive career extended across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

During Flash, he established himself as a successful striker with Antartic Lions, reaching consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals and becoming a European champion.

Years later, Meyer remained capable of competing in major tournaments during HTML5 and became an international champion with Israel at Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Winning major titles in different periods of the game added an important element of longevity to his career.

Playing Style

Meyer played primarily as a striker.

At his strongest, he was regarded as one of the leading and talented strikers of his generation, possessing the attacking quality required to play for championship-level teams.

His equalizing goal in the HaxBall Champions League #9 final provided one of the clearest examples of his ability to contribute on the biggest stage. After YaaDon created the opportunity, Meyer converted to bring Antartic Lions back into the match.

His career was ultimately defined less by extensive individual statistics and more by his attacking level, major final appearances and ability to remain part of successful teams across different eras.

Legacy

Meyer is remembered as one of the leading Israeli strikers of his generation and as an important player in Antartic Lions' strongest HaxBall Champions League period.

His HaxBall Champions League #8 championship gave him success on the European stage, while his return to the final in #9 demonstrated that Antartic Lions remained competitive at the highest level across consecutive editions.

Meyer's equalizing goal against Stiff Wind in the #9 final remains one of the clearest individual moments attached to his European career.

His later Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy championship with Israel added international success and demonstrated the longevity of a career that stretched from Flash into HTML5.

With two major championships from three documented major finals, Meyer built a successful record across both club and international competition.
