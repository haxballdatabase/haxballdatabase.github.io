Deliric is a HaxBall player from Romania who competed during the Flash era. He was regarded as one of the leading Romanian players of his era, alongside DrG.

At a time when Romania had a relatively small presence in top-level competitive HaxBall, Deliric and DrG became two of the country's most notable players.

Deliric won the HaxBall Champions League #14 with Omertà, scoring the Golden Goal in the final against Klub Emeryta. He also represented Romania in the 2017 Nations Cup final.

Biography

Deliric emerged as a notable Romanian player during the Flash era.

Alongside DrG, he stood out among the relatively small group of Romanian players competing at the highest level during the period.

His two major recorded achievements came at club and international level, winning the HaxBall Champions League with Omertà and later appearing in a major international final with Romania.

Omertà

Deliric's most notable club association came with Omertà.

During HaxBall Champions League #14, Omertà lined up with Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

The team reached the final against Klub Emeryta, where Deliric would eventually score the championship-winning goal.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Deliric's major recorded HaxBall Champions League campaign came in Edition #14 with Omertà.

The campaign ended with Deliric scoring the decisive goal in the final, giving him one of the defining moments of the championship match.

HaxBall Champions League #14: A Romanian Duel

Omertà faced Klub Emeryta in the HaxBall Champions League #14 final. Deliric lined up alongside Pringles, aguero10 and Mamba, while Klub Emeryta began with kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, with ANDREA also appearing during the match.

The final placed two of Romania's notable players of the era on opposite sides, with Deliric representing Omertà and DrG representing Klub Emeryta.

Klub Emeryta struck first through DrG, but Omertà responded after the break. Mamba scored twice to turn the match around and give Omertà a 2–1 lead.

Klub Emeryta recovered when Liebe scored the 2–2 equalizer. Neither side could find another goal during the remaining regulation period or the first stage of extra time, sending the championship into Golden Goal.

The decisive moment came through the two Romanian players. After a physical battle between Deliric and DrG, Deliric managed to finish the opportunity and score the Golden Goal, giving Omertà a 3–2 victory and the HaxBall Champions League title.

International Career

Deliric also represented Romania at international level, with his most notable recorded national-team campaign coming in the 2017 Nations Cup.

Nations Cup 2017: International Run

Deliric represented Romania during the 2017 Nations Cup, helping the national team reach the final alongside elite, DrG and Kappa.

Romania faced Turkey in the final. The Turkish squad featured Liéds, Gilbert, Vanessa, Marble and Misaj.

For a Romanian competitive scene with a relatively limited presence at the highest international level during the era, reaching the Nations Cup final represented an important achievement.

Romania ultimately finished as runner-up, giving Deliric a major international final appearance alongside his HaxBall Champions League success.

Legacy

Deliric is primarily remembered for his decisive role in Omertà's HaxBall Champions League #14 championship.

His Golden Goal against Klub Emeryta personally secured the championship, while his run to the 2017 Nations Cup final with Romania added an important international achievement to his career.

The two campaigns also created an unusual connection with DrG: the pair represented Romania together on the international stage, before finding themselves on opposite sides of a HaxBall Champions League final decided by Deliric's Golden Goal.
