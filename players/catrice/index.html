Catrice is a competitive HaxBall player regarded as one of the best strikers of the Flash era. Playing primarily as an ST, he combined major team success with several decisive performances in HaxBall Champions League finals.

Catrice won the HaxBall Champions League three times, capturing titles in Editions #9, #12 and #16. His championships came with Stiff Wind in #9 and #16 and with Skins in #12.

His finals record included some of the most productive attacking performances of the period, most notably a four-goal performance in the #12 final and the championship-winning Golden Goal in #16.

Biography

Catrice established his reputation during the Flash era, where he became one of the most successful strikers in European HaxBall competition.

He became particularly associated with Stiff Wind, joining a team built around players such as ROCKY BALBOA and R9. Catrice played at ST in Stiff Wind's first championship lineup and later returned to the position when the club won its third title.

Between those two championships, Catrice also represented Skins in HaxBall Champions League #12 and delivered one of the strongest individual final performances of the Flash era.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Catrice's HaxBall Champions League career was defined by success across multiple teams and periods of the Flash era.

He won three championships, appearing in the title-winning lineups of Stiff Wind in #9, Skins in #12 and Stiff Wind again in #16.

HaxBall Champions League #9: The First Crown

Catrice won his first HaxBall Champions League title with Stiff Wind in Edition #9.

The championship lineup consisted of Tonnnek, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Catrice, with Catrice occupying the striker position.

Stiff Wind faced Antarctic Lions in the final. Catrice opened the scoring and later contributed to the attack that produced Stiff Wind's second goal.

Stiff Wind won the match 2–1, giving Catrice his first European championship.

Championship Lineup (#9): Tonnnek • R9 • ROCKY BALBOA • Catrice

HaxBall Champions League #12: Four Goals in the Final

Catrice's second championship came with Skins in HaxBall Champions League #12.

Skins faced Pas De Nom in the final with a lineup consisting of Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Catrice opened the scoring after receiving a pass from Elysion. The same combination produced the second goal early in the second half, giving Skins a 2–0 lead.

He later reacted to a rebound behind the Pas De Nom defense and completed his hat-trick.

Pas De Nom pulled one goal back, but Catrice struck again in the final seconds. He reacted first to another loose ball and scored his fourth goal of the match.

Skins won the final 4–1, meaning Catrice scored all four of his team's goals. It became one of the clearest examples of his finishing ability and big-match attacking impact.

Championship Lineup (#12): Liéds • Gilbert • Elysion • Catrice

HaxBall Champions League #16: The Golden Goal Comeback

Catrice later returned to the top with Stiff Wind in HaxBall Champions League #16.

The final lineup featured PiperSalvia in goal, ROCKY BALBOA at DM, Altidore at AM and Catrice at ST.

Their opponents were Klub Emeryta, who took control of the match early. Two goals from aguero10 gave Klub Emeryta a 2–0 lead.

Near the end of the first half, Stiff Wind produced a rapid comeback. Altidore found Catrice, who scored to reduce the deficit to 2–1.

Moments later, the roles were reversed. Catrice provided the assist for Altidore, who equalized at 2–2.

The second half remained scoreless and the championship moved into Golden Goal.

Catrice then scored the decisive goal in extra time, completing Stiff Wind's 3–2 comeback victory and securing his third HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Lineup (#16): PiperSalvia • ROCKY BALBOA • Altidore • Catrice

Playing Style

Catrice played primarily as a striker and built his reputation around his ability to finish attacking situations at the highest level.

His major finals provide several examples of his attacking qualities. He was capable of converting direct chances, reacting quickly to rebounds and loose balls, and making an impact in crowded situations around goal.

The HaxBall Champions League #12 final represented the clearest statistical example of his finishing. Scoring all four goals in a European final demonstrated the level of attacking responsibility he could carry.

His #16 performance also showed that his contribution was not limited to goals. Catrice scored during Stiff Wind's comeback, assisted Altidore for the equalizer and then delivered the Golden Goal himself.

These performances contributed to Catrice being regarded as one of the best strikers of the Flash era.

Community Reputation

Catrice's reputation within the competitive scene was not based solely on his performances on the pitch.

Despite his ability and success, Catrice was also remembered by parts of the competitive community for a toxic and confrontational attitude. This reputation became another part of how he was perceived during his playing career.

As a result, Catrice's historical profile contains a clear contrast: an exceptionally successful striker with major performances in some of the era's biggest matches, but also a player whose behaviour could create controversy away from the game itself.

Legacy

Catrice left behind one of the strongest HaxBall Champions League records among strikers of the Flash era.

His three championships were accompanied by major individual contributions in finals. The four-goal performance against Pas De Nom in #12 and his decisive involvement in Stiff Wind's comeback against Klub Emeryta in #16 stand among the clearest examples.

Winning with two different teams also demonstrated that his European success was not limited to a single lineup. He became champion twice with Stiff Wind and once with Skins.

While his toxic reputation remained a controversial part of his presence in the community, his competitive record and performances established Catrice as one of the leading strikers of the Flash era.
