SAKY is a Portuguese HaxBall player widely regarded as one of the greatest players the game has ever seen. One of the leading attacking players of his generation, he became known for his physical strength, direct attacking presence and ability to decide important matches.

His career was highlighted by three HaxBall Champions League titles, including consecutive championships in Editions #17 and #18. His performances in major matches established him as one of the most accomplished players of his era.

SAKY's reputation was built not only on trophies, but on the way he could influence difficult games. Particularly dangerous in physical battles and crowded attacking situations, he repeatedly produced important goals when matches were at their most tense.

At international level, SAKY became a Nations Cup champion with Portugal in 2018. He later reached two more major finals with Portugal in 2019, finishing runner-up in both Nations Cup 2019 and Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

Biography & Early Career

SAKY emerged during a highly competitive period of European HaxBall and progressed rapidly through the competitive scene. Only a few seasons before reaching the highest level, he had been competing in Division 2.

A major part of his early rise came alongside xSha. The two developed into one of the most dangerous young attacking partnerships of the period and eventually joined Klub Emeryta, where they played alongside kojo and Ter.

Their progression was remarkably quick. Despite still being relatively early in their careers, SAKY and xSha were already capable of deciding matches against experienced opponents at the highest level. That rise culminated in HaxBall Champions League #17, where Klub Emeryta won the championship and SAKY produced a goal and an assist in the final.

SAKY did not remain tied to a single team. In the following edition he joined The Elders and immediately won another HaxBall Champions League title, becoming a back-to-back champion with two different teams.

His rise also extended to international competition. With Portugal, SAKY won the 2018 Nations Cup, providing the assist for Bot's decisive goal in the 3–2 final victory over Turkey.

Years later, he again reached the top of European competition with Flaviu 1997. By that stage he was already an established veteran and joined an international lineup featuring PiperSalvia, Misaj and Marble, eventually adding a third HaxBall Champions League title to his career.

HaxBall Champions League Career

SAKY won the HaxBall Champions League three times, lifting the trophy with Klub Emeryta, The Elders and Flaviu 1997.

His first two championships came in consecutive editions, while his third arrived several seasons later as part of one of the most talented international squads assembled during the later era of the competition.

HaxBall Champions League #17: The Breakthrough

HaxBall Champions League #17 became the tournament in which SAKY announced himself on the biggest European stage.

Klub Emeryta entered the final with a lineup consisting of kojo, Ter, SAKY and xSha. SAKY and xSha were still young players who had competed in Division 2 only a few seasons earlier, but together they had developed into a dangerous attacking partnership.

Their opponents were BLACKOUT, the Seeders core playing under another banner. BLACKOUT began the final strongly and controlled much of the early possession, eventually taking a 1–0 lead through Yannex.

Klub Emeryta's response began with SAKY. On a counter-attack, he found xSha, who converted the opportunity to make the score 1–1.

SAKY then produced the next major moment himself. Using his strength in a corner-spam situation, he forced his way through and scored to put Klub Emeryta 2–1 ahead.

BLACKOUT managed to equalize late through Isco, sending the final into Golden Goal. In extra time, xSha finished a rapid counter-attack to complete a 3–2 victory.

For SAKY, the championship represented a remarkable rise. Having only recently been playing in the lower divisions, he had now provided both a goal and an assist in a HaxBall Champions League final and finished the tournament as a European champion.

Championship Lineup (#17): kojo • Ter • SAKY • xSha

HaxBall Champions League #18: Fifteen Seconds to Glory

Only one edition after his breakthrough with Klub Emeryta, SAKY returned to the final in HaxBall Champions League #18, this time representing The Elders.

The Elders entered the final with LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak, facing a Seeders team that had returned under its own name after the disappointment of the previous season.

Regular time ended 0–0, sending the championship into extra time. Seeders struck first when Marble found Yannex, but Vak responded to make it 1–1.

Seeders regained the lead after a chaotic corner sequence resulted in a deflection goal, putting The Elders 2–1 behind.

With less than two minutes remaining in extra time, SAKY delivered. He scored the equalizer to make it 2–2, rescuing The Elders and pushing the final into Golden Goal.

What followed became one of the fastest championship conclusions in HaxBall Champions League history.

Only 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY struck again.

The goal immediately ended the match, completing a 3–2 comeback victory and giving The Elders the championship.

SAKY had scored the final two goals of the match, including the championship-winning Golden Goal, and became a back-to-back HaxBall Champions League winner across two different teams.

Championship Lineup (#18): LaggerMet • P4ER • SAKY • Vak

HaxBall Champions League #23: The All-Star Chapter

Several editions after his back-to-back championships, SAKY returned to the top level of the competition as part of Flaviu 1997.

Originally a Romanian team, Flaviu 1997 had transformed into an international all-star project. Captain and founder Flaviu assembled a squad featuring PiperSalvia, Misaj, SAKY and Marble.

Each player brought a different strength to the team, creating one of the most talented lineups of the later competitive era. SAKY, by then already a two-time HaxBall Champions League champion, added another experienced attacking presence to the squad.

Flaviu 1997 successfully completed its championship campaign and won HaxBall Champions League #23, securing the club's first and only title.

For SAKY, the victory brought his career total to three HaxBall Champions League championships, with his titles now spanning different teams and different periods of the competition.

Championship Lineup (#23): PiperSalvia • Misaj • SAKY • Marble

International Career

SAKY represented Portugal across several major international tournaments, winning the 2018 Nations Cup before reaching two further finals in 2019.

Nations Cup 2018: International Champion

SAKY became an international champion with Portugal at the 2018 Nations Cup.

Portugal lined up with Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot, while Turkey fielded Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Turkey took the lead near the end of the first half through Müllewa, giving the Turkish side a 1–0 advantage.

Portugal equalized during the second half. Following a misunderstanding in the Turkish defense, the ball was left in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity to make it 1–1.

The final then produced a notable fair-play moment. Turkey conceded during a situation affected by ping. Portugal chose not to keep the resulting advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to score and restoring the match to 2–2.

The decisive attack came later when SAKY provided the pass for Bot. Bot converted the opportunity to make it 3–2, securing the Nations Cup championship for Portugal.

Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
SAKY: Assist for the winning goal
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2019

SAKY represented Portugal at Nations Cup 2019 and was part of the Portuguese team that reached the tournament final.

Portugal entered the final against Turkey with a talented squad featuring players including SAKY and Pringles.

The Portuguese side made an excellent start, taking a 2–0 lead and placing Turkey under severe pressure.

Turkey eventually produced a dramatic comeback, forcing extra time before winning the match 5–2. SAKY therefore finished Nations Cup 2019 as runner-up with Portugal.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy: Israel vs Portugal

During the same year, SAKY again represented Portugal in Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy. Portugal reached another international final, this time against Israel.

The two finalists entered the match with the following lineups:

Israel: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Portugal: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY

The final developed into a difficult and closely contested match. Neither side managed to decide the championship during regulation time, and the match continued into extra time.

The decisive moment came when Ter attempted to play the ball backwards. His touch instead left the ball directly in front of LaggerMet.

LaggerMet converted the opportunity and scored the decisive extra-time goal, securing Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy for Israel. SAKY and Portugal therefore finished as runners-up.

Portugal Final Lineup: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY
Result: 🥈 Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy Runner-up

Playing Style

SAKY was primarily known as an attacking player, with a game particularly suited to physical battles, crowded areas and situations in which control of the ball had to be fought for rather than freely created.

One of the clearest characteristics of his play was his strength in spamming situations. Rather than avoiding contact-heavy exchanges, SAKY was capable of using them as an attacking weapon, forcing the ball through defenders and creating scoring opportunities from situations that initially appeared blocked.

This was especially dangerous around corners and the goal area. His goal in the HaxBall Champions League #17 final came directly from this kind of situation, when his strength in a corner-spam battle put Klub Emeryta ahead.

He was also capable of combining effectively with other strikers. His partnership with xSha during the Klub Emeryta period demonstrated that his game was not based solely on scoring, with SAKY also creating the equalizer for his attacking partner in the #17 final.

His assist for Bot's championship-winning goal in the 2018 Nations Cup final provided another example of his ability to contribute creatively in decisive situations.

Perhaps the strongest feature of SAKY's career was his ability to remain dangerous in decisive moments. His two late goals against Seeders in the HaxBall Champions League #18 final, particularly the championship-winning strike only 15 seconds into Golden Goal, became a perfect example of that quality.

Legacy

SAKY is remembered as one of the greatest players in HaxBall history, with a career highlighted by three HaxBall Champions League championships and repeated decisive performances in major matches.

His first title with Klub Emeryta represented one of the fastest rises of his generation. Only a short time after competing in lower divisions, SAKY was already contributing a goal and an assist in a HaxBall Champions League final.

The following edition strengthened that reputation considerably. With The Elders trailing late in extra time against Seeders, SAKY scored the equalizer before delivering the Golden Goal only 15 seconds later. The performance gave him consecutive HaxBall Champions League titles with two different teams.

His international record added another major championship to his career. SAKY won the 2018 Nations Cup with Portugal, providing the assist for Bot's decisive goal in the 3–2 final victory over Turkey.

His return to the championship stage with Flaviu 1997 years later demonstrated the longevity of his career. Playing alongside Misaj, PiperSalvia and Marble, SAKY became a European champion for the third time.

Portugal later reached two more major finals in 2019, finishing runner-up against Turkey in Nations Cup 2019 before reaching another final against Israel in Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

Across different teams and eras, SAKY remained closely associated with direct attacking play, physical strength and decisive performances under pressure. His three HaxBall Champions League championships and Nations Cup title place him among the most accomplished players of his generation.
