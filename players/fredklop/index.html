Fredklop is a competitive HaxBall player best known as one of the core members of Nerdlucks, the team that won the first two editions of the HaxBall Champions League.

His career is closely connected to the beginning of organized European HaxBall competition. As part of the Nerdlucks generation, Fredklop competed during a period when many of the tactical structures and positional ideas later associated with high-level HaxBall were still developing.

One of the notable aspects of Fredklop's early career was his positional versatility. He initially operated as a goalkeeper, but later moved into the DM position as Nerdlucks adjusted its lineup for HaxBall Champions League #2.

That transition allowed Fredklop to remain an important part of the team across two different championship-winning lineups and two different roles.

At club level, Fredklop won HaxBall Champions League #1 and #2 consecutively with Nerdlucks. The achievement made him one of the first players in the history of the competition to become a two-time HCL champion.

By winning the opening two editions without interruption, Fredklop also became part of the first back-to-back championship run in HaxBall Champions League history.

Biography

Fredklop competed during the earliest period of organized European HaxBall and became one of the central players of Nerdlucks during the team's historic opening run in the HaxBall Champions League.

Nerdlucks emerged at a time when competitive HaxBall was still establishing its tactical identity. The team quickly became one of the strongest sides of the period and won the first two editions of the HaxBall Champions League.

Fredklop played in both championship-winning lineups, but his role changed between the two tournaments. That positional transition became one of the most distinctive parts of his early career.

Nerdlucks

Fredklop became primarily associated with Nerdlucks, one of the foundational teams of early competitive HaxBall.

The team became the first champion in HaxBall Champions League history and immediately defended its title in the following edition, establishing the first major dynasty of the competition.

The championship generation included players such as aguero10, Rene, Nesquik, Fredklop and later GQKU. The core played an important role in establishing the first benchmark for sustained success in the competition.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Fredklop won two HaxBall Champions League championships, both with Nerdlucks.

He was part of the championship lineup in the inaugural HaxBall Champions League #1 and remained with the team as it successfully defended the title in Edition #2.

The consecutive championships placed Fredklop among the first players in HCL history to win the competition twice and made him part of the first group of players to achieve back-to-back Champions League titles.

HaxBall Champions League #1: The First Champions

Fredklop's first major championship came in the inaugural edition of the HaxBall Champions League.

Nerdlucks competed with a primary lineup of Fredklop, Nesquik, aguero10 and Rene.

Fredklop operated as the team's goalkeeper during this period, forming part of the structure behind Nerdlucks' first championship run.

By winning Edition #1, Nerdlucks became the first HaxBall Champions League champions, giving Fredklop and his teammates a permanent place in the competition's earliest history.

Championship Lineup (#1): Fredklop • Nesquik • aguero10 • Rene

HaxBall Champions League #2: Back-to-Back Champions

Nerdlucks returned in HaxBall Champions League #2 attempting to defend the first championship in competition history.

The arrival of GQKU produced an important change in the team's structure. With GQKU joining the lineup, Fredklop moved from goalkeeper to DM.

The championship lineup consisted of GQKU, Fredklop, aguero10 and Rene.

Nerdlucks made an immediate impact in the final. Rene scored after just 11 seconds from an aguero10 assist, producing an exceptionally early opening goal.

The team eventually completed a 4–1 victory, successfully defending its championship and becoming the first team to win consecutive editions of the HaxBall Champions League.

For Fredklop, the victory secured his second HCL championship in two editions, this time after completing the tournament in a different position.

Playing Style and Role

The available record of Fredklop's early career shows him operating in two different positions across Nerdlucks' consecutive championship runs.

He initially played as a GK during HaxBall Champions League #1. When GQKU joined the lineup for the following edition, Fredklop moved higher up the pitch and played as a DM.

The change is notable because Nerdlucks remained successful despite altering an important part of its structure. Fredklop retained a place in the championship lineup while adapting to a fundamentally different role.

Beyond this positional versatility, the currently preserved records do not provide enough detailed information to make broader claims about Fredklop's individual playing style.

Legacy

Fredklop's place in HaxBall history is closely tied to the beginning of the HaxBall Champions League and the rise of Nerdlucks.

By winning HCL #1 and #2 consecutively, he became one of the first two-time champions in the history of the competition and part of its first back-to-back championship team.

His presence in both championship lineups is also notable because his role changed between the two tournaments. Fredklop contributed to the first title as a goalkeeper before moving to DM for Nerdlucks' successful title defense.

Together with aguero10, Rene, Nesquik and GQKU, Fredklop formed part of the generation that established Nerdlucks as the first dynasty of European competitive HaxBall.
