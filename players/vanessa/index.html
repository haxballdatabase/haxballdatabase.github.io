Vanessa is a Turkish HaxBall player regarded as one of Turkey's leading players at the Attacking Midfielder (AM) position. She is best known for representing Skins and the Turkish national team.

She reached the HaxBall Champions League #15 final with Skins and later became a major international champion with Turkey by winning the Nations Cup 2017.

Club Career

Vanessa is most closely associated with Skins, with whom she reached the final of the HaxBall Champions League.

HaxBall Champions League #15: Final with Skins

Vanessa was part of the Skins lineup that reached the final of HaxBall Champions League #15.

The final lineup consisted of kojo, Gilbert, Vanessa and Altidore.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, who won the final 3–1. The result left Vanessa and Skins as runners-up.

Final Lineup (#15): kojo • Gilbert • Vanessa • Altidore
Final: Angry Bulls 3–1 Skins
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

Vanessa also represented Turkey in international competition and became a Nations Cup champion in 2017.

Nations Cup 2017: International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2017

Vanessa was part of the Turkish squad that won the standard Nations Cup 2017.

The championship lineup consisted of Liéds, Gilbert, Vanessa, Marble and Misaj.

Turkey: Liéds • Gilbert • Vanessa • Marble • Misaj
Result: 🏆 Winner

Playing Style

Vanessa played primarily as an Attacking Midfielder (AM) and is regarded as one of the leading Turkish players associated with the position.
