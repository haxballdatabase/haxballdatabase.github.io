Overkill is a Turkish HaxBall player from the Flash era, best documented for representing Turkey in major international competition.

His clearest preserved achievement in the current archive is Turkey's run to the ESL CC 2014 final, where he played alongside TinerMania, R9 and Kranjcar.

Turkey faced the Czech Republic in the championship match and finished the tournament as runner-up after a 3–0 defeat.

International Career

ESL CC 2014 Final

Overkill was part of the Turkish lineup that reached the ESL CC 2014 final against the Czech Republic.

Turkey lined up with TinerMania, R9, Overkill and Kranjcar.

The Czech Republic fielded KEANU, Roman, Forsbi and CvRk.

The Czech Republic won the final 3–0, leaving Overkill and Turkey as ESL CC 2014 runners-up.
