Jéal is regarded as one of the stronger AMs of his generation, establishing himself as a high-level attacking midfielder during an important period of competitive HaxBall.

His strongest results came in the HaxBall Champions League, where he reached two finals with two different teams. Jéal finished runner-up with Deathwish in Edition #20 before becoming champion with Pafküf in Edition #22.

His first final also produced one of the most dramatic individual matches of his career. Against Smokin' Aces in HaxBall Champions League #20, Jéal scored twice for Deathwish and temporarily put his team ahead in extra time, before the final took a painful turn and eventually ended in defeat.

Two editions later, Jéal returned to the highest stage as part of a Pafküf lineup featuring Misaj, Necro and Knap. This time, the campaign ended with Jéal lifting his first HaxBall Champions League trophy.

Biography

Jéal became a recognizable AM during his generation through appearances at the highest level of European competition. Rather than being considered among the absolute greatest players of the era, he developed a reputation as a strong and reliable attacking midfielder capable of playing important roles in competitive teams.

A notable part of Jéal's career was his relationship with Misaj. The two were known to be close friends, but HaxBall Champions League #20 temporarily placed them on opposite sides of one of the most controversial matches of the period.

Jéal represented Deathwish, while Misaj played for Angry Bulls. Deathwish also featured former Angry Bulls players Müllewa and Abduction, adding another layer to the meeting between the two teams.

Two editions later, the situation had changed completely. Jéal and Misaj became teammates with Pafküf, where they eventually won the HaxBall Champions League together alongside Necro and Knap.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Jéal reached two HaxBall Champions League finals during his career, first with Deathwish in Edition #20 and later with Pafküf in Edition #22. His record across those finals stands at one championship and one runner-up finish.

HaxBall Champions League #20: Two Goals, One Step Short

Jéal's first HaxBall Champions League final appearance came during a dramatic campaign with Deathwish. The edition became memorable for two very different reasons: the controversial semi-final against Angry Bulls and an emotional 4–3 final against Smokin' Aces.

Semi-final: The Angry Bulls Controversy

Deathwish faced Angry Bulls in the semi-final. During the match, Angry Bulls goalkeeper Liéds suffered severe connection problems, leading to a major fair-play dispute between the two teams.

The Deathwish side included Müllewa, Abduction and Jéal, while Misaj was one of Angry Bulls' central players. The situation carried an additional personal element for Jéal because he and Misaj had previously been close friends.

Despite Misaj producing an extraordinary individual performance of three goals and two assists, Deathwish eliminated Angry Bulls and secured their place in the final.

The events of the match created tension between Misaj and several Deathwish players, including a deterioration in his previously close relationship with Jéal. For Jéal, however, the victory meant reaching his first HaxBall Champions League final.

Final: Heartbreak Against Smokin' Aces

In the final, Deathwish faced Smokin' Aces, whose championship lineup featured vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha.

After Deathwish fell behind, a move involving Müllewa created the equalizing opportunity. Müllewa's attempt took a deflection and Jéal reacted quickly to the loose ball, finishing the chance to make the score 1–1.

Smokin' Aces regained the advantage, but Deathwish responded again and brought the match back level at 2–2, keeping the championship completely open.

The match then moved into extra time, where Jéal delivered another major moment. Müllewa found Jéal in attack and the AM converted the opportunity to put Deathwish 3–2 ahead.

Having already scored twice in the final, Jéal was suddenly one of the central figures in what appeared capable of becoming a championship-winning performance.

The match then took a painful turn for him. While attempting to defend a Smokin' Aces attack, Jéal accidentally put the ball into his own goal, bringing the score back to 3–3.

The own goal was an especially difficult moment for Jéal. After scoring twice at the opposite end and helping place Deathwish within reach of the trophy, he was left devastated by the mistake that brought Smokin' Aces back into the final.

With the score level once again, the championship was decided by Golden Goal. xSha produced the final decisive moment, scoring to complete a 4–3 victory for Smokin' Aces.

For Jéal, the final became one of the most emotional matches of his career. He had scored two goals, helped Deathwish move 3–2 ahead in extra time and come extremely close to winning the competition, only for the own goal and eventual Golden Goal defeat to turn the night into heartbreak.

HaxBall Champions League #22: Back to His Roots

Two editions after his runner-up finish with Deathwish, Jéal returned to Pafküf, the club with which he had long been associated, for another HaxBall Champions League campaign.

The lineup brought together Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro. One of the unusual aspects of the team was its decision to use Knap, naturally an outfield player, in goal.

The campaign also reunited Jéal with Misaj. Their previously close relationship had deteriorated after the events surrounding HaxBall Champions League #20, when they had found themselves on opposite sides of the controversial Angry Bulls and Deathwish semi-final. By Edition #22, however, the two had reconciled and were once again playing together.

Pafküf faced immediate danger in the quarter-finals against Invincible. The team fell 3–0 behind early and appeared close to elimination, but recovered to complete a dramatic comeback and keep the campaign alive.

After surviving that test, Pafküf defeated Wicked Guys in the semi-finals and advanced to the final against Romanian side FEAR.

The final developed very differently from Jéal's previous championship match. Pafküf took control early and built a commanding 3–0 lead by halftime.

Misaj contributed one goal and two assists during the final, while the Pafküf lineup maintained control of the match and completed the championship run.

The victory gave Jéal his first HaxBall Champions League title. Returning to the club he had long been associated with, reconciling with Misaj and finally lifting the trophy together gave the championship a particularly fitting conclusion to the Pafküf chapter of his career.

Championship Lineup (#22): Knap • Misaj • Jéal • Necro

Playing Style

Jéal played primarily as an AM and was regarded as one of the stronger players in that position among his generation.

His reputation should not be confused with that of the absolute all-time elite, but Jéal consistently showed that he could compete at the highest level and contribute within teams capable of reaching major finals.

The surviving match record also shows that Jéal was capable of providing a direct goal threat from midfield. The clearest example came in the HaxBall Champions League #20 final, where he scored twice against Smokin' Aces and briefly put Deathwish ahead in extra time.

His later championship with Pafküf showed another side of his career. Playing alongside Misaj and Necro, Jéal formed part of a balanced attacking structure that recovered from difficult situations and ultimately won the competition.

Legacy

Jéal is remembered as one of the stronger AMs of his generation, with a career that included important appearances at the highest level of European HaxBall.

His HaxBall Champions League record gives his career a clear arc. He first came close to the trophy with Deathwish, producing a dramatic two-goal performance in a final that ultimately ended in heartbreak, before returning two editions later and becoming champion with Pafküf.

The contrast between Editions #20 and #22 also created an interesting personal story. Jéal and Misaj had once been close friends, but their relationship deteriorated following the controversial Angry Bulls and Deathwish semi-final. By Edition #22, however, the two had reconciled and reunited at Pafküf, the club Jéal had long been associated with. Returning under a familiar banner brought their partnership back together, and this time they completed their unfinished business by winning the HaxBall Champions League together.

With two HaxBall Champions League finals, one championship and a memorable two-goal performance in a final, Jéal built a respectable career as one of the notable AM players of his generation.
