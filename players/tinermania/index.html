TinerMania is a Turkish HaxBall player from the early Flash era who played as a GK.

He became part of Silas Beavers during one of the club's most important early periods and won the HaxBall Champions League #3.

Later in his career, after Silas Beavers had disbanded, TinerMania also played for Angels of Death, one of Silas' former major rivals.

At international level, he also represented Turkey and reached the ESL CC 2014 final.

Silas Beavers and Angels of Death

TinerMania was part of Silas Beavers during one of the most successful periods in the club's early history.

Silas Beavers and Angels of Death had previously developed one of the major rivalries of the early Turkish HaxBall scene.

After Silas Beavers was disbanded, TinerMania later became an Angels of Death player. His career therefore connected him with both sides of one of the most recognizable rivalries from the period.

His time with AoD came after the end of Silas Beavers rather than as a direct transfer between the two active rivals.

HaxBall Champions League #3

TinerMania's major competitive achievement came with Silas Beavers in HaxBall Champions League #3.

Silas Beavers lined up with TinerMania, Dexter, Doctore and Clinical Striker in the championship final.

Their opponents were Angels of Death, already one of Silas Beavers' major Turkish rivals. The final developed into an unusually long contest lasting approximately 60 minutes.

Silas Beavers eventually won the match and secured its first HaxBall Champions League championship, with TinerMania serving as the team's GK.

International Career

TinerMania also represented Turkey during the early international competitive period.

ESL CC 2014 Final

TinerMania was part of the Turkish lineup that reached the ESL CC 2014 final against the Czech Republic.

Turkey lined up with TinerMania, R9, Overkill and Kranjcar, while the Czech Republic fielded KEANU, Roman, Forsbi and CvRk.

The Czech Republic won the final 3–0, leaving TinerMania and Turkey as runners-up.

Turkey: TinerMania • R9 • Overkill • Kranjcar
Czech Republic: KEANU • Roman • Forsbi • CvRk
Final: Turkey 0–3 Czech Republic
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Legacy

TinerMania is remembered as a respected player in Turkish HaxBall history and one of the recognizable names from the early Flash era.

His HaxBall Champions League championship with Silas Beavers, his appearance in the ESL CC 2014 final with Turkey and his later period with Angels of Death connected his career with several important parts of early Turkish HaxBall history.
