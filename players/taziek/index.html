Taziek is a Portuguese competitive HaxBall player regarded as one of the leading Portuguese players of his generation. A talented and elite-level player within the European competitive scene, he combined strong technical ability with particular strength in physical and spam-heavy situations.

His major achievements include winning HaxBall Champions League #24 with Wicked Guys and reaching an international final with the Portuguese National Team.

His defining club moment came in the HaxBall Champions League #24 Grand Final, where Taziek scored the only goal in Wicked Guys' 1–0 victory over Good Guys, directly deciding the championship.

At international level, Taziek was also part of the Portugal lineup that reached the final of Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy, competing alongside Pringles, Ter and SAKY.

Biography

Taziek emerged as one of the leading players of the Portuguese competitive scene, establishing himself as a talented player capable of competing at elite European level. He represented Portugal in international competition and later became associated with Wicked Guys at club level.

His preserved competitive record includes appearances on both the international and European championship stages. With Portugal, he reached the final of Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy. With Wicked Guys, he later achieved the most important club success of his recorded career by winning the HaxBall Champions League.

The HCL #24 championship became particularly significant because Taziek was not simply part of the winning lineup. He scored the goal that ultimately decided the Grand Final.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Taziek's principal European achievement came with Wicked Guys in HaxBall Champions League #24.

HaxBall Champions League #24: The Championship Goal

Wicked Guys reached the Grand Final with a lineup consisting of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Their opponents were Good Guys, whose final lineup consisted of gggggk, B ull, William and Torment.

The decisive moment arrived during the opening minutes of the first half. Taziek found enough space to take a shot and placed the ball into the corner, giving Wicked Guys an early 1–0 lead.

The narrow advantage remained under pressure throughout the match. Good Guys stayed within one goal, but Wicked Guys successfully protected the lead and prevented an equalizer.

No further goals were scored. The final ended 1–0, meaning Taziek's strike became the goal that directly delivered Wicked Guys its first HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Lineup (#24): Wiagoal • Vichoexe • Taziek • Makenzie
Final: Wicked Guys 1–0 Good Guys
Championship Goal: Taziek

International Career

Taziek represented the Portuguese National Team in international HaxBall competition.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy: International Final

Portugal reached the final of Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy, where Taziek lined up alongside Pringles, Ter and SAKY.

Their opponents were Israel, whose lineup consisted of Yannex, Isco, P4ER and LaggerMet.

The final remained closely contested throughout regulation time, with neither team able to secure the championship before the match continued into extra time.

The decisive moment came when Ter attempted to play the ball backwards. The touch instead left the ball directly in front of LaggerMet, who immediately converted the opportunity.

The goal decided the championship in Israel's favor, leaving Taziek and Portugal as Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy runners-up.

Portugal Final Lineup: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY

Playing Style

Taziek was known as a naturally talented and technically capable player, with the overall quality required to compete at an elite level in European HaxBall.

One of the more recognizable strengths of his game was his effectiveness in spam-heavy situations. Taziek possessed considerable strength in repeated physical exchanges for the ball and was capable of gaining an advantage in crowded areas where possession could not always be created through clean passing alone.

This made him particularly effective around the goal area and in situations where several players were competing for control of the same ball. His physical strength in these exchanges complemented his technical ability and gave him another way to influence difficult matches.

Rather than relying entirely on one specific aspect of his game, Taziek combined natural talent, technical quality and physical effectiveness, allowing him to contribute in several different types of match situations.

His overall level made him one of the stronger representatives of the Portuguese competitive scene during his generation, eventually competing at both Champions League and international-final level.

Legacy

Taziek is remembered as one of the notable Portuguese players of his generation. His talent, strength in spam-heavy situations and ability to perform at a high competitive level allowed him to represent his country internationally while also achieving the biggest club title in European HaxBall.

His strongest individual historical moment came in HaxBall Champions League #24. In a final ultimately decided by a single goal, Taziek scored the only goal of the match and directly secured the European championship for Wicked Guys.

His international appearances with Portugal further reflected his standing within the Portuguese scene, with Taziek forming part of a national-team lineup capable of reaching a major international final.

That combination of international representation, elite-level ability and a decisive Champions League final goal gives Taziek a clear place in the competitive history of both Portuguese and European HaxBall.
