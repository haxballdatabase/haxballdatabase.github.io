Vak is regarded as one of the best attacking players in HaxBall history, building a reputation through prolific scoring in the Feed-Me League and success on the European stage.

He became particularly well known through Feed-Me, where he won the league three times and finished as the competition's top scorer twice. His strongest individual statistical achievement came when he scored 47 goals in a single season, setting the record for the most goals scored by one player in a Feed-Me League campaign.

Vak shares the 47-goal single-season record with Marble, placing the pair together at the top of one of the league's most important attacking records.

His European career also included a major success with The Elders in HaxBall Champions League #18. Vak played a decisive role in the final against Seeders, scoring an equalizer and later creating another as The Elders eventually won the championship through Golden Goal.

Biography

Vak established himself as one of the leading attacking players of his era through a combination of domestic scoring success and performances in major European competition.

A large part of his reputation was built in the Feed-Me League. Vak won three league championships and claimed two top-scorer titles, becoming one of the competition's most recognizable attacking figures.

His ability also translated to the HaxBall Champions League. In Edition #18, he represented The Elders alongside LaggerMet, P4ER and SAKY, with the team going on to win the competition.

Feed-Me League

Vak became one of the leading attacking figures in Feed-Me League history. His career in the competition combined both individual scoring success and sustained team achievement.

He won the Feed-Me League three times and finished as the league's top scorer on two occasions. These achievements made him one of the more decorated attacking players of his period.

His reputation in Feed-Me was built primarily around his ability to consistently produce goals. At his peak, Vak was capable of carrying a substantial part of a team's attacking output himself, culminating in one of the strongest individual scoring seasons recorded in the league.

47 Goals: The Single-Season Record

Vak scored 47 goals in a single Feed-Me League season, setting the competition record for the most goals scored by one player during a league campaign.

The same mark was also reached by Marble, meaning Vak and Marble share the record at 47 goals.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Vak's principal European achievement came with The Elders during HaxBall Champions League #18.

The Elders reached the final with a lineup consisting of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak, where they faced a highly successful Seeders side.

HaxBall Champions League #18: The Elders Comeback

The final against Seeders remained scoreless through regular time before the match moved into extra time.

Seeders struck first when Marble created the opening for Yannex, putting The Elders 1–0 behind.

Vak responded for The Elders. From a corner-spam situation, he found the equalizer to bring the score back to 1–1.

Seeders regained the lead at 2–1, putting The Elders under pressure once again. With the match moving toward its conclusion, Vak became involved in another crucial moment, providing the assist for SAKY to score the late 2–2 equalizer.

The final then moved into Golden Goal. Only 15 seconds into the deciding period, SAKY scored again to complete the comeback and give The Elders a 3–2 victory.

Vak therefore contributed directly to two of The Elders' three goals in the final, scoring one and assisting another on the way to his HaxBall Champions League championship.

Playing Style

Vak's game was centered around his ability to influence matches in attacking areas. His scoring record shows a player capable of producing goals at an unusually high rate, making him one of the most dangerous offensive players of his period.

His attacking contribution was not limited only to finishing. The HaxBall Champions League #18 final provided a clear example: Vak scored The Elders' first goal himself before later creating the second for SAKY.

He was also effective in crowded attacking situations. His equalizer in the #18 final came through a corner-spam finish, showing his ability to convert opportunities even when space around goal was limited.

What separated Vak statistically was his scoring output over entire league campaigns. His two Feed-Me top-scorer titles demonstrated that his goalscoring was repeatable across different seasons, while the 47-goal campaign represented the peak of that production.

Legacy

Vak's place in HaxBall history is closely tied to his reputation as one of the game's leading attacking players.

His Feed-Me career combined both individual and team success. Vak won the league three times, finished as top scorer twice and produced one of the competition's most notable individual records.

By reaching 47 goals in a single season, he established a benchmark that is shared only with Marble, leaving both players as joint holders of the Feed-Me single-season scoring record.

Vak also made his attacking quality count on the European stage. In the HaxBall Champions League #18 final, his goal and assist played an important part in The Elders' comeback before SAKY completed the victory in Golden Goal.

With three Feed-Me championships, two scoring titles, the joint single-season goals record and a HaxBall Champions League championship, Vak built one of the stronger attacking résumés of his era.
