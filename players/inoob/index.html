iNoob is a HaxBall player from the Flash era, best documented for reaching two consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals. He finished as runner-up with TI SPARTA! in HaxBall Champions League #5 and returned to the final one edition later with Die Galaktischen.

The two campaigns place iNoob among the players who appeared in back-to-back Champions League finals during the early competitive era. His HCL #5 run ended in a 5–3 defeat against Zebras, while HCL #6 ended with a 4–0 defeat against Hellhounds.

Biography

iNoob competed during the Flash era and is primarily documented in the archive through his appearances in HaxBall Champions League #5 and #6.

In Edition #5, he appeared for TI SPARTA! in the final against Zebras. One season later, he reached the championship match again, this time as part of Die Galaktischen alongside Caccapupù, Nino and Razmo.

HaxBall Champions League Career

iNoob reached consecutive Champions League finals in Editions #5 and #6, finishing as runner-up in both campaigns.

HaxBall Champions League #5

iNoob reached the HaxBall Champions League #5 final with TI SPARTA!. The surviving record identifies Rooney1878, Alan10 and iNoob among the players on the TI SPARTA! side.

Their opponents were Zebras, represented by Иukroyš, Dexter, Doctore playing under the name David Luiz, and Kranjcar.

The match became one of the more unusual early Champions League finals because of multiple restarts, fair-play disputes and an administrative intervention before the second half.

Late in the match, with TI SPARTA! trailing 4–3, iNoob became involved in a direct battle with David Luiz. The ball eventually deflected off iNoob and travelled into the TI SPARTA! goal, producing the final 5–3 scoreline.

Team: TI SPARTA!
Final: TI SPARTA! 3–5 Zebras
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #6

iNoob returned to the Champions League final in Edition #6 with Die Galaktischen. The final lineup consisted of iNoob, Caccapupù, Nino and Razmo.

They faced Hellhounds, whose championship lineup featured Adriano, aguero10, Stingox and Ced.

Hellhounds controlled the final and won 4–0. The fourth goal came after pressure involving aguero10 and iNoob caused a decisive deflection around the Die Galaktischen goal.

Final Lineup: Caccapupù • iNoob • Nino • Razmo
Final: Die Galaktischen 0–4 Hellhounds
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Legacy

iNoob's clearest documented legacy comes from reaching back-to-back Champions League finals with two different teams during the Flash era.

Although both campaigns ended as runner-up finishes, appearances in the HCL #5 and HCL #6 finals place him directly within two consecutive editions of early Champions League history.
