Biography

Wiagoal is a Turkish-origin player whose documented competitive career is closely connected with Wicked Guys. He played as a goalkeeper for the club and became a HaxBall Champions League champion with the team.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #22

Wiagoal was part of the Wicked Guys side that reached the semi-finals of HaxBall Champions League #22.

Wicked Guys faced eventual champions Pafküf with a lineup of Wiagoal, Bullas, Vichoexe and Buub.

Pafküf won the semi-final and advanced to the Grand Final, ending Wicked Guys' campaign one stage before the championship match.

Wicked Guys: Wiagoal • Bullas • Vichoexe • Buub
Opponent: Pafküf
Stage: Semi-final

🎥 Semi-Final Replay: Pafküf vs. Wicked Guys

HaxBall Champions League #24: Champion

Wiagoal later won the HaxBall Champions League #24 with Wicked Guys.

The championship lineup consisted of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Their opponents in the Grand Final were Good Guys. An early goal from Taziek gave Wicked Guys the lead.

Wicked Guys protected the advantage throughout the match and completed a 1–0 victory, giving the club its first HaxBall Champions League championship and Wiagoal his first documented HCL title.

Final: Wicked Guys 1–0 Good Guys
Championship Lineup: Wiagoal • Vichoexe • Taziek • Makenzie
Result: 🏆 Winner
