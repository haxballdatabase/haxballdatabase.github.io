Adekyz is a Turkish HaxBall player from the Flash era best known for his association with Pas De Nom and his decisive performance in the HaxBall Champions League #7 final.

Although his wider competitive career was not defined by a large number of major achievements, his contribution to Pas De Nom's greatest championship became enough to establish him as a Pas De Nom legend.

His appearance in the final came under unusual circumstances. Kranjcar had played for Pas De Nom during the earlier stages of the competition but was unable to attend on the day of the final, leading to Adekyz taking his place in the lineup.

Adekyz responded by producing the defining performance of his career, scoring a hat-trick and providing an assist in Pas De Nom's 4–1 victory.

Biography

Adekyz became most closely associated with Pas De Nom during the club's championship-winning period.

His defining appearance came in the final of HaxBall Champions League #7. Pas De Nom had used Kranjcar throughout the earlier stages of the competition, but Kranjcar was unable to attend on the day of the final.

As a result, Adekyz was brought into the lineup in his place. Despite arriving for the most important match of the campaign under those circumstances, he went on to produce the defining performance of his recorded career.

HaxBall Champions League #7

The Final Performance

Pas De Nom faced Turkish rival Angels of Death in the HaxBall Champions League #7 final.

The expected Pas De Nom lineup changed on the day of the final when Kranjcar was unable to attend. Adekyz therefore replaced him for the championship match.

Pas De Nom lined up with Guti.Haz, Leu, Tino and Adekyz, while Angels of Death fielded Rai, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Dark.

Adekyz immediately became the central figure of the match. In the third minute, he emerged from a battle with Rai and scored to give Pas De Nom a 1–0 lead.

He struck again later in the first half after another physical battle, this time involving ROCKY BALBOA, doubling Pas De Nom's advantage to 2–0.

Angels of Death responded through Dark in the second half, reducing the deficit to 2–1 and bringing the final back into contention.

Pas De Nom responded quickly. Leu recovered the ball and found Tino in midfield, who immediately played it into Adekyz's path. Adekyz converted the opportunity to complete his hat-trick and restore the two-goal advantage at 3–1.

Adekyz then contributed to the final goal as well, assisting Tino on the counter-attack to complete a 4–1 victory and secure Pas De Nom's first HaxBall Champions League championship.

Legacy

Adekyz's place in HaxBall history is closely tied to Pas De Nom.

Having entered the HaxBall Champions League #7 final lineup only after Kranjcar was unable to attend, Adekyz produced a hat-trick and an assist in the biggest match in Pas De Nom's history.

That performance made him one of the central figures in the club's championship victory and established him as a Pas De Nom legend.
