Knap is a Turkish competitive HaxBall player who won HaxBall Champions League #22 with Pafküf.

Although Knap was primarily known as a DM, his HaxBall Champions League #22 campaign came in an entirely different role. Misaj made the unexpected decision to use him as Pafküf's goalkeeper for the tournament.

The positional change was unusual, and Knap did not produce a standout goalkeeping campaign. However, the decision did not become a major weakness for Pafküf either. He made several useful saves during the tournament and was generally reliable enough for the team to complete its championship run.

His previous experience as a DM was particularly visible with the ball. Knap was comfortable in possession and capable of playing clean, accurate passes from goal, giving Pafküf another option when moving the ball out from the back.

Biography

Knap was primarily associated with the DM position, where his ability on the ball and passing were natural parts of his game.

His role changed significantly during HaxBall Champions League #22. Pafküf did not use a conventional goalkeeper, with Misaj instead choosing Knap to play in goal.

The move represented a considerable positional change. Knap was not transformed into an elite goalkeeper by the switch, but he adapted well enough to the role and became part of the lineup that eventually won the competition.

The Unexpected Goalkeeper

Knap's use as goalkeeper was one of the more unusual aspects of Pafküf's HaxBall Champions League #22 campaign. Until that point, he had been associated much more closely with playing as a DM than as a specialist goalkeeper.

Despite that background, Misaj chose Knap as Pafküf's starting goalkeeper. The decision was based on confidence in his adaptability and general ability rather than an established history in the position.

Knap's performances in goal were generally solid rather than exceptional. He made several useful saves during the tournament and avoided becoming a major problem for the team, which allowed Pafküf's strong outfield lineup to function effectively throughout the campaign.

His experience as a DM was more obvious when Pafküf had possession. Knap was already comfortable receiving the ball and could play accurate passes from deep, making him more natural with the ball than many conventional goalkeepers.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Knap's principal European achievement came with Pafküf in HaxBall Champions League #22.

The championship lineup consisted of Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro. While the three outfield players competed in more familiar roles, Knap's position in goal gave Pafküf an unconventional setup.

HaxBall Champions League #22: An Unusual Role

Pafküf faced an early crisis in the knockout stages. In the quarter-finals against Invincible, the team fell 3–0 behind and appeared close to elimination before producing a major comeback to survive the tie.

After overcoming that difficult match, Pafküf later defeated Wicked Guys in the semi-final to secure a place in the championship match.

The final was played against FEAR. Pafküf took control during the opening half and built a 3–0 lead by halftime, placing themselves in a strong position to finish the tournament as champions.

Knap's tournament in goal was not the central reason behind Pafküf's success, but he fulfilled the role adequately. He contributed several useful saves, remained comfortable when receiving possession and helped the team complete its run to the HaxBall Champions League title.

Championship Lineup (#22): Knap • Misaj • Jéal • Necro

Playing Style

Knap was naturally more familiar with playing as a DM than as a goalkeeper. His comfort in possession and passing ability were therefore among the clearest qualities he carried into the goalkeeper role.

Having previously played as an outfield player, Knap was comfortable controlling the ball and finding teammates with clean, accurate passes. This made him useful when Pafküf wanted to move possession out from the back.

His goalkeeping itself was more functional than spectacular. Knap was capable of making good saves and generally handled the position without causing major problems, but his HaxBall Champions League #22 campaign was not based on dominant shot-stopping performances.

The combination of adequate goalkeeping and strong ball use made the experiment workable. His DM background remained an important part of what separated his style in goal from that of a more traditional goalkeeper.

Legacy

Knap's HaxBall Champions League history is closely connected with the unusual goalkeeper role he occupied during Pafküf's championship campaign.

Primarily known as a DM, he was moved into goal by Misaj for HaxBall Champions League #22. The experiment was not an overwhelming individual success, but it worked well enough for Pafküf to compete without the position becoming a significant weakness.

Knap made useful saves when required, while his passing and comfort in possession reflected his background as an outfield player.

The campaign ultimately ended with Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro winning HaxBall Champions League #22, giving Knap a championship title from one of the more unusual roles of his competitive career.
