Knap is a Turkish competitive HaxBall player who won HaxBall Champions League #22 with Pafküf.

Although Knap was originally known as a DM, his career changed direction when he moved into goal. During HaxBall Champions League #22, Misaj chose him as Pafküf's goalkeeper, giving him a major tournament opportunity in the new role.

Knap adapted well to the position. His campaign was not built around spectacular or dominant shot-stopping, but he delivered a good and reliable performance, made several important saves and handled the role effectively throughout Pafküf's championship run. The switch ultimately became more than a temporary experiment, as Knap continued his career as a goalkeeper afterwards.

His previous experience as a DM was particularly visible with the ball. Knap was comfortable in possession and capable of playing clean, accurate passes from goal, giving Pafküf another option when moving the ball out from the back.

Biography

Knap began as a DM, where his ability on the ball and passing were natural parts of his game.

His role changed significantly during HaxBall Champions League #22. Pafküf did not use a conventional goalkeeper, with Misaj instead choosing Knap to play in goal.

Rather than remaining a one-tournament experiment, the move became an important transition in Knap's career. He adapted well to goalkeeping, performed reliably during the title run and later continued his career in the position.

The Unexpected Goalkeeper

Knap's move into goal was one of the more unusual aspects of Pafküf's HaxBall Champions League #22 campaign. Until that point, he had been associated much more closely with playing as a DM than as a goalkeeper.

Despite that background, Misaj chose Knap as Pafküf's starting goalkeeper. Knap adjusted quickly enough for the role to become a genuine part of his career rather than a short-term solution.

His performances during the tournament were good and reliable. While he was not the dominant figure of Pafküf's title run, he made several useful saves, handled his responsibilities well and gave the team stability in goal throughout the campaign.

His experience as a DM was especially valuable in possession. Knap was already comfortable receiving the ball and could play accurate passes from deep, making his distribution a natural strength in goal. After adapting to the position, he continued his career as a goalkeeper.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Knap's principal European achievement came with Pafküf in HaxBall Champions League #22.

The championship lineup consisted of Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro. While the three outfield players competed in more familiar roles, Knap's position in goal gave Pafküf an unconventional setup.

HaxBall Champions League #22: An Unusual Role

Pafküf faced an early crisis in the knockout stages. In the quarter-finals against Invincible, the team fell 3–0 behind and appeared close to elimination before producing a major comeback to survive the tie.

After overcoming that difficult match, Pafküf later defeated Wicked Guys in the semi-final to secure a place in the championship match.

The final was played against FEAR. Pafküf took control during the opening half and built a 3–0 lead by halftime, placing themselves in a strong position to finish the tournament as champions.

Knap was not the central star of Pafküf's title run, but he gave the team a good performance in goal. He contributed several useful saves, remained composed when receiving possession and played his part in completing the run to the HaxBall Champions League title.

Playing Style

Knap's background as a DM strongly influenced the way he played after moving into goal. His comfort in possession and passing ability became useful qualities in his new position.

Having previously played as an outfield player, Knap was comfortable controlling the ball and finding teammates with clean, accurate passes. This made him useful when Pafküf wanted to move possession out from the back.

As a goalkeeper, Knap developed into a reliable option. He was capable of making good saves and adapted well to the positional demands without needing to be a spectacular shot-stopper. His HaxBall Champions League #22 campaign showed that he could perform effectively in the role at a major tournament.

The combination of dependable goalkeeping and strong ball use made the transition successful. Knap continued playing as a goalkeeper afterwards, with his DM background remaining an important part of his style in goal.

Legacy

Knap's HaxBall Champions League history is closely connected with the goalkeeper transition he made during Pafküf's championship campaign.

Originally known as a DM, he was moved into goal by Misaj for HaxBall Champions League #22. Knap adapted well, produced a good and reliable tournament in goal and later continued his career in the position.

His useful saves, composure and passing from deep reflected both his development as a goalkeeper and the technical qualities he carried over from his earlier role as an outfield player.

The campaign ultimately ended with Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro winning HaxBall Champions League #22, giving Knap a championship title from one of the more unusual roles of his competitive career.
