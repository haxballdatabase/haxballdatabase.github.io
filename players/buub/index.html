Buub is a Turkish HaxBall player who plays primarily as an attacking player. He is closely associated with Bullas, his twin brother, with the two spending a large part of their competitive careers together.

Buub and Bullas were the founders of Wicked Guys, a Turkish team that later became one of the notable clubs of the modern competitive scene. Buub was also part of the Wicked Guys lineup that reached the semi-finals of HaxBall Champions League #22.

At international level, his strongest documented result came with Turkey in Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy, where the national team reached the final and finished as runner-up.

Current Status: Buub remained active in competitive HaxBall as of 2026.

Biography

Buub is a Turkish player whose competitive career has been strongly connected with his twin brother Bullas. The brothers frequently appeared together in the same teams and projects rather than following completely separate paths through the scene.

Together, they founded Wicked Guys, and Buub remained associated with the club during its development into an established Turkish competitive team.

Partnership with Bullas

A defining part of Buub's career has been his long-standing partnership with his twin brother Bullas. The pair spent much of their HaxBall careers together and were repeatedly associated with the same teams and competitive groups.

Their most important shared club project became Wicked Guys, which they founded together.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #22

Buub was part of the Wicked Guys side that reached the semi-finals of HaxBall Champions League #22.

Wicked Guys faced eventual champions Pafküf with a lineup of Wiagoal, Bullas, Vichoexe and Buub.

Pafküf won the semi-final and advanced to the Grand Final, ending Wicked Guys' campaign one stage before the championship match.

Wicked Guys: Wiagoal • Bullas • Vichoexe • Buub
Opponent: Pafküf
Stage: Semi-final

🎥 Semi-Final Replay: Pafküf vs. Wicked Guys

Wicked Guys later won HaxBall Champions League #24, but Buub was not part of the championship lineup. The title-winning four-player lineup consisted of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

International Career

Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy

Buub reached a major international final with Turkey in the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup.

Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Buub and Kamil.

Their opponents were the Netherlands, represented by Lyreco, Hulk, veil and xSha.

The Netherlands won the tournament, leaving Buub and Turkey as Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy runners-up.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Buub • Kamil
Netherlands: Lyreco • Hulk • veil • xSha
Result: 🥈 Runner-up
