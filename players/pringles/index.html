Pringles is widely regarded as one of the greatest goalkeepers in HaxBall history, establishing a reputation at the highest level of both domestic and European competition.

One of the most notable aspects of Pringles' career was his ability to remain an elite goalkeeper across both the historic Flash era and the later HTML5 era. His career stretched across two very different periods of competitive HaxBall, making his longevity an important part of his reputation.

At club level, Pringles became a HaxBall Champions League champion with Omertà in Edition #14 and later reached consecutive finals with the Seeders core in Editions #17 and #18. Across those campaigns, he finished his career with three HaxBall Champions League final appearances.

Pringles was also an important member of the celebrated QuickSwans and Seeders generation. The core first won the Feed-Me League under the QuickSwans banner before continuing together as Seeders and winning Seasons 24 and 25, completing three consecutive league championships.

His domestic record as a goalkeeper was equally notable. Pringles recorded 53 clean sheets in Feed-Me Division 1, sharing the all-time Division 1 record with kojo.

At international level, Pringles became a Nations Cup champion with Portugal in 2018. He later reached two more major finals with Portugal in 2019, finishing runner-up in both Nations Cup 2019 and Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

Biography

Pringles built a long career as a goalkeeper at the highest levels of European HaxBall. His competitive history included major domestic success, several appearances on the HaxBall Champions League stage and international competition with Portugal.

Unlike players whose careers were concentrated entirely within one version of the game, Pringles remained relevant through the transition from Flash to HTML5. That longevity became particularly notable for a goalkeeper, a position where consistency and defensive results could be measured across many seasons.

Pringles became associated with several major teams during his career, most notably Omertà and the QuickSwans / Seeders core. These periods brought both his major European championship and some of the strongest domestic results of his career.

His international career with Portugal also produced major success. Pringles won the 2018 Nations Cup before returning to two more international finals during 2019.

Feed-Me Career

Pringles was part of one of the strongest generations in Feed-Me League history. The core originally competed under the QuickSwans banner and won the league championship before transitioning together into Seeders.

The Seeders core included Isco, Yannex, Marble, Pringles, aguero10, shaxlele and Echo. With Isco as captain, the team continued its domestic dominance by winning Feed-Me League Seasons 24 and 25.

Combined with the previous championship under QuickSwans, the group completed three consecutive Feed-Me League titles, a period remembered as the Seeders three-season run of dominance.

Behind that highly successful team, Pringles established one of the strongest statistical records ever recorded by a goalkeeper in the competition. His 53 Division 1 clean sheets place him level with kojo for the all-time Feed-Me Division 1 record.

The record is particularly important to Pringles' career because it provides a measurable reflection of his consistency in goal across a long period of top-level domestic competition.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Pringles reached three HaxBall Champions League finals during his career. He won his first with Omertà in Edition #14 before later finishing runner-up in consecutive finals with the Seeders core in Editions #17 and #18.

HaxBall Champions League #14: European Champion

Pringles won the HaxBall Champions League in Edition #14 with Omertà, playing alongside Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

Omertà faced Klub Emeryta in the final, with the match developing into one of the closest championship games of the era.

Klub Emeryta scored first through DrG before Mamba struck twice to turn the match around and give Omertà a 2–1 lead. A misplaced ball from Pringles was later intercepted, allowing Liebe to equalize and force the match beyond regulation.

The championship was ultimately decided in Golden Goal, where Deliric won a physical battle with DrG and scored the decisive goal to complete a 3–2 victory.

The result gave Pringles his first and only HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Lineup (#14): Pringles • Deliric • aguero10 • Mamba

HaxBall Champions League #17: Back in the Final

Pringles later returned to the HaxBall Champions League final as part of the dominant Seeders core, which competed in Edition #17 under the name BLACKOUT.

The squad included Isco, Yannex, Marble, Pringles, aguero10, shaxlele and Echo, bringing much of the same group that had dominated the Feed-Me League into European competition.

BLACKOUT faced Klub Emeryta in the final and initially took control of the match. Isco created the opening goal for Yannex before Klub Emeryta responded through xSha and SAKY to turn the score around.

With less than a minute remaining, aguero10 supplied Isco for a 2–2 equalizer, sending the championship into Golden Goal.

The match ended when xSha scored the decisive goal on a rapid transition, giving Klub Emeryta a 3–2 victory and leaving Pringles with the first runner-up finish of his HaxBall Champions League career.

HaxBall Champions League #18: Another Golden Goal

The following edition, Pringles and the same core returned under the official Seeders name and once again reached the HaxBall Champions League final.

Their route included a major quarter-final victory over defending powerhouse Stiff Wind. After falling behind early, Seeders recovered to win 3–1 and continued their run toward a second consecutive final.

In the final, Seeders faced The Elders. Regulation ended 0–0, before Yannex gave Seeders the lead in extra time following a pass from Marble.

The Elders equalized through Vak, but Seeders moved ahead again after pressure from Marble resulted in a deflected goal. With less than two minutes remaining, SAKY made it 2–2 and forced another Golden Goal.

Only 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY scored again to give The Elders a 3–2 victory. For Pringles and the Seeders core, it meant consecutive HaxBall Champions League final defeats, both decided by Golden Goal.

International Career

Pringles represented Portugal in international competition and reached three documented major finals between 2018 and 2019.

His first of those appearances ended with a championship in the 2018 Nations Cup, before Portugal finished runner-up in two further tournaments during 2019.

Nations Cup 2018: International Champion

Pringles won the 2018 Nations Cup with Portugal in a closely contested final against Turkey.

Portugal lined up with Pringles, Ter, SAKY and Bot, while Turkey fielded Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Turkey took the lead near the end of the first half when Müllewa found the goal, sending his side into a 1–0 advantage.

Portugal responded during the second half. Following a misunderstanding in the Turkish defense, the ball was left in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity to make it 1–1.

The final then produced an unusual fair-play moment. Turkey conceded another goal during a situation affected by ping. Rather than keeping the advantage, Portugal deliberately opened its goal and allowed Turkey to score, restoring the competitive balance and allowing the match to continue from 2–2.

Later in the match, SAKY found Bot with a pass and Bot scored again. The goal proved decisive, giving Portugal a 3–2 victory and making Pringles a Nations Cup champion.

Portugal: Pringles • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2019: Portugal's Final Run

Pringles represented Portugal during Nations Cup 2019, reaching the grand final alongside players including SAKY.

Portugal faced Turkey in the final and produced an excellent opening period, taking a 2–0 lead and putting themselves in a strong position to win the championship.

Turkey eventually responded through Misaj and Necro, scoring twice to force the match into extra time at 2–2.

The momentum changed completely in extra time, with Turkey completing the comeback and eventually winning 5–2. Pringles therefore finished the tournament as a Nations Cup 2019 runner-up with Portugal.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy: Israel vs Portugal

During the same year, Pringles represented Portugal in another major international competition, Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy. Portugal once again reached the final, this time facing Israel.

The two teams entered the final with the following lineups:

Israel: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Portugal: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY

The final developed into a difficult and closely contested match. Neither side was able to decide the championship during regulation time, sending the match into extra time.

The decisive moment came when Ter attempted to play the ball backwards. The touch instead left the ball directly in front of LaggerMet, who immediately took advantage of the opportunity.

LaggerMet scored the decisive goal in extra time, securing the championship for Israel and leaving Pringles and Portugal with their second international runner-up finish of 2019.

Portugal Final Lineup: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY
Result: 🥈 Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy Runner-up

Playing Style

Pringles was primarily known as a goalkeeper. His reputation at the position was built over a long career that crossed both the Flash and HTML5 eras rather than being limited to a single period of competitive HaxBall.

His domestic results provide one of the clearest measures of his performance. Recording 53 clean sheets in Feed-Me Division 1 and sharing the competition's all-time Division 1 record with kojo demonstrated the consistency Pringles maintained across high-level league competition.

Pringles was also capable of operating within the possession-heavy structure of Seeders, a team known for constant ball circulation and controlling matches through collective passing. His role placed him behind one of the most successful and recognizable systems of the era.

Legacy

Pringles' place in HaxBall history is closely connected to his longevity and his standing among the game's elite goalkeepers. Few players at the position maintained a high-level career across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

His HaxBall Champions League #14 championship with Omertà gave him success on the European stage, while the consecutive finals with BLACKOUT and Seeders added further depth to his HaxBall Champions League record.

Domestically, Pringles was part of the QuickSwans and Seeders generation that won three consecutive Feed-Me League championships, placing him inside one of the strongest team periods in the competition's history.

His 53 Feed-Me Division 1 clean sheets, tied with kojo for the all-time record, remain one of the strongest statistical marks attached to his career.

Internationally, Pringles won the 2018 Nations Cup with Portugal before returning to the final stage twice more in 2019, finishing runner-up against Turkey in Nations Cup 2019 and against Israel in Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

With success across domestic leagues, three HaxBall Champions League finals, a European championship and a Nations Cup championship with Portugal, Pringles is remembered as one of the greatest goalkeepers to play competitive HaxBall.
