Bot is a HaxBall player from Portugal best known for his appearances in consecutive Nations Cup finals in 2018 and 2019.

He became an international champion in 2018, scoring two goals in Portugal's 3–2 final victory over Turkey, including the championship-winning goal.

The following year, Bot returned to the Nations Cup final with Portugal. Despite Portugal taking a 2–0 lead, Turkey produced a major comeback and eventually won the match 5–2 after extra time.

Biography

Bot represented Portugal during one of the national team's strongest international periods.

His documented major tournament record includes two consecutive Nations Cup final appearances, winning the competition in 2018 before finishing runner-up in 2019.

His most notable individual performance came in the 2018 final, where he scored twice against Turkey and delivered the championship-winning goal.

International Career

Bot reached consecutive major international finals with Portugal in 2018 and 2019, recording one championship and one runner-up finish.

Nations Cup 2018: Two Goals in the Final

Portugal reached the 2018 Nations Cup final against Turkey with a lineup of Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot.

Turkey fielded Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Turkey took the lead near the end of the first half when Müllewa scored to make it 1–0.

Portugal responded during the second half. Following a misunderstanding in the Turkish defense, the ball was left in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity to equalize at 1–1.

The match later produced a notable fair-play moment. Turkey conceded during a situation affected by ping. Portugal chose not to keep the resulting advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to score and restoring the match to 2–2.

The championship was eventually decided when SAKY found Bot with a pass. Bot scored his second goal of the final to make it 3–2, securing the Nations Cup title for Portugal.

Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Bot: 2 Goals
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2019: The 2–0 Lead Slips Away

One year after becoming Nations Cup champion, Bot returned to the final with Portugal.

Portugal lined up with Pringles, SAKY, Ole and Bot, while Turkey fielded Liéds, Misaj, Marble and Necro.

Portugal made the stronger start. A goal late in the first half gave the team a 1–0 lead, before another goal early in the second half extended the advantage to 2–0.

Turkey responded through Misaj, who scored to reduce the deficit to 2–1.

Portugal remained ahead until the closing stages. With approximately one minute remaining, Misaj played the ball into a crowded goal area and Necro converted the opportunity to equalize at 2–2, sending the final into extra time.

Turkey then completed the turnaround. Marble scored to give Turkey a 3–2 lead, completely changing the direction of the final.

Turkey continued to extend its advantage during extra time and eventually completed a 5–2 victory. Bot therefore finished his second consecutive Nations Cup final as runner-up after having won the competition the previous year.

Portugal: Pringles • SAKY • Ole • Bot
Turkey: Liéds • Misaj • Marble • Necro
Final: Turkey 5–2 Portugal
Portugal Lead: 2–0
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Legacy

Bot's documented competitive legacy is primarily connected to his international career with Portugal.

His defining achievement came in the 2018 Nations Cup final, where he scored twice against Turkey, including the decisive goal in Portugal's 3–2 championship victory.

He returned to the final the following year as Portugal attempted to win consecutive Nations Cup titles. Portugal established a 2–0 lead, but Turkey recovered to force extra time and eventually won 5–2.

With a championship and a runner-up finish from two consecutive Nations Cup finals, Bot was part of a successful period for the Portuguese national team.
