International Career

Nations Cup 2022: International Champion

Al1s was part of the Romania lineup that reached the Nations Cup 2022 final against Turkey.

Romania fielded Al1s, aMp, Modric and Hakut. Turkey's lineup consisted of Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Romania won the tournament, giving Al1s a documented Nations Cup championship.
