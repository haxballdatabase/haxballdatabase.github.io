P4ER is a HaxBall player from Israel widely regarded as one of the best players of his generation. His career extended across both the Flash and HTML5 eras, with P4ER remaining a high-level competitive player through two different periods of the game.

He became particularly well known within the Israel HaxBall community, where he established himself as one of the country's most recognizable and prominent competitive players of his generation.

His career included major success at both club and international level, most notably winning HaxBall Champions League #18 and becoming a multiple-time Nations Cup champion with Israel.

Biography

P4ER established himself as one of the leading players from Israel of his generation and competed at a high level across both club and international HaxBall.

He became a particularly familiar name within the Israel HaxBall community and was part of a generation that achieved significant success in international competition.

P4ER also competed in Feed Me, where he played for Guns N’ Moses. He registered on 13 April 2013 and recorded a total of 51 goals and 67 assists.

At club level, his most important achievement came with The Elders in HaxBall Champions League #18, while his international career included multiple major championships with Israel.

Flash and HTML5

P4ER's career spanned both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

He remained among the notable high-level players of his generation across the transition between the two versions of the game, maintaining a competitive presence despite the changes in gameplay that accompanied the move from Flash to HTML5.

Playing Style

P4ER was known for his exceptional versatility. He could perform across virtually every attacking position and was capable of adapting to other roles when required, including goalkeeper.

What made this flexibility particularly valuable was that he was not simply capable of filling different positions. P4ER could maintain a high competitive level in almost every role he was asked to play, allowing teams to use him in very different tactical structures.

He was also regarded as a strongly competitive player, with his willingness to take responsibility and adapt to the needs of his team becoming an important characteristic of his game.

HaxBall Champions League #18

P4ER became a HaxBall Champions League champion with The Elders in Edition #18.

The Elders lined up with LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak in the final against Seeders.

Regular time ended 0–0, sending the championship into extra time. Seeders struck first when Marble created the opportunity for Yannex, before Vak responded for The Elders to make the score 1–1.

Seeders regained the lead at 2–1, but with less than two minutes remaining SAKY scored the equalizer and forced the final into Golden Goal.

Only 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY scored again, completing a dramatic 3–2 comeback victory and giving P4ER and The Elders the HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Lineup (#18): LaggerMet • P4ER • SAKY • Vak
Final: The Elders 3–2 Seeders
Result: 🏆 Winner

International Career

P4ER represented Israel in international competition and became part of multiple championship-winning national-team lineups.

His international success became an important part of his career and further strengthened his standing within the Israel HaxBall community.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy

P4ER was part of the Israel team that won the Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

Israel lined up with Yannex, Isco, P4ER and LaggerMet in the final against Portugal, whose lineup consisted of Pringles, Ter, Taziek and SAKY.

The match remained undecided through regulation time and continued into extra time. The decisive moment came when a backward touch from Ter left the ball in front of LaggerMet, who converted the opportunity and secured the championship for Israel.

Israel: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Portugal: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy

P4ER added another major international championship with Israel in Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Israel faced Romania in the final. The Israel lineup consisted of Yannex, P4ER, Unique and Meyer, while Romania lined up with El Drago, aMp, GirlontaR and Hakut.

Israel won the final and secured the championship, giving P4ER another Nations Cup title with the national team.

Israel: Yannex • P4ER • Unique • Meyer
Romania: El Drago • aMp • GirlontaR • Hakut
Result: 🏆 Winner

Legacy

P4ER is remembered as one of the leading players from Israel of his generation and one of the notable competitors whose career successfully extended across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

His standing was particularly significant within the Israel HaxBall community, where his long competitive presence and involvement in successful national-team generations made him one of the community's most recognizable players.

A HaxBall Champions League championship with The Elders and multiple international championships with Israel gave P4ER major success at both club and national-team level, securing his place as an important figure in Israel's competitive HaxBall history.
