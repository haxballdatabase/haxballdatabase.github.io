Kyrai is a HaxBall player from Turkey who primarily played as a striker. Known as a productive goalscorer with reliable finishing, he competed at a high level in both club and international competition.

His major recorded achievements include reaching the HaxBall Champions League #19 final with Stiff Wind, winning the 2021 Nations Cup with Turkey and returning to the international final in Nations Cup 2022.

Kyrai also built a strong individual scoring record in the Feed Me Division 1. With 271 goals, he ranks second on the all-time Division 1 scoring list behind Marble's 284, while also finishing as the league's top scorer in Seasons 28, 34 and 35.

Biography

Kyrai became involved in high-level competitive HaxBall through appearances with teams including Stiff Wind and Smokin' Aces.

Primarily playing as a striker, he developed a reputation as a productive attacking player whose strongest quality was his ability to convert opportunities consistently.

His most important recorded club campaign came with Stiff Wind in HaxBall Champions League #19, where the team reached the championship match and came close to becoming the first club to win four HaxBall Champions League titles.

Kyrai also achieved major success internationally, representing Turkey during its championship run in the 2021 Nations Cup.

One year later, he returned to the Nations Cup final with Turkey in 2022, this time finishing runner-up against Romania.

Outside the Champions League, his long-term scoring record in Feed Me Division 1 further demonstrated his effectiveness as a striker. Kyrai accumulated 271 goals, the second-highest total in the competition's recorded history.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Kyrai's principal recorded HaxBall Champions League achievement was a runner-up finish in Edition #19 with Stiff Wind.

The campaign placed him in a lineup alongside several experienced players and brought Stiff Wind within one match of establishing a new championship record.

HaxBall Champions League #19: One Goal from History

Kyrai reached the HaxBall Champions League final in Edition #19 as part of Stiff Wind.

The Stiff Wind final lineup consisted of PiperSalvia, Gilbert, ROCKY BALBOA and Kyrai. The club entered the final attempting to win a record fourth HaxBall Champions League championship.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, represented by nova, Misaj, ES Pierre and Necro.

Stiff Wind took the lead and moved into a 1–0 advantage, placing the team in a strong position to secure another championship.

Angry Bulls responded when Misaj created the equalizer for Necro. The match remained level and eventually continued into extra time.

Misaj then scored the winning goal to complete a 2–1 comeback. The result left Kyrai and Stiff Wind as runners-up and prevented the club from becoming the first four-time HaxBall Champions League champion.

Final: Stiff Wind 1–2 Angry Bulls

Smokin' Aces

Kyrai also represented Smokin' Aces during another period of his club career.

The club's historical records include Kyrai among its notable players alongside names such as wroz, jasko, vegeta, PiperSalvia, xSha and JC7.

Kyrai was not part of the documented championship lineup that won HaxBall Champions League #20, so that title is not recorded as one of his personal achievements.

International Career

Kyrai represented Turkey at international level and reached consecutive Nations Cup finals in 2021 and 2022.

He became an international champion in 2021 before returning to the final the following year, where Romania prevented Turkey from winning consecutive Nations Cup titles.

Nations Cup 2021: International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2021

Kyrai was part of the Turkish national team that won the 2021 Nations Cup.

Turkey lined up with Vichoexe, Bullas, Kyrai and Altidore. Their opponents Poland featured kojo, miso, Kise and frenkie.

Turkey won the tournament, giving Kyrai a major international championship and adding national-team success to his HaxBall Champions League final appearance.

Turkey: Vichoexe • Bullas • Kyrai • Altidore
Opponent: Poland
Poland: kojo • miso • Kise • frenkie
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2022: Consecutive Finals

One year after becoming Nations Cup champion, Kyrai returned to the final with Turkey in 2022.

Turkey entered the final with Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Their opponents were Romania, whose lineup consisted of Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut.

Romania won the tournament, leaving Kyrai and Turkey as Nations Cup 2022 runners-up. The result ended Turkey's attempt to win consecutive Nations Cup championships.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Vichoexe • Kyrai
Romania: Modric • aMp • Al1s • Hakut
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Feed Me Division 1

Kyrai developed one of the strongest long-term scoring records in Feed Me Division 1.

He scored 271 goals in the competition, placing him second on the all-time Division 1 goalscoring list. Only Marble, with 284 goals, recorded a higher total.

Kyrai also finished as the Division 1 top scorer in Seasons 28, 34 and 35, giving him three separate goalscoring titles.

Feed Me Division 1 Goals: 271
All-Time Ranking: 2nd
Top Scorer: Season 28 • Season 34 • Season 35

Playing Style

Kyrai played primarily as a striker and was most notable for his goalscoring.

His finishing was a reliable part of his game. Rather than being defined by spectacular individual actions, Kyrai was effective at converting opportunities and consistently producing goals over long periods of league competition.

His Feed Me Division 1 record provides the clearest evidence of that quality. Scoring 271 goals and finishing as the league's top scorer in three different seasons reflected a striker capable of maintaining strong attacking production across multiple campaigns.

While not necessarily regarded among the absolute greatest attacking players in HaxBall history, Kyrai was a reliable and productive striker with good finishing whose scoring record placed him among the more successful goalscorers of his period.

Legacy

Kyrai's career combined international success, major final appearances and a strong long-term scoring record.

His appearance in the HaxBall Champions League #19 final with Stiff Wind placed him within a team competing directly for an all-time championship record.

At international level, Kyrai won the 2021 Nations Cup with Turkey and returned to the championship match in 2022, finishing runner-up against Romania.

Those results gave Kyrai three documented major final appearances across club and international competition, with one championship and two runner-up finishes.

His most notable individual record came in Feed Me Division 1. With 271 goals, Kyrai ranks behind only Marble on the all-time scoring list, while his three top-scorer seasons further demonstrate the consistency of his goalscoring.

Together, those achievements established Kyrai as a productive and reliable striker whose career included both important team results and one of the stronger scoring records in Division 1 history.
