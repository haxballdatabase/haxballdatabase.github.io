Hulk is a HaxBall player from the Netherlands who primarily plays as a DM. Having competed across both the Flash and HTML5 eras, he has maintained a long presence in competitive HaxBall and remains active as of 2026.

Throughout his long competitive career, Hulk has remained a respected player within the HaxBall scene. His longevity, continued involvement at a high level and appearances in major international competition have made him one of the notable Dutch players to compete across multiple eras of the game.

His strongest documented achievements have come with the Netherlands national team. Hulk reached the final of Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, finishing runner-up to Turkey, before returning four years later and winning Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy.

The four-year gap between those finals, combined with his continued activity into 2026, makes Hulk one of the players whose competitive career stretches across distinctly different periods of HaxBall.

Biography

Hulk established himself as a Dutch DM during the Flash era and continued competing following HaxBall's transition to HTML5.

His career has been characterized by considerable longevity. Rather than disappearing from the competitive scene following the end of Flash, Hulk remained involved through the HTML5 period and continues to play in 2026.

Over this period, Hulk maintained a respected position within the competitive community and became one of the recognizable Dutch players to remain involved across different generations of the game.

At international level, Hulk represented the Netherlands in major competition across different periods, reaching Nations Cup Big Easy finals in both 2017 and 2021.

International Career

Hulk's strongest documented tournament results have come with the Netherlands national team.

He reached two major Nations Cup - Big Easy finals four years apart, first finishing runner-up during the Flash era before later becoming an international champion during HTML5.

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy: Final Against Turkey

Hulk reached a major international final with the Netherlands at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy.

The Netherlands lined up with Dereh, Hulk, Chro and Styl.

Their opponents were Turkey, whose core lineup consisted of Misaj, Liebe, Marble and Andrea.

The Netherlands made the stronger start and took an early 1–0 lead.

Turkey eventually responded, with Misaj creating the equalizer that brought the match back level and changed the momentum of the final.

Turkey completed the comeback and won the tournament, leaving Hulk and the Netherlands with a runner-up finish.

Netherlands: Dereh • Hulk • Chro • Styl
Turkey: Misaj • Liebe • Marble • Andrea
Netherlands Lead: 1–0
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy: International Champion

Four years after his 2017 Big Easy final appearance, Hulk returned to the championship stage with the Netherlands at Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy.

The Netherlands lined up with Lyreco, Hulk, veil and xSha.

Their opponents were Turkey, represented by Bullas, Gilbert, Buub and Kamil.

The Netherlands won the tournament, giving Hulk a major international championship and reversing the disappointment of his earlier Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy final appearance.

Netherlands: Lyreco • Hulk • veil • xSha
Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Buub • Kamil
Result: 🏆 Winner

From Flash to HTML5

Hulk's career has extended across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

He was already competing at international level during Flash, reaching the Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy final with the Netherlands.

Unlike many players whose careers were closely tied to one version of the game, Hulk continued playing after HaxBall's transition to HTML5. Four years after his first documented major final, he returned to the Big Easy championship stage and became an international champion.

His career continued well beyond that victory, with Hulk remaining an active competitive player in 2026.

Playing Style

Hulk plays primarily as a DM.

His longevity in the position is one of the notable aspects of his career. Hulk has remained competitive as a DM across both Flash and HTML5 and continued playing years after many players from his earlier generation had left the scene.

Although detailed records of his individual playing style are limited, his sustained presence in competitive teams and continued respect within the community have helped establish his reputation as a strong and reliable player in the position.

HaxBall Champions League Record

Despite his long competitive career, Hulk has never won the HaxBall Champions League.

However, the absence of an HCL championship has not prevented him from being regarded as a good and respected DM. Across the periods in which he competed, Hulk remained among the better players in his position and continued to earn respect within the competitive scene.

His career therefore represents an example of a player whose quality cannot be judged purely through HaxBall Champions League trophies. His longevity, international success and sustained level as a DM remain important parts of his reputation.
