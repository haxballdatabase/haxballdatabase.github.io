R9 is a Turkish HaxBall DM regarded as one of the best DMs of his generation and an important figure in the early development of competitive Turkish HaxBall. His entire career belonged to the Flash era, where he became closely associated with Angels of Death and later Stiff Wind.

During the early years of the Turkish scene, R9 established himself as one of the leading players in his position and became an important figure during a formative period of competitive HaxBall in Turkey.

R9 had already achieved success in Turkish domestic competition, but the HaxBall Champions League remained the major title missing from his career. He reached the final twice with Angels of Death, first against Silas Beavers in #3 and later against Pas De Nom in #7, losing both.

Those defeats became an important part of his career. Together with long-time teammate ROCKY BALBOA, R9 eventually left the Angels of Death identity behind and continued under the new name Stiff Wind.

The change finally produced the result he had been chasing. R9 won HaxBall Champions League #9 with Stiff Wind, defeating Antartic Lions 2–1 in the final. Having finally won the one major title that had remained missing from his career, he considered his mission in competitive HaxBall complete and retired shortly afterwards.

At international level, R9 also represented Turkey and reached the ESL CC 2014 final.

Biography

R9 emerged during the early years of competitive Turkish HaxBall and became one of the strongest DMs of his generation.

His career was strongly connected with the development of two important Turkish teams. He first became a major figure for Angels of Death, one of the most recognizable clubs of the early scene, before later becoming part of the first Stiff Wind championship team.

R9 achieved domestic success in Turkey during his career and established himself near the top of the country's early competitive generation. His reputation as a DM was already firmly established before his major European breakthrough.

The major achievement that continued to escape him, however, was the HaxBall Champions League.

That missing championship eventually shaped the final stages of his career. After two unsuccessful finals with Angels of Death, R9 and ROCKY BALBOA began again under the Stiff Wind name and made another attempt at the title.

Early Turkish Career

R9 was part of a generation that helped establish the early competitive identity of Turkish HaxBall.

Playing primarily as a DM, he became one of the most highly regarded players in the position during the early Flash era. For a period, he was viewed by many Turkish players as the leading DM in the scene.

R9's domestic success further strengthened that reputation. He had already won championships within Turkish competition, meaning that by the time his HaxBall Champions League campaigns became central to his story, he was already an established and successful player.

The contrast between his domestic record and his HaxBall Champions League results became an important part of his career. R9 had already achieved success in Turkey, but repeated defeats on the European stage left one major objective unfinished.

Angels of Death

R9 became closely identified with Angels of Death, one of the strongest and most recognizable Turkish clubs of the early Flash era.

Alongside players from different periods of the AoD roster, R9 became one of the central names associated with the team. His relationship with ROCKY BALBOA would become particularly important, with the two later carrying their partnership into Stiff Wind.

AoD was successful in Turkish competition but developed an unfortunate record on the major European stage. R9 was directly involved in both of the club's HaxBall Champions League final appearances.

The first came against Silas Beavers in Edition #3. Four editions later, R9 returned to the final with AoD against another Turkish rival, Pas De Nom.

Both matches ended in defeat, leaving R9 with two HaxBall Champions League finals and no championship.

HaxBall Champions League Career

R9 reached the HaxBall Champions League final three times during his career.

His first two appearances came with Angels of Death in #3 and #7. Both ended as runner-up finishes.

His third and final appearance came with Stiff Wind in #9. This time, R9 finally completed the achievement that had remained missing from his career.

HaxBall Champions League #3: A Long Night Between Giants

R9's first HaxBall Champions League final came with Angels of Death in Edition #3.

The opponent was Silas Beavers, one of AoD's greatest rivals. The relationship between the two clubs had already developed into an intense rivalry, making the championship meeting one of the most important confrontations of the early Turkish scene.

The period also placed R9 in direct competition with Dexter, another Turkish DM who had emerged as one of the strongest players in the same position. R9 had previously been regarded by many Turkish players as the leading DM of the scene, and Dexter's rise created a notable rivalry between two of the prominent Turkish DMs of the era.

Angels of Death lined up with Chilavert, R9, Raki and Micran.

The match became extraordinary because of its length. The final lasted approximately 60 minutes, with both sides continuing to compete at a high level through an unusually long battle.

For R9 and AoD, the match represented more than an opportunity to win a trophy. It was also a direct confrontation with one of the clubs they most wanted to defeat, at a time when the hierarchy of Turkish competitive HaxBall was still developing.

Silas Beavers eventually found a way to win the marathon final, leaving R9 one step short of his first HaxBall Champions League championship.

Final Lineup (#3): Chilavert • R9 • Raki • Micran

HaxBall Champions League #7

R9 returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Angels of Death in Edition #7.

This time, AoD faced Pas De Nom, another Turkish club with which it had developed a hostile rivalry.

Angels of Death fielded Rai, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Dark.

Pas De Nom took control of the match and moved into a 2–0 lead. AoD responded during the second half through its passing game, with R9 and ROCKY BALBOA combining in the build-up before Dark finished the move to reduce the score to 2–1.

The comeback did not continue. Pas De Nom restored its advantage and eventually won the final 4–1.

For R9, the defeat meant a second HaxBall Champions League final loss. After already falling short against Silas Beavers, he had now lost another championship match against another major Turkish rival.

Final Lineup (#7): Rai • R9 • ROCKY BALBOA • Dark

Stiff Wind

After the repeated European disappointments with Angels of Death, R9 and ROCKY BALBOA decided to begin again.

The AoD identity had become closely associated with major-final disappointment. Despite the club's strength and domestic success, two HaxBall Champions League finals had ended in defeat.

R9 and ROCKY BALBOA therefore continued their project under a new name: Stiff Wind.

For the new championship attempt, they were joined by goalkeeper tonnnek and striker Catrice, creating the lineup that would finally break through in HaxBall Champions League #9.

HaxBall Champions League #9: The Breakthrough

R9's third HaxBall Champions League final came with Stiff Wind in Edition #9.

The championship lineup consisted of tonnnek in goal, R9 at DM, ROCKY BALBOA at AM and Catrice as striker.

Stiff Wind faced Antartic Lions, the team with which tonnnek had won the previous edition.

Catrice opened the scoring before Antartic Lions responded when Yaadon created an opportunity for Meyer. Stiff Wind later regained the lead and moved ahead 2–1.

The final minutes became especially important for R9. With Stiff Wind protecting the lead, he stopped pushing forward and remained deep, focusing entirely on protecting the defensive structure and preventing Antartic Lions from finding another equalizer.

The approach worked. Stiff Wind held the score and won the match 2–1.

After two lost finals with Angels of Death, R9 had finally become a HaxBall Champions League champion.

Championship Lineup (#9): tonnnek • R9 • ROCKY BALBOA • Catrice

Playing Style

R9 played primarily as a DM and built his reputation at the position during the early Flash era.

He was regarded as one of the best DMs of his generation, establishing himself as one of the leading players in the position during the early years of competitive Turkish HaxBall.

His role placed him behind the attacking players, where he was responsible for helping maintain the structure of his team and deciding when to support play further forward and when to remain deeper.

One of the clearest examples of this came in the HaxBall Champions League #9 final. With Stiff Wind protecting a 2–1 lead during the closing minutes, R9 abandoned any attempt to push into attack and remained deep to protect the result.

That decision became a memorable part of the championship match and reflected the disciplined side of his game as a DM.

International Career

R9 also represented Turkey at international level during the Flash era.

ESL CC 2014 Final

R9 was part of the Turkish team that reached the ESL CC 2014 final against the Czech Republic.

Turkey lined up with TinerMania, R9, Overkill and Kranjcar, while the Czech Republic fielded KEANU, Roman, Forsbi and CvRk.

The Czech Republic won the final 3–0, leaving R9 and Turkey as ESL CC 2014 runners-up.

Turkey: TinerMania • R9 • Overkill • Kranjcar
Czech Republic: KEANU • Roman • Forsbi • CvRk
Final: Turkey 0–3 Czech Republic

Retirement

The HaxBall Champions League #9 championship became the final major chapter of R9's competitive career.

By that point, he had already experienced championship success within Turkish competition. The HaxBall Champions League had remained the one major objective he had not completed.

After losing two finals with Angels of Death, R9 finally achieved that goal with Stiff Wind.

With the missing championship finally secured, R9 considered his mission in competitive HaxBall complete. He retired following the victory and did not return to competitive play.

Legacy

R9's legacy is closely tied to the early development of competitive Turkish HaxBall.

As a Turkish DM playing during the Flash era, he established himself as one of the best DMs of his generation and became one of the leading players in the position during the early years of the Turkish scene.

His club career became closely connected with two major names. R9 was one of the important figures of Angels of Death before helping carry the project into its next identity as Stiff Wind.

His international career also included the ESL CC 2014 final, where he represented Turkey alongside TinerMania, Overkill and Kranjcar.

The repeated HaxBall Champions League defeats with AoD became an important part of his story. R9 stood on the losing side of the marathon #3 final against Silas Beavers and later suffered another major disappointment against Pas De Nom in #7.

His career eventually received a fitting conclusion. With Stiff Wind, R9 returned to the final in #9, played at DM in the championship lineup and helped protect the decisive 2–1 lead during the closing stages.

The victory finally completed the major objective that had remained unfinished throughout his career. Rather than extending his playing years after reaching the summit, R9 chose to leave competitive HaxBall immediately after accomplishing what he had set out to achieve.

For that reason, R9's career is remembered through more than a single championship. His domestic success, international final appearance, his standing as one of the leading DMs of early Turkish HaxBall, his years with Angels of Death and his role in the beginning of Stiff Wind all formed part of his reputation.

After two failed attempts, he finally won the HaxBall Champions League, considered his mission complete and walked away from competitive HaxBall as a champion.
