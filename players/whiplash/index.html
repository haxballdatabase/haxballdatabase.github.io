Whiplash is a Turkish HaxBall player best documented for his international career with Turkey. He reached two major Nations Cup finals with the national team, first at Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy and later at the 2018 Nations Cup.

The two final appearances came five years apart, giving Whiplash a documented presence across two different periods of Turkish international HaxBall. Turkey finished as runner-up on both occasions.

Biography

Whiplash represented Turkey in major international competition and is currently documented in the archive through two Nations Cup final appearances.

His first recorded major final came in 2013, when Turkey reached the Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy championship match. He later returned to a Nations Cup final in 2018 as part of another Turkish lineup.

International Career

Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy

Whiplash reached the final of Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy with Turkey.

Turkey lined up with Lev Yashin, barya, Whiplash and Doctore. Their opponents Portugal fielded Markogol, Wigas, Tortogol and Dark.

Portugal won the final, leaving Whiplash and Turkey as runners-up. The current archive does not preserve a confirmed final score or detailed individual match events.

Turkey: Lev Yashin • barya • Whiplash • Doctore
Portugal: Markogol • Wigas • Tortogol • Dark
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2018

Whiplash returned to a major international final with Turkey at the 2018 Nations Cup.

Turkey entered the final with Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa. Portugal's lineup featured Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot.

Turkey took a 1–0 lead near the end of the first half when Müllewa scored. Portugal equalized during the second half through Bot.

The final also produced a notable fair-play sequence. Turkey conceded during a situation affected by ping, but Portugal chose not to retain the resulting advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to restore the score to 2–2.

The championship was eventually decided when SAKY created the final opportunity for Bot, who scored to give Portugal a 3–2 victory. Whiplash and Turkey therefore finished the tournament as runners-up.

Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Result: 🥈 Runner-up
