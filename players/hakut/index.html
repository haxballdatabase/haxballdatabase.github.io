Biography

Hakut represented Romania in major international HaxBall competition and appeared in Nations Cup finals across multiple editions.

His earliest major final currently documented in the archive is the Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy final. Two years later, he returned to the Nations Cup final with a different Romanian lineup and finished the tournament as champion.

Because the current archive contains considerably more information about his national-team appearances than his club career, Hakut's profile is presently centered on his documented international record.

International Career

Hakut reached at least two documented major international finals with Romania, finishing runner-up in 2020 before becoming a Nations Cup champion in 2022.

Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy: First Documented Final

▸ Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy

Hakut reached the Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy final with Romania.

Romania lined up with El Drago, aMp, GirlontaR and Hakut.

Their opponents were Israel, represented by Yannex, P4ER, Unique and Meyer.

Israel won the tournament, leaving Hakut and Romania with a runner-up finish.

Romania: El Drago • aMp • GirlontaR • Hakut
Israel: Yannex • P4ER • Unique • Meyer
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2022: International Champion

Two years after the 2020 runner-up finish, Hakut returned to a Nations Cup final with Romania.

Romania fielded Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut.

Their opponents were Turkey, whose lineup consisted of Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Romania won the tournament, giving Hakut a documented Nations Cup championship and completing a progression from runner-up in 2020 to champion in 2022.

Romania: Modric • aMp • Al1s • Hakut
Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Vichoexe • Kyrai
Result: 🏆 Winner
