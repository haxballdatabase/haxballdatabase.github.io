Razmo is a HaxBall player from the Flash era, best documented for reaching two HaxBall Champions League finals. He finished as runner-up with Les 5 Fantastiques in HaxBall Champions League #4 and later returned to the final with Die Galaktischen in Edition #6.

His two recorded final appearances came against two of the strongest teams of the early Champions League period. Les 5 Fantastiques lost 1–0 to Silas Beavers in HCL #4, while Die Galaktischen were defeated 4–0 by Hellhounds in HCL #6.

Biography

Razmo competed during the Flash era and is primarily documented in the archive through two HaxBall Champions League final appearances across Editions #4 and #6.

In Edition #4, he represented Les 5 Fantastiques alongside pkp, Rooney and Ced. Two editions later, he appeared in another final with Die Galaktischen alongside Caccapupù, iNoob and Nino.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Razmo reached two HaxBall Champions League finals during the early Flash-era period, finishing as runner-up in both campaigns.

HaxBall Champions League #4

Razmo reached his first documented Champions League final with Les 5 Fantastiques. The final lineup consisted of pkp, Rooney, Razmo and Ced.

Their opponents were defending champions Silas Beavers, represented by SeRGeN, Dexter, Иukroyš and Clinical Striker.

The final was decided by a single goal. Dexter supplied the pass and Иukroyš scored the decisive finish, giving Silas Beavers a 1–0 victory and leaving Razmo and Les 5 Fantastiques as runners-up.

Final Lineup: pkp • Rooney • Razmo • Ced
Final: Les 5 Fantastiques 0–1 Silas Beavers
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #6

Razmo returned to the Champions League final in Edition #6 with Die Galaktischen. The final lineup consisted of Razmo, Caccapupù, iNoob and Nino.

Their opponents were Hellhounds, whose championship lineup featured Adriano, aguero10, Stingox and Ced.

Hellhounds controlled the match and completed a 4–0 victory, giving Razmo his second documented Champions League runner-up finish.

Final Lineup: Caccapupù • iNoob • Nino • Razmo
Final: Die Galaktischen 0–4 Hellhounds
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Legacy

Razmo's clearest documented legacy comes from appearing in two HaxBall Champions League finals with two different teams during the Flash era.

Although neither campaign ended with a championship, his presence in the HCL #4 and HCL #6 finals places him among the recurring finalists of the early Champions League period.
