Yannex is a HaxBall player from Israel who competed across both the Flash and HTML5 eras. During his prime, he was regarded as one of the leading players of his generation.

He became particularly well known as one of the central members of the successful QuickSwans and Seeders generation, combining major domestic success with a long record of appearances at the highest level of competitive HaxBall.

His HaxBall Champions League career included a championship with Antartic Lions in Edition #8 before later reaching consecutive finals with the Seeders core in Editions #17 and #18. Yannex scored the opening goal in both of those later finals.

At international level, Yannex also became a multiple-time champion with Israel, winning the Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy and Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Biography

Yannex's early Champions League success came with Antartic Lions, while the later and more recognizable part of his club career became closely connected with the Feed-Me competitive scene and the group that eventually formed Seeders.

He belonged to a core containing several prominent players of the period, including Isco, Marble, Pringles, aguero10 and shaxlele.

The group achieved sustained domestic success before carrying the same core into European competition, where Yannex became part of consecutive HaxBall Champions League final lineups.

Yannex also had significant success with Israel, collecting consecutive Nations Cup championships during the later part of his career.

Seeders

Yannex was one of the main members of the Seeders generation that established one of the strongest periods of domestic dominance in Feed-Me League history.

The team's core initially competed under the QuickSwans banner and won the Feed-Me League championship before continuing together under the Seeders name, with Isco taking over as captain.

Seeders then won Feed-Me League Seasons 24 and 25. Combined with the previous QuickSwans championship, Yannex and the core completed three consecutive league titles.

Yannex was also the club's leading player statistically. With 85 goals and 53 assists, he became both Seeders' all-time top goalscorer and all-time leading assist provider.

Seeders became especially recognizable for its possession-based passing game, continuously circulating the ball and creating passing angles throughout matches.

During the same period, the team recorded a 118-goal Division 1 season, establishing the single-season team scoring record in the competition.

This period became the defining club chapter of Yannex's career and established him as one of the central figures associated with the Seeders generation.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Yannex appeared in three HaxBall Champions League finals across his career, winning one championship and later finishing runner-up twice.

His first final came in Edition #8 with Antartic Lions. Years later, he returned to the European stage with the Seeders core, reaching consecutive finals in #17 and #18.

A particularly notable aspect of the latter two appearances was his direct contribution in the finals themselves. Yannex scored the opening goal in both matches.

HaxBall Champions League #8: European Champion

Yannex's first HaxBall Champions League success came in Edition #8 as part of Antartic Lions.

The team's established lineup included tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer. During the final, Yannex entered the match from the bench, replacing Meyer.

Antartic Lions went on to secure the championship, giving Yannex the first HaxBall Champions League title of his career.

Team: Antartic Lions

HaxBall Champions League #17: So Close

For HaxBall Champions League #17, the Seeders core entered the competition under the BLACKOUT banner.

BLACKOUT reached the final against Klub Emeryta, whose lineup consisted of kojo, Ter, SAKY and xSha.

BLACKOUT controlled much of the early possession and took the lead when Isco created the opportunity for Yannex, who finished to make the score 1–0.

Klub Emeryta responded through xSha and SAKY to move 2–1 ahead. With less than a minute remaining, aguero10 found Isco, whose equalizer sent the final into Golden Goal.

The match was eventually decided by xSha, who converted a rapid counter-attack to give Klub Emeryta a 3–2 victory. Yannex therefore finished the final as runner-up despite scoring the opening goal.

Final: BLACKOUT 2–3 Klub Emeryta
Yannex: Opening Goal
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #18: Seeders' Nightmare

One edition later, Yannex returned with the same core, this time officially representing Seeders.

Their route to the final included an important quarter-final against Stiff Wind. After falling behind early, a combined effort from Yannex and Isco brought Seeders level before the team completed a 3–1 comeback victory.

Seeders reached the final against The Elders, whose lineup consisted of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak.

Regular time ended 0–0. In extra time, Marble found Yannex, who converted the opportunity and gave Seeders a 1–0 lead.

Vak equalized before Seeders regained the lead through a deflected attacking sequence involving Isco and Yannex. With less than two minutes remaining, SAKY made the score 2–2 and forced Golden Goal.

Only 15 seconds into Golden Goal, SAKY scored again to complete a 3–2 victory for The Elders. For Yannex and Seeders, it completed a second consecutive HaxBall Champions League final defeat through Golden Goal.

Final: Seeders 2–3 The Elders
Yannex: Opening Goal
Deciding Goal: 15 seconds into Golden Goal
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

Yannex represented Israel in international competition and became part of consecutive Nations Cup championship teams.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy

Israel reached the final with Yannex, Isco, P4ER and LaggerMet, facing Portugal's Pringles, Ter, Taziek and SAKY.

The final developed into a closely contested match and remained undecided during regulation time, continuing into extra time.

The decisive moment came when Ter attempted to play the ball backwards but left it in front of LaggerMet, who converted the opportunity and secured the championship for Israel.

Israel: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Portugal: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy

Yannex added another major international championship with Israel in Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Israel faced Romania in the final. The Israel lineup consisted of Yannex, P4ER, Unique and Meyer, while Romania lined up with El Drago, aMp, GirlontaR and Hakut.

Israel won the final, giving Yannex a second consecutive Nations Cup championship.

Israel: Yannex • P4ER • Unique • Meyer
Romania: El Drago • aMp • GirlontaR • Hakut
Result: 🏆 Winner

Legacy

Yannex built a career that combined success at European, domestic and international level.

His appearance in Antartic Lions' HaxBall Champions League #8 championship gave him a European title before the later Seeders period became the defining club chapter of his career.

With Seeders, Yannex was not only part of the QuickSwans and Seeders core that completed three consecutive Feed-Me League championships, but also became the club's record goalscorer and record assist provider.

His later HaxBall Champions League career produced another distinctive record. Yannex scored the opening goal in consecutive HCL finals, but both matches eventually ended in 3–2 Golden Goal defeats.

Together with consecutive Nations Cup championships for Israel in 2019 and 2020, his HCL #8 title and long-running success in Feed-Me gave Yannex one of the more accomplished competitive careers associated with the Israel HaxBall community of the period.
