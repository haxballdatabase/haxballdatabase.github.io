Biography

CRonaldo is a HaxBall player from Belgium who played during the Flash era.

He was able to play in both the DM and AM roles, giving him the ability to operate in different areas of midfield.

His most notable recorded achievement came with Antartic Lions, where he became a HaxBall Champions League champion.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #8: Champion with Antartic Lions

CRonaldo won the HaxBall Champions League #8 as part of Antartic Lions.

The championship lineup began with tonnnek, Rq*, CRonaldo and Meyer, with Yannex later appearing from the bench.

Antartic Lions secured the championship, giving CRonaldo the major recorded title of his competitive career.
