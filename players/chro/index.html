Chro is a HaxBall player from the Flash era, best documented for his time with YeS AllStars. He was part of the team's run to the final of HaxBall Champions League #11.

Chro produced the most significant individual scoring performance for YeS AllStars in the final, scoring both of the team's goals in the 5–2 defeat to Angry Bulls.

His first goal came from a pass by DrG and gave YeS AllStars an early 1–0 lead. Later in the match, Fritz found Chro for his second goal, reducing the score to 4–2.

Chro also represented the Netherlands at international level, reaching the final of Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy. The Netherlands took an early 1–0 lead against Turkey but were eventually defeated, leaving Chro with a second documented major runner-up finish.

Biography

Chro competed during the Flash era and was part of YeS AllStars, one of the prominent teams of that period.

His clearest documented major tournament appearance in the current archive comes from the team's HaxBall Champions League #11 campaign, where he reached the final alongside Caccapupù, Fritz and DrG.

His documented career also includes an international final with the Netherlands at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #11

Chro reached the HaxBall Champions League #11 final with YeS AllStars. The final lineup consisted of Chro, Caccapupù, Fritz and DrG.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, represented by Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

YeS AllStars made the stronger start. DrG found Chro with a pass, and Chro finished the opportunity cleanly to give his team an early 1–0 lead.

Angry Bulls recovered and eventually moved into a 4–1 lead, but Chro scored again. This time Fritz provided the pass, and Chro converted the chance for his second goal of the final, reducing the score to 4–2.

Angry Bulls scored once more before the end of the match, completing a 5–2 victory. Chro therefore finished the final with two goals, scoring every YeS AllStars goal in the match, while the team ended the edition as runners-up.

Final: YeS AllStars 2–5 Angry Bulls

International Career

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

Chro reached the final of Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy with the Netherlands.

The Netherlands lined up with Dereh, Hulk, Chro and Styl.

Their opponents were Turkey, represented by Misaj, Liebe, Marble and Andrea.

The Netherlands made the stronger start and took an early 1–0 lead, but Turkey recovered and completed the comeback to win the tournament.

The result left Chro and the Netherlands as Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy runners-up.

Legacy

Chro is documented through major final appearances at both club and international level, reaching the HaxBall Champions League #11 final with YeS AllStars and the Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy final with the Netherlands.

His two-goal performance in the HCL #11 final remains the clearest individual contribution currently preserved in the archive. His later Nations Cup final appearance added another major runner-up finish to his documented competitive record.
