Altidore is a Turkish HaxBall player remembered as a strong and consistently competitive attacking player in HaxBall history. Known for his versatility and ability to remain effective at a high level, he competed across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

Playing as both a striker and AM, Altidore combined scoring ability with creativity and proved capable of contributing in different attacking roles. He reached three HaxBall Champions League finals, winning Editions #13 and #16 with Stiff Wind and finishing runner-up with Skins in #15.

Most closely associated with Stiff Wind, Altidore also achieved significant results with Turkey. He finished runner-up at the 2018 Nations Cup before later becoming an international champion by winning the 2021 Nations Cup.

Biography

Altidore competed across both the Flash and HTML5 eras of European HaxBall, maintaining a level that allowed him to remain relevant in competitive play across different periods of the game.

He became most closely associated with Stiff Wind, but also spent a period representing Skins. His time with both teams included appearances at the highest level of European competition.

His career was built less around individual records and more around his ability to remain effective in strong competitive lineups. Altidore could play as both a striker and AM, allowing him to take different attacking responsibilities depending on the structure of the team.

Between Editions #13 and #16, Altidore reached three HaxBall Champions League finals in four editions, winning twice with Stiff Wind and reaching another final with Skins.

His national-team career also produced two major Nations Cup finals, first finishing runner-up with Turkey in 2018 before winning the competition in 2021.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Altidore reached three HaxBall Champions League finals, recording two championships and one runner-up finish.

His first title came with Stiff Wind in #13. Two editions later he reached the final with Skins in #15, before returning to Stiff Wind and winning the competition again in #16.

HaxBall Champions League #13: The First Championship

Altidore won his first HaxBall Champions League title in Edition #13 with Stiff Wind.

The championship lineup consisted of Liéds in goal, Liebe at DM, ROCKY BALBOA at AM and Altidore as striker.

In the semi-final against Gladiators, Altidore scored the decisive goal after receiving a pass from ROCKY BALBOA, sending Stiff Wind through with a 1–0 victory.

In the final against Lux Aeterna, Altidore remained heavily involved in Stiff Wind's attack. He assisted ROCKY BALBOA for two goals before later scoring himself as Stiff Wind secured a 3–1 victory.

The result gave Stiff Wind its second HaxBall Champions League title and Altidore his first.

Championship Lineup (#13): Liéds • Liebe • ROCKY BALBOA • Altidore
Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #15: Final with Skins

Two editions after his first championship, Altidore reached another HaxBall Champions League final, this time representing Skins.

The Skins final lineup consisted of kojo, Gilbert, Vanessa and Altidore.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, whose lineup featured Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

The final placed Altidore against a strong Angry Bulls side attempting to win the club's second HaxBall Champions League title.

Angry Bulls won the final 3–1, leaving Altidore and Skins as runners-up. Despite the defeat, the campaign gave Altidore a second HaxBall Champions League final appearance.

Final Lineup (#15): kojo • Gilbert • Vanessa • Altidore
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #16: From 0–2 to Champion

In the following edition, Altidore returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Stiff Wind.

The structure of the team differed from his first championship. PiperSalvia played in goal, ROCKY BALBOA operated at DM, Altidore played at AM and Catrice occupied the striker position.

Stiff Wind faced Klub Emeryta and found itself in serious trouble early in the final. Two goals from aguero10 gave Klub Emeryta a 2–0 lead.

Near the end of the first half, Altidore became one of the main figures in Stiff Wind's response. He found Catrice with a pass, allowing the striker to reduce the deficit to 2–1.

Only moments later, Catrice returned the pass and Altidore scored the 2–2 equalizer. Stiff Wind had erased a two-goal deficit in less than a minute.

The match eventually continued into Golden Goal, where Catrice scored the deciding goal to complete the comeback.

Stiff Wind won 3–2, giving Altidore his second HaxBall Champions League championship. After finishing runner-up with Skins one edition earlier, he had returned to the final and finished on the winning side.

Championship Lineup (#16): PiperSalvia • ROCKY BALBOA • Altidore • Catrice
Result: 🏆 Winner

International Career

Altidore represented Turkey in international competition and reached two major Nations Cup finals during his career.

Turkey finished runner-up in 2018 before Altidore later became a Nations Cup champion with the national team in 2021.

Nations Cup 2018: Final Against Portugal

Altidore reached the 2018 Nations Cup final with Turkey, facing Portugal.

Turkey lined up with Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa, while Portugal fielded Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot.

Turkey took the lead near the end of the first half when Müllewa scored to make it 1–0.

Portugal responded during the second half. Following a misunderstanding, the ball was left in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity to equalize at 1–1.

The final then produced an unusual fair-play sequence. Turkey conceded another goal during a situation affected by ping. Portugal chose not to retain the advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to score and restoring the match to 2–2.

Later, SAKY created the decisive opportunity for Bot, who scored to give Portugal a 3–2 victory. Altidore and Turkey therefore finished the tournament as runners-up.

Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2021: International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2021

Three years after finishing runner-up in 2018, Altidore represented Turkey during its successful 2021 Nations Cup campaign.

Turkey lined up with Vichoexe, Bullas, Kyrai and Altidore, while Poland featured kojo, miso, Kise and frenkie.

Turkey won the tournament, giving Altidore his first Nations Cup championship and adding a major international title to a career that already included two HaxBall Champions League victories.

Turkey: Vichoexe • Bullas • Kyrai • Altidore
Opponent: Poland
Poland: kojo • miso • Kise • frenkie
Result: 🏆 Winner

Playing Style

Altidore was an attacking player capable of operating as both a striker and AM.

His game allowed him to contribute in more than one way. He could occupy the advanced striker position and provide a finishing option, but was also comfortable dropping into a more creative attacking role and linking with the player ahead of him.

The #16 final provided a clear example of that balance. Altidore first created Catrice's goal before moving into a scoring position himself for the equalizer.

Rather than being remembered as one of the game's dominant individual superstars, Altidore's strength was his ability to remain consistently competitive at a high level. He could contribute to strong lineups, adapt to different attacking roles and remain effective across different periods of competitive HaxBall.

Legacy

Altidore's competitive record reflects a player who maintained a strong level across different periods of HaxBall and remained capable of contributing to high-level teams.

Reaching three HaxBall Champions League finals and winning two of them gave him a strong European record. His appearances with both Stiff Wind and Skins also showed that his presence at the highest level was not limited to a single lineup.

His contribution to the dramatic #16 comeback is particularly representative of his career. With Stiff Wind two goals behind in a major final, Altidore produced an assist and a goal within the sequence that brought the team back into the match.

Internationally, Altidore reached the Nations Cup final with Turkey in 2018, narrowly losing 3–2 to Portugal. Three years later, he returned to the tournament and became a 2021 Nations Cup champion.

Altidore is therefore remembered as a good and successful attacking player who may not have belonged to the very highest individual tier of HaxBall history, but possessed the quality, versatility and consistency required to remain competitive across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.
