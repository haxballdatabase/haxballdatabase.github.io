Vichoexe is a HaxBall player from Turkey regarded as one of the leading players of his generation. Known for his versatility, he has been capable of performing across several different roles rather than being associated with a single primary position, playing effectively as GK, DM and AM.

His major achievements include winning the HaxBall Champions League twice, first with Slappers in Edition #21 and later with Wicked Guys in Edition #24.

At international level, Vichoexe won the 2021 Nations Cup with Turkey and returned to the final the following year, finishing runner-up to Romania in Nations Cup 2022.

Still active in 2026, Vichoexe continues to compete at a high level in the current competitive scene.

Biography

Vichoexe developed into a versatile player capable of contributing from several areas of the pitch. During his career, he appeared as GK, DM and AM, with no single position completely defining his game.

Before HaxBall Champions League #21, however, Vichoexe had yet to establish a major championship record at the highest level.

That changed when Misaj selected him for a newly assembled Slappers lineup. Vichoexe was trusted in the AM role, while Norbigo was given responsibility in goal and Marble completed the lineup alongside Misaj.

Slappers went on to win the tournament, giving Vichoexe, Norbigo and Marble their first HaxBall Champions League titles.

Vichoexe later became an international champion with Turkey in 2021 and reached another Nations Cup final in 2022.

He eventually returned to the top of European club competition with Wicked Guys, winning his second HaxBall Champions League title in Edition #24.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Vichoexe won two HaxBall Champions League championships, achieving success with two different teams: Slappers in Edition #21 and Wicked Guys in Edition #24.

His first championship represented the major breakthrough of his career, while his later victory with Wicked Guys added a second European title several editions later.

HaxBall Champions League #21: Project Slappers

Before HaxBall Champions League #21, Vichoexe had yet to establish a major championship record at the highest level.

Following the events of the previous Champions League season, Misaj took control of Slappers and assembled a new project motivated by unfinished business from the previous campaign.

For the new lineup, Misaj placed his trust in Norbigo and Vichoexe. Norbigo was given responsibility in goal, while Vichoexe was trusted in the AM role. Marble completed the lineup alongside Misaj.

The newly assembled Slappers side faced defending champions Smokin' Aces in the semi-final. The match remained scoreless through regular time before Slappers secured a 1–0 victory in extra time.

In the final, Slappers faced Angry Bulls, one of the most successful teams in the competition and winners of each of their previous HaxBall Champions League finals.

The final developed very differently from the closely contested semi-final. Slappers took control early and produced a dominant attacking performance, eventually winning 7–2.

The result completed Vichoexe's breakthrough at the highest level, giving Vichoexe, Norbigo and Marble their first HaxBall Champions League titles.

Vichoexe Role: AM
Final: Slappers 7–2 Angry Bulls
Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #24: Champion Again

Vichoexe later returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Wicked Guys.

The Wicked Guys championship lineup consisted of Wiagoal, Vichoexe, Taziek and Makenzie.

Their opponents were Good Guys, represented by gggggk, B ull, William and Torment.

The decisive moment came during the opening minutes of the first half, when Taziek placed the ball into the corner to give Wicked Guys a 1–0 lead.

The rest of the match remained closely contested, but Wicked Guys protected the advantage and secured the championship with a 1–0 victory.

The title gave Vichoexe his second HaxBall Champions League championship.

Championship Lineup (#24): Wiagoal • Vichoexe • Taziek • Makenzie
Final: Wicked Guys 1–0 Good Guys
Result: 🏆 Winner

International Career

Vichoexe represented Turkey at international level and reached consecutive Nations Cup finals in 2021 and 2022.

Turkey won the tournament in 2021 before returning to the final the following year, where Romania prevented Vichoexe from winning consecutive international championships.

Nations Cup 2021: International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2021

Vichoexe became a major international champion with Turkey in the 2021 Nations Cup.

Turkey lined up with Vichoexe, Bullas, Kyrai and Altidore.

Their opponents Poland were represented by kojo, miso, Kise and frenkie.

Turkey won the tournament, giving Vichoexe a major international championship alongside his club achievements.

Turkey: Vichoexe • Bullas • Kyrai • Altidore
Opponent: Poland
Poland: kojo • miso • Kise • frenkie
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2022: Back in the Final

One year after winning the Nations Cup, Vichoexe returned to the final with Turkey in 2022.

Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Their opponents were Romania, represented by Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut.

Romania won the final, ending Turkey's attempt to win consecutive Nations Cup championships and leaving Vichoexe with a runner-up finish.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Vichoexe • Kyrai
Romania: Modric • aMp • Al1s • Hakut
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Playing Style

Versatility has been one of the defining characteristics of Vichoexe's game.

Rather than having one clear primary position, he has been capable of playing effectively as GK, DM and AM. This flexibility has allowed him to take on significantly different responsibilities depending on the needs of his team.

His role during HaxBall Champions League #21 demonstrated the attacking side of that versatility, with Vichoexe operating at AM for the championship-winning Slappers lineup.

His ability to move between defensive, midfield and attacking responsibilities has remained an important part of his value as a player.

Legacy

Vichoexe's career is defined by versatility and major success across different teams.

His breakthrough with Slappers in HaxBall Champions League #21 established him at the highest competitive level, while his later championship with Wicked Guys gave him a second HCL title with a different club.

His international record added another important part to his career. Vichoexe won the 2021 Nations Cup with Turkey and returned to the final in 2022, finishing runner-up against Romania.

Those consecutive Nations Cup final appearances, combined with two HaxBall Champions League championships, gave Vichoexe a strong record across both club and international competition.

Still active in 2026, his ability to perform across GK, DM and AM has allowed him to remain an important player in the modern competitive scene and one of the leading players of his generation.
