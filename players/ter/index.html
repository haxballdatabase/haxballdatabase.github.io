Ter is a Portuguese HaxBall player best remembered as the DM of Klub Emeryta's HaxBall Champions League #17 championship team.

He was part of a heavily rebuilt Klub Emeryta lineup alongside kojo, SAKY and xSha. The team reached the final against BLACKOUT and won one of the competition's more dramatic championship matches, securing the title through Golden Goal.

Ter also achieved major success with Portugal, winning the 2018 Nations Cup after a 3–2 final victory over Turkey.

He later returned to a major international final at Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy, where Portugal finished runner-up against Israel.

Biography

Ter became part of Klub Emeryta during a period of major change for the team.

The club had reached the HaxBall Champions League #16 final with a lineup of Per, kojo, ANDREA and aguero10, narrowly losing the championship to Stiff Wind through Golden Goal.

By the following edition, the lineup had been almost completely rebuilt. kojo was the only player from the previous final to remain, with Ter, SAKY and xSha joining him for HaxBall Champions League #17.

Ter occupied the DM role in the new lineup, playing behind the younger attacking partnership of SAKY and xSha.

His career also included major international success with Portugal. Ter became a Nations Cup champion in 2018 before reaching another international final in Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Ter's principal European achievement came in HaxBall Champions League #17, where he represented Klub Emeryta during the club's championship campaign.

The rebuilt team reached the final against BLACKOUT, a side formed around the Seeders core and characterized by a possession-heavy style of play.

HaxBall Champions League #17: Champion with Klub Emeryta

Klub Emeryta entered the final with kojo in goal, Ter at DM and SAKY and xSha forming the attacking part of the lineup.

Their opponents, BLACKOUT, controlled much of the early possession and took a 1–0 lead when Isco found Yannex for the opening goal.

Klub Emeryta responded through a quick counter-attack. SAKY supplied xSha, who scored the equalizer to make it 1–1.

SAKY later put Klub Emeryta ahead from a crowded corner situation, giving the team a 2–1 advantage.

With less than a minute remaining, BLACKOUT found a late response. aguero10 set up Isco, whose goal brought the final back to 2–2 and sent the championship into Golden Goal.

For Klub Emeryta, the situation carried an unusual sense of repetition. Only one edition earlier, the club had also entered Golden Goal at 2–2 in the HaxBall Champions League final, only to lose against Stiff Wind.

This time the ending was different. Klub Emeryta broke forward and xSha converted the decisive opportunity, scoring the Golden Goal for a 3–2 victory.

The result gave Klub Emeryta its first HaxBall Champions League title and made Ter a HaxBall Champions League champion.

Championship Lineup (#17): kojo • Ter • SAKY • xSha
Final: Klub Emeryta 3–2 BLACKOUT

Role at Klub Emeryta

Ter played as the DM in Klub Emeryta's championship lineup.

The structure of the team placed kojo behind him in goal, while SAKY and xSha operated in the attacking positions. Ter therefore occupied the deeper outfield role within the four-player lineup.

This made his role different from the two younger attackers who produced most of the final's decisive offensive moments. Ter's recorded role in the team was primarily connected with the DM position rather than individual attacking statistics.

International Career

Ter represented Portugal in major international competition, winning the Nations Cup in 2018 and later reaching another final in 2019 - Big Easy.

Nations Cup 2018: International Champion

Ter became a major international champion with Portugal at the 2018 Nations Cup.

Portugal lined up with Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot, while Turkey fielded Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Turkey took the lead near the end of the first half when Müllewa scored to make it 1–0.

Portugal responded in the second half. Following a misunderstanding in the Turkish defense, the ball was left in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity and equalized at 1–1.

The final then produced a notable fair-play moment. Turkey conceded another goal during a situation affected by ping. Portugal chose not to retain the resulting advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to score and restoring the match to 2–2.

The championship was decided later when SAKY found Bot with a pass. Bot converted the opportunity to give Portugal a 3–2 lead, which became the final score.

Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy: Israel vs Portugal

Ter represented Portugal during Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy and reached the tournament final against Israel.

The two teams entered the final with the following lineups:

Israel: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Portugal: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY

The final developed into a very difficult and closely contested match. Neither side was able to settle the championship during regulation time, and the match eventually continued into extra time.

The decisive moment came during the extra period. Ter attempted to play the ball backwards, but the touch instead left the ball directly in front of LaggerMet.

LaggerMet immediately took advantage of the opportunity and scored the decisive goal, giving Israel the lead in extra time.

The goal ultimately decided the final, with Israel winning Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy and Portugal finishing as runners-up.

Portugal Final Lineup: Pringles • Ter • Taziek • SAKY
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Legacy

Ter's place in HaxBall history is primarily connected with Klub Emeryta's HaxBall Champions League #17 championship and his international career with Portugal.

He formed part of a Klub Emeryta lineup that was almost completely rebuilt from the previous edition and managed to win the title only one season after the club had suffered a Golden Goal defeat in the final.

Playing at DM alongside kojo, SAKY and xSha, Ter was part of the four-player lineup that defeated BLACKOUT 3–2 after Golden Goal and delivered Klub Emeryta its first HaxBall Champions League championship.

His success extended to international competition in 2018, when Portugal defeated Turkey 3–2 and Ter became a Nations Cup champion.

The following year, he reached another major final with Portugal at Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy, where Israel won during extra time.

With major championships at both club and international level, Ter's career includes a HaxBall Champions League title and a Nations Cup title.
