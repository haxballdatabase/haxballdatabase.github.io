kojo is a HaxBall goalkeeper best known as the captain of Klub Emeryta and for holding one of the strongest statistical goalkeeping records in Feed Me history.

He holds the all-time Feed Me record for most clean sheets, recording 85 across his career. At Division 1 level, kojo recorded 53 clean sheets, a historical record he shares with Pringles.

As captain, kojo became the central figure across several versions of Klub Emeryta. The players around him changed considerably over time, but he remained closely connected to the club throughout one of the most successful periods in its history.

His HaxBall Champions League career was especially unusual. kojo reached four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals from Editions #14 through #17, representing Klub Emeryta in three of them and Skins in Edition #15.

After finishing as runner-up in the first three finals of that sequence, kojo returned to Klub Emeryta and finally became a HaxBall Champions League champion in Edition #17.

Biography

kojo became one of the players most closely associated with Klub Emeryta, serving as the club's captain during its strongest competitive period.

Klub Emeryta underwent several major changes in personnel across different seasons, but kojo remained the constant figure around which successive versions of the team were built. His presence connected squads featuring players such as Liebe and DrG with later groups containing aguero10, ANDREA, SAKY and xSha.

His European career extended beyond Klub Emeryta as well. During HaxBall Champions League #15, kojo represented Skins and reached another final, giving him four consecutive final appearances between Editions #14 and #17.

The sequence was initially painful. kojo finished runner-up in Editions #14, #15 and #16 before returning as Klub Emeryta captain for another attempt in Edition #17.

That fourth appearance finally produced the championship, completing a remarkable run of four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals.

Away from European competition, kojo's longevity as a goalkeeper is reflected most clearly by his Feed Me statistics. With 85 clean sheets, he stands alone as the competition's all-time record holder.

HaxBall Champions League Career

kojo reached the HaxBall Champions League final in four consecutive editions, appearing in every championship match from #14 through #17.

Three of those appearances came with Klub Emeryta, while Edition #15 saw him reach the final as a Skins player. His overall record from the sequence was one championship and three runner-up finishes.

The Klub Emeryta finals produced an additional historical curiosity: Editions #14, #16 and #17 all finished 3–2 after Golden Goal.

HaxBall Champions League #14

kojo's first final in the sequence came in Edition #14, when he captained Klub Emeryta against Omertà.

Klub Emeryta's lineup featured kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, with ANDREA also entering during the second half.

DrG opened the scoring for Klub Emeryta before Mamba scored twice to turn the match around for Omertà. Liebe then equalized to bring the final to 2–2.

After the match remained level through the main period of extra time, the championship moved into Golden Goal. Deliric eventually scored the winner to give Omertà a 3–2 victory.

Final Lineup (#14): kojo • Liebe • DrG • Echo

HaxBall Champions League #15: Final with Skins

One edition later, kojo returned to the HaxBall Champions League final, this time representing Skins.

The final placed Skins against fellow Turkish side Angry Bulls, who entered the match with a championship lineup of Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Angry Bulls took control of the match through the partnership between Misaj and Müllewa. Misaj provided two assists, while Müllewa scored twice as Angry Bulls established the advantage.

Skins were ultimately defeated 3–1, giving kojo his second consecutive HaxBall Champions League runner-up finish.

The appearance also showed that kojo's run at the highest European level was not limited entirely to Klub Emeryta. Even during a season away from his main club, he once again reached the championship match.

HaxBall Champions League #16

In Edition #16, kojo returned to Klub Emeryta and reached the HaxBall Champions League final for the third consecutive edition.

The Klub Emeryta lineup consisted of Per, kojo, ANDREA and aguero10, with Stiff Wind standing between them and the title.

Klub Emeryta made an exceptional start. aguero10 scored twice to establish a commanding 2–0 lead.

The match changed rapidly near the end of the opening half. Catrice and Altidore scored in quick succession, bringing Stiff Wind back to 2–2.

The second half remained scoreless and the final moved into Golden Goal, where Catrice completed the comeback to give Stiff Wind a 3–2 victory.

kojo had now reached three consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals, but all three had ended with runner-up finishes.

Final Lineup (#16): Per • kojo • ANDREA • aguero10

HaxBall Champions League #17: Fourth Final, First Title

Edition #17 saw kojo return to the final yet again, extending his remarkable run to four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals.

As Klub Emeryta captain, kojo remained the central link to the club's previous campaigns, but the lineup around him had changed almost completely. He was joined by Ter, SAKY and xSha.

Klub Emeryta entered the final as the underdog against BLACKOUT, a side built around the established Seeders core. SAKY and xSha had been competing in Division 2 only a few seasons earlier.

BLACKOUT controlled much of the early match through possession and took a 1–0 lead when Isco found Yannex.

Klub Emeryta responded on the counter. SAKY created the equalizer for xSha before scoring himself from a corner-spam situation to put the team 2–1 ahead.

With less than a minute remaining, aguero10 found Isco for the BLACKOUT equalizer, sending the final into Golden Goal at 2–2.

For the third time in kojo's three Klub Emeryta HaxBall Champions League finals, the title would be decided by Golden Goal with the score level at 2–2.

This time, however, Klub Emeryta found the winner. xSha converted a rapid one-on-one counter-attack to complete a 3–2 victory.

After three consecutive runner-up finishes across two different clubs, kojo finally became a HaxBall Champions League champion on his fourth consecutive trip to the final.

For Klub Emeryta's captain, the title completed a long European journey. He had experienced the club's Golden Goal defeat to Omertà in #14, reached another final with Skins in #15, returned to lose another Golden Goal final with Klub Emeryta in #16, and finally lifted the trophy in #17.

Championship Lineup (#17): kojo • Ter • SAKY • xSha

Feed Me League

While kojo's European career included four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals, his strongest individual statistical record came in the Feed Me League.

Across all recorded Feed Me competition, kojo accumulated 85 clean sheets, the highest total of any goalkeeper in the competition's history.

At Division 1 level, kojo recorded 53 clean sheets. He and Pringles both hold the historical Division 1 record with 53 clean sheets each.

The records underline the longevity of kojo's career as a goalkeeper. His overall total of 85 stands alone as the Feed Me all-time record, while his Division 1 record is shared with Pringles.

Playing Style

kojo was primarily known as a goalkeeper, with his statistical record built around long-term defensive consistency.

Clean sheets are influenced by the entire defensive structure of a team rather than one player alone, but accumulating 85 across a career reflects sustained involvement in successful defensive sides over a long period.

His Division 1 record is particularly notable because it was achieved at the highest domestic league level. With 53 clean sheets, kojo remains one of the two holders of the historical record alongside Pringles.

His role as Klub Emeryta captain also gave him importance beyond individual statistics. Across several changes in personnel, kojo remained one of the constants around which the club continued to compete at the highest level.

Legacy

kojo's place in HaxBall history is built around his longevity as a goalkeeper, his leadership of Klub Emeryta and an unusual run of repeated appearances on the European stage.

His 85 career clean sheets make him Feed Me's all-time clean-sheet record holder. At Division 1 level, kojo and Pringles share the historical record with 53 clean sheets each.

As Klub Emeryta captain, kojo became the constant figure across several different versions of the club. Despite major changes to the squad around him, the team repeatedly remained among Europe's leading sides.

His European record is equally unusual. kojo reached four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals from #14 to #17, representing Klub Emeryta in three and Skins in one.

After finishing runner-up in the first three, he finally won the competition with Klub Emeryta in Edition #17. The three Klub Emeryta finals themselves all ended 3–2 after Golden Goal, giving his Champions League career an especially unusual pattern.

By combining the all-time Feed Me clean-sheet record, the captaincy of Klub Emeryta, four consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals and a European championship, kojo built one of the more distinctive goalkeeping careers in competitive HaxBall history.
