nova is a competitive HaxBall player who won HaxBall Champions League #19 with Angry Bulls.

Although he was not considered one of the elite individual players of his generation, nova became part of an important chapter in Angry Bulls' history after unexpectedly playing as goalkeeper in the #19 final against Stiff Wind.

He later joined the Smokin' Aces squad for HaxBall Champions League #20, where he served as a reserve player.

Biography

nova competed in the European HaxBall scene during the period surrounding HaxBall Champions League #19 and #20.

His career was not defined by major individual statistics or recognition among the game's greatest players. Instead, his most notable contribution came as part of an Angry Bulls side competing for its third HaxBall Champions League championship.

After his time with Angry Bulls, nova was also included in the Smokin' Aces squad during the following edition of the competition.

HaxBall Champions League Career

nova's HaxBall Champions League career is primarily associated with two consecutive editions of the tournament, representing Angry Bulls in #19 before later appearing in the wider Smokin' Aces squad in #20.

His championship came in #19, where he was required to take on an unexpectedly important role in the final.

HaxBall Champions League #19: Called Upon in the Final

Angry Bulls reached the HaxBall Champions League #19 final against Stiff Wind, with both clubs competing for their third championship.

The original plan was for Pringles to play in goal for Angry Bulls. However, he was unable to attend the final, leaving the team without its expected goalkeeper.

nova had been part of the squad as a reserve player, but the late change meant he was suddenly required to start in goal in one of the biggest matches of the season.

Before the match, Misaj showed confidence in nova and encouraged him to trust himself in the role. The two had a good relationship, and Misaj's backing helped nova approach the unexpected responsibility with greater confidence.

Stiff Wind took the lead during the final, putting Angry Bulls 1–0 behind. Despite the pressure and the late change in goalkeeper, nova produced a solid performance and kept the team in a position to recover.

Angry Bulls eventually equalized when Misaj used his trademark X-fake to create an opening for Necro, who finished the move to make it 1–1.

The match continued into extra time, where Misaj scored the winning goal to complete the comeback.

Angry Bulls won the final 2–1, securing its third HaxBall Champions League title. For nova, the match turned an expected reserve appearance into the most important performance of his European career.

Championship Lineup (#19): nova • Misaj • ES Pierre • Necro

HaxBall Champions League #20: Smokin' Aces

In the following edition, nova was included in the Smokin' Aces squad for HaxBall Champions League #20.

He served as a reserve player during the campaign. Smokin' Aces ultimately won the competition, but nova did not play in the championship final and the title is therefore not counted among his individual HaxBall Champions League championships on this page.

Legacy

nova's historical profile is closely tied to a single unusual but important moment in HaxBall Champions League #19.

Originally expected to remain on the bench, he was required to replace the unavailable Pringles in goal for the final against Stiff Wind. He handled the unexpected assignment well enough for Angry Bulls to remain competitive and eventually complete its comeback.

His championship with Angry Bulls remains the main achievement of his HaxBall Champions League career, while his later place in the Smokin' Aces #20 squad represents a secondary chapter rather than an additional title credited to his record.
