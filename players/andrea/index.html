ANDREA was one of the leading attacking players of his generation during the Flash era. He represented Turkey at international level and reached major finals with Klub Emeryta.

His documented career includes two HaxBall Champions League final appearances, in Edition #14 and Edition #16, as well as an international championship with Turkey at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy.

His appearances across Editions #14 and #16 also place his documented club career across the transition from the late Flash period into HTML5 competition.

Biography

ANDREA's documented club career is closely associated with Klub Emeryta, one of the regular contenders in the HaxBall Champions League during this period.

He appeared in two European finals for the club, first entering the HCL #14 final as a second-half substitute before returning as part of the starting lineup in HCL #16.

At international level, ANDREA was part of Turkey's championship-winning squad at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy.

HaxBall Champions League Career

ANDREA reached two HaxBall Champions League finals with Klub Emeryta, finishing runner-up in Editions #14 and #16.

HaxBall Champions League #14

ANDREA was part of Klub Emeryta's first HaxBall Champions League final in Edition #14.

The starting lineup featured kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, with ANDREA entering the match during the second half.

Klub Emeryta faced Omertà in a closely contested final. The match eventually reached Golden Goal, where Deliric scored the decisive goal to give Omertà a 3–2 victory.

Starting Lineup (#14): kojo • Liebe • DrG • Echo
Second-half substitute: ANDREA
Final: Klub Emeryta 2–3 Omertà
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #16

Two editions later, ANDREA returned to the final with a very different Klub Emeryta lineup consisting of Per, kojo, ANDREA and aguero10.

Klub Emeryta made an excellent start against Stiff Wind. In the sixth minute of the first half, ANDREA found aguero10, who finished the opportunity to give the team a 1–0 lead.

aguero10 soon scored again to make it 2–0, but Stiff Wind responded with two quick goals before the break. The second half remained scoreless and the final moved into Golden Goal, where Catrice completed the comeback for Stiff Wind.

Final Lineup (#16): Per • kojo • ANDREA • aguero10
ANDREA: Assist for the opening goal
Final: Klub Emeryta 2–3 Stiff Wind
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

ANDREA represented Turkey in international competition and was part of the championship-winning Turkish lineup at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy.

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

▸ Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

ANDREA was also part of Turkey's championship-winning lineup at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, alongside Misaj, Liebe and Marble.

Turkey defeated the United Kingdom 3–1 in the quarter-finals and Russia 2–1 in the semi-finals before meeting the Netherlands in the final.

After falling behind early, Turkey recovered and completed the comeback to win the tournament.

Turkey: Misaj • Liebe • Marble • ANDREA
Result: 🏆 Winner

Legacy

ANDREA's documented record places him in major matches at both club and international level. He reached two HaxBall Champions League finals with Klub Emeryta and was part of Turkey's championship-winning Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy campaign.

His HCL #16 final also produced a documented individual contribution, with ANDREA creating the opening goal for aguero10 as Klub Emeryta came within one Golden Goal of the championship.
