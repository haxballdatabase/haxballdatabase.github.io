Bullas is a HaxBall player from Turkey regarded as one of the talented DMs of his generation. Playing primarily at DM, he has competed since 2016 and remained active in the competitive scene as of 2026.

Despite his ability and unusually long competitive career, Bullas' major-trophy record remained relatively limited compared with the length of his time in the game. His most important major success came at international level, where he won the 2021 Nations Cup with Turkey.

Bullas also reached two further major international finals. Turkey finished runner-up in the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup before returning to another final in Nations Cup 2022, where Romania won the tournament.

A distinctive part of Bullas' career has been his close association with his twin brother Buub. The two spent the majority of their HaxBall careers together, frequently playing for the same teams and remaining closely connected throughout their time in the competitive scene.

Biography

Bullas has been active in competitive HaxBall since 2016. Over the following decade, he remained part of the scene through several different periods of the game and continued competing into 2026.

He developed primarily as a DM and was regarded as a talented player in the position. His individual ability allowed him to remain competitive against strong opposition over a long period, even as the wider player base and competitive environment changed around him.

One of the more unusual aspects of Bullas' career is the contrast between his longevity and his trophy record. Despite competing since 2016 and possessing the level to remain involved in high-level HaxBall, his major achievements remained relatively limited compared with the length of his career.

Bullas did achieve domestic championship success in HaxLife, although the exact number of titles he won in the competition is not currently documented.

His strongest major results came internationally with Turkey. Bullas reached three major national-team finals across 2021 and 2022, winning one championship and finishing runner-up twice.

Partnership with Buub

One of the most recognizable elements of Bullas' career has been his connection with his twin brother Buub.

The brothers spent much of their competitive careers together and were frequently associated with the same teams and groups. Rather than following completely separate paths through the scene, Bullas and Buub became a long-standing pair whose HaxBall careers were closely connected.

Their partnership continued across different periods of the game and remained a recurring feature of Bullas' competitive career.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Despite his longevity and ability, Bullas did not achieve major success in the HaxBall Champions League and finished without a championship in the competition.

During his career, Bullas also received a HaxBall Champions League ban related to macro use. The incident became a controversial part of his competitive history.

The ban is treated as a separate event within his career rather than as an explanation for his lack of HaxBall Champions League success. Bullas' overall record in the competition remained limited despite the length of his wider competitive career.

International Career

Bullas represented Turkey at international level and reached three documented major national-team finals.

His strongest international period began in 2021, when he won the Nations Cup and reached another final in the Big Easy edition. The following year, Bullas returned to the Nations Cup final once again.

Nations Cup 2021: International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2021

Bullas achieved the most important major championship of his career when Turkey won the 2021 Nations Cup.

Turkey's lineup featured Vichoexe, Bullas, Kyrai and Altidore.

Their opponents Poland were represented by kojo, miso, Kise and frenkie.

Turkey won the tournament, giving Bullas his first major international championship and the most significant trophy of his competitive career.

Turkey: Vichoexe • Bullas • Kyrai • Altidore
Poland: kojo • miso • Kise • frenkie
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy: Another Final

Bullas reached another major international final with Turkey in the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup.

This time, Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Buub and Kamil. Their opponents were the Netherlands, represented by Lyreco, Hulk, veil and xSha.

The Netherlands won the tournament, leaving Bullas and Turkey as runners-up. It gave Bullas a second major international final appearance during 2021, following his championship earlier in the year.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Buub • Kamil
Netherlands: Lyreco • Hulk • veil • xSha
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2022: A Third International Final

Bullas returned to the Nations Cup final with Turkey in 2022, extending his run of major international final appearances.

Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Their opponents were Romania, represented by Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut.

Romania won the tournament, leaving Bullas and Turkey as Nations Cup 2022 runners-up. It became Bullas' third documented major international final.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Vichoexe • Kyrai
Romania: Modric • aMp • Al1s • Hakut
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Playing Style

Bullas played primarily as a DM.

He was regarded as a talented player in the position and possessed the level required to compete against strong opposition. His long competitive lifespan also demonstrated an ability to remain effective as HaxBall changed across different eras.

Bullas was also capable of performing effectively in other positions when required, giving him additional versatility beyond his primary DM role.

Rather than being defined by a large collection of trophies, Bullas' reputation was based more on the quality he showed as a player and his ability to remain part of the competitive scene for many years.

Legacy

The contrast between Bullas' longevity and his major-trophy record became one of the defining aspects of his career. Having competed since 2016, he spent many years at a competitive level without accumulating the extensive collection of major championships that his longevity might suggest.

Bullas can consequently be viewed as a player whose career produced less major success than his individual ability might have suggested. While talented enough to remain competitive for many years, that level did not translate into an extensive collection of major trophies.

His strongest major period came internationally. Bullas became a Nations Cup champion with Turkey in 2021, reached another final in the Big Easy edition during the same year and then returned to the Nations Cup final again in 2022.

Those results gave Bullas three documented major international final appearances, consisting of one championship and two runner-up finishes.

Although primarily a DM, Bullas was also capable of performing effectively in other positions when required, adding further versatility to his game.

Still active in 2026, a decade after beginning his competitive career, Bullas remains a notable example of a talented and long-serving DM whose reputation extends beyond the number of championships attached to his name.
