Splat is a HaxBall player best documented for his time with Deathwish during HaxBall Champions League #20.

He was part of the Deathwish lineup that reached the HCL #20 final against Smokin' Aces, playing alongside Abduction, Müllewa and Jéal.

Splat scored one of Deathwish's three goals in the final, finishing a move created by Müllewa to make the score 2–2. Deathwish later lost the match 4–3 through Golden Goal.

Biography

The current archive documents Splat primarily through Deathwish's HaxBall Champions League #20 campaign. Deathwish's surviving competitive history is concentrated around that edition, and Splat was one of the players associated with the team during its run to the final.

The available records do not currently provide enough verified information to establish Splat's nationality, regular playing position, wider club career or individual playing style without speculation.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #20

Splat reached the HaxBall Champions League #20 final with Deathwish.

The final lineup consisted of Abduction, Müllewa, Jéal and Splat. Their opponents were Smokin' Aces, represented by vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha.

After Smokin' Aces moved into a 2–1 lead, Deathwish responded through a transition led by Müllewa. Müllewa found Splat, who finished the move to equalize the final at 2–2.

Deathwish later moved 3–2 ahead in extra time through Jéal, but Smokin' Aces recovered. The match reached 3–3 before xSha scored the Golden Goal that completed a hat-trick and gave Smokin' Aces a 4–3 victory.
