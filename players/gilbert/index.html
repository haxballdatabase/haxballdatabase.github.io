Gilbert is a HaxBall player from Turkey regarded as one of the talented DMs of his generation. Primarily playing at DM, he established himself during the Flash era before continuing to compete at a strong level following the transition to HTML5.

Gilbert reached three HaxBall Champions League finals, winning the competition with Skins in Edition #12 before finishing runner-up in #15 with Skins and #19 with Stiff Wind. His three final appearances across different periods of the game reflected his ability to remain competitive at the highest level.

His career also included major international success with Turkey. Gilbert won the 2017 Nations Cup, later finished runner-up in the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup, and returned to another international final in Nations Cup 2022, where Turkey finished runner-up to Romania.

Although his overall trophy record does not fully reflect the level he maintained across both eras, Gilbert became particularly associated with Skins, where he served as captain, before later joining Stiff Wind and spending a long period with the club.

Biography

Gilbert established himself during the Flash era as a talented and successful DM. His strongest qualities allowed him to compete at the highest level of European HaxBall and become part of several major HaxBall Champions League campaigns.

The club most closely associated with his early career was Skins. Gilbert served as the team's captain and became one of the players most closely identified with the club during its competitive history.

His period with Skins produced both the greatest club success and one of the major disappointments of his career. Gilbert won the HaxBall Champions League in #12, before returning to the final with the club in #15.

Later in his career, Gilbert joined Stiff Wind and remained connected with the club for a long period. His time there extended his competitive career beyond his Skins years and eventually brought him to another HaxBall Champions League final.

His international career showed similar longevity. After becoming a Nations Cup champion in 2017, Gilbert returned to major finals in both 2021 and 2022.

Skins

Gilbert's career is particularly closely connected with Skins.

As captain, he became one of the central figures of the team and was part of both its championship success in HaxBall Champions League #12 and its return to the final in Edition #15.

The association between Gilbert and Skins became strong enough that the club forms an important part of his historical identity. Even after later moving to Stiff Wind, his championship and captaincy with Skins remained among the defining elements of his career.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Gilbert reached the HaxBall Champions League final three times, recording one championship and two runner-up finishes.

His first final ended with a championship for Skins in #12. He returned with the same club in #15, where Skins were defeated by Angry Bulls, before reaching another final years later with Stiff Wind in #19.

A notable feature of Gilbert's record is that his two final defeats both came against teams featuring Misaj, placing his later championship opportunities directly within one of the most successful periods of Misaj's career.

HaxBall Champions League #12: Champion with Skins

Gilbert won his first and only HaxBall Champions League championship in Edition #12 as part of Skins.

The final lineup consisted of Liéds, Gilbert, Elysion and Catrice.

Skins faced Pas De Nom in the final. The match became particularly memorable for Catrice, who scored all four Skins goals.

Skins won 4–1, giving Gilbert the HaxBall Champions League championship and establishing the greatest European achievement of his career.

Championship Lineup (#12): Liéds • Gilbert • Elysion • Catrice
Final: Skins 4–1 Pas De Nom
Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #15: A Second Final

Gilbert returned to the HaxBall Champions League final with Skins in Edition #15.

The Skins final lineup consisted of kojo, Gilbert, Vanessa and Altidore.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, whose lineup featured Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Angry Bulls took control of the final and eventually secured a 3–1 victory. Misaj provided two assists during the match, while Müllewa scored twice.

The defeat left Gilbert with a runner-up finish and denied Skins a second HaxBall Champions League championship.

Final Lineup (#15): kojo • Gilbert • Vanessa • Altidore
Final: Angry Bulls 3–1 Skins
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #19: Another Meeting with Misaj

By HaxBall Champions League #19, Gilbert was representing Stiff Wind, the club with which he would spend a significant period of his later career.

Stiff Wind reached the final attempting to win a record fourth HaxBall Champions League championship. Standing in their way were Angry Bulls.

Stiff Wind opened the scoring and took a 1–0 lead, placing the club in a strong position to secure another championship.

Angry Bulls responded when Misaj used his trademark X-fake to create the equalizer for Necro. The match eventually continued into extra time.

There, Misaj scored the winning goal to complete a 2–1 comeback. For Gilbert, it meant a third HaxBall Champions League final and a second runner-up finish.

International Career

Gilbert represented Turkey in international competition and reached three documented major international finals across different periods of his career.

He became a Nations Cup champion in 2017 before later returning to consecutive major international finals in 2021 and 2022.

Nations Cup 2017: International Champion

In 2017, Gilbert was part of the Turkish national team that won the Nations Cup.

The title gave Gilbert major success at both club and international level, complementing his HaxBall Champions League championship with Skins.

Team: Turkey
Competition: Nations Cup 2017
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2021 - Big Easy: Another International Final

Years after his 2017 championship, Gilbert returned to a major international final with Turkey in the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup.

Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Buub and Kamil. Their opponents were the Netherlands, whose lineup consisted of Lyreco, Hulk, veil and xSha.

The Netherlands won the tournament, leaving Gilbert and Turkey as runners-up. The campaign nevertheless gave Gilbert another major international final appearance several years after his Nations Cup championship.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Buub • Kamil
Netherlands: Lyreco • Hulk • veil • xSha
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Nations Cup 2022: Back-to-Back International Finals

Gilbert returned to another major international final with Turkey in the 2022 Nations Cup, giving him major final appearances in consecutive years.

Turkey lined up with Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Their opponents were Romania, represented by Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut.

Romania won the tournament, leaving Gilbert and Turkey as Nations Cup 2022 runners-up. For Gilbert, it represented his third documented major international final and his second consecutive runner-up finish.

Turkey: Bullas • Gilbert • Vichoexe • Kyrai
Romania: Modric • aMp • Al1s • Hakut
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

From Flash to HTML5

Gilbert's career extended across both the Flash and HTML5 eras.

His strongest period came during Flash, where he established himself as a talented DM, became a HaxBall Champions League champion with Skins and won the Nations Cup with Turkey.

Unlike some Flash-era players who struggled considerably after the game's transition, Gilbert remained a good competitive player in HTML5. He continued attempting to compete near the highest level and later reached another HaxBall Champions League final with Stiff Wind.

His international appearances provided further evidence of that longevity. Gilbert reached the 2021 Big Easy Nations Cup final and then returned to the 2022 Nations Cup final, years after his earlier Flash-era successes.

Playing Style

Gilbert played primarily as a DM.

During his Flash-era prime, he was regarded as a particularly talented player in the position and possessed the quality required to operate in championship-level teams.

His career also demonstrated longevity. Gilbert remained capable of competing at a strong level after the move to HTML5 and continued challenging for places among the better players in his position.

Rather than being remembered through overwhelming individual statistics, Gilbert's reputation came from his overall level, consistency and ability to remain useful within strong competitive teams across different periods of the game.

Legacy

Gilbert's career left behind the record of a talented and successful DM whose quality was not always reflected by the number of major championships he ultimately won.

His strongest association remains with Skins. As captain and HaxBall Champions League champion, Gilbert became one of the players most closely identified with the club.

His international career added another important dimension to that record. Gilbert won the 2017 Nations Cup with Turkey and later reached consecutive international finals in 2021 and 2022, finishing runner-up on both occasions.

His later years with Stiff Wind and Turkey demonstrated that his career was not limited to one team or one period of HaxBall. Gilbert remained competitive into HTML5 and continued challenging at a high level years after his original Flash-era peak.

Across his documented career, Gilbert reached six major finals: three in the HaxBall Champions League and three with the Turkish national team.

For this reason, Gilbert can be considered one of the most underrated DMs of his generation: a player with the ability to compete at the top level across both Flash and HTML5, but whose final trophy count only tells part of his career.
