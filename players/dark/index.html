Dark is a Portuguese HaxBall player from the Flash era. His documented competitive record includes a Nations Cup championship with Portugal and a run to the final of the HaxBall Champions League with Angels of Death.

Dark became a major international champion at Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy, where Portugal defeated Turkey in the final. He later appeared in the HaxBall Champions League #7 final and scored Angels of Death's only goal in the 4–1 defeat to Pas De Nom.

Biography

Dark competed during the Flash era and represented Portugal at international level. The current archive documents him through major appearances in both international competition and the HaxBall Champions League.

At club level, he appeared for Angels of Death in the HCL #7 final. At international level, he was part of the Portugal side that won Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #7

Dark reached the HaxBall Champions League #7 final with Angels of Death.

The Angels of Death final lineup consisted of Rai, R9, ROCKY BALBOA and Dark. Their opponents were Pas De Nom.

Pas De Nom moved into a 2–0 lead before Angels of Death responded during the second half. R9 and ROCKY BALBOA combined in the build-up before Dark finished the move, reducing the deficit to 2–1.

The comeback ended there. Pas De Nom restored its advantage and eventually won the final 4–1. Dark therefore finished the edition as runner-up while scoring Angels of Death's only goal in the championship match.

International Career

Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy

Dark represented Portugal at Nations Cup 2013 - Big Easy, where the team reached the final against Turkey.

Portugal lined up with Markogol, Wigas, Tortogol and Dark. Turkey's lineup consisted of Lev Yashin, barya, Whiplash and Doctore.

Portugal won the tournament, giving Dark a major international championship. The current archive does not preserve a confirmed final score or detailed individual events from the match.

Legacy

Dark's documented record connects him to two major Flash-era finals: a Nations Cup championship with Portugal and a HaxBall Champions League runner-up finish with Angels of Death.

His goal in the HCL #7 final remains his clearest preserved individual contribution in the current archive, while the 2013 Big Easy Nations Cup title stands as his major championship achievement.
