Biography

Adriano is an Israeli HaxBall player who played primarily as a goalkeeper.

His most notable recorded achievement came with Hellhounds, where he became a HaxBall Champions League champion in Edition #6.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #6: Champion with Hellhounds

Adriano won the HaxBall Champions League #6 as the goalkeeper of Hellhounds.

Hellhounds lined up with Adriano, aguero10, Stingox and Ced, facing Die Galaktischen in the final.

Hellhounds controlled the match and secured a convincing 4–0 victory, completing the championship campaign without conceding in the final.

Final: Hellhounds 4–0 Die Galaktischen

Legacy

Adriano is primarily remembered as the goalkeeper of the Hellhounds team that won HaxBall Champions League #6.

The championship placed him among the players from the Israel HaxBall community to win one of the game's major club competitions.
