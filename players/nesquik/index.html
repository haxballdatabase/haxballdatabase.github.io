Nesquik is a German HaxBall player from the earliest period of competitive HaxBall, best known for his time with Nerdlucks.

Playing as a DM, Nesquik was part of the Nerdlucks lineup that won the inaugural HaxBall Champions League.

Nerdlucks

Nesquik was a member of Nerdlucks during the beginning of the HaxBall Champions League.

In HaxBall Champions League #1, Nerdlucks lined up with Fredklop, Nesquik, aguero10 and Rene.

The team went on to win the competition, making Nesquik part of the first championship-winning lineup in HaxBall Champions League history.
