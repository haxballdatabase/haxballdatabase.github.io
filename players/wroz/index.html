wroz is a competitive HaxBall player best remembered for winning HaxBall Champions League #20 with Smokin' Aces.

Playing primarily as a DM, wroz was part of the championship lineup alongside vegeta, jasko and xSha. He also served as captain of Smokin' Aces and later led the team to the Feed-Me League Season 27 title.

A recurring partnership throughout this period was his connection with jasko. The pair had already played together for Trailer Park Penguins before continuing as teammates under the Smokin' Aces name, becoming a familiar duo across both stages of the team's history.

Unlike several more individually decorated players in the position, wroz's historical reputation is tied primarily to his contribution within successful teams rather than to major individual records or a claim among the game's greatest DMs.

Biography

wroz competed primarily as a DM and became most closely associated with Smokin' Aces during the later period of competitive HaxBall.

His most important competitive achievement came with the team's run in HaxBall Champions League #20. Smokin' Aces assembled a lineup consisting of vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha, eventually reaching the final and winning the championship.

Alongside the European title, wroz also experienced domestic success as captain of Smokin' Aces, winning the Feed-Me League in Season 27.

Trailer Park Penguins & Smokin' Aces

Before the Smokin' Aces championship period, wroz had already competed under the Trailer Park Penguins banner.

One of the players most consistently associated with him during this period was jasko. The two played together at Trailer Park Penguins and later continued their partnership with Smokin' Aces.

Their time together eventually included the biggest achievement of both players' Smokin' Aces period, with wroz and jasko forming part of the lineup that won HaxBall Champions League #20.

HaxBall Champions League Career

wroz's most notable appearance at the highest level of European competition came during HaxBall Champions League #20, when he won the tournament with Smokin' Aces.

HaxBall Champions League #20: Champion with Smokin' Aces

Smokin' Aces reached the HaxBall Champions League #20 final with a lineup of vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha.

Their opponents were Deathwish, who had reached the championship match after eliminating Angry Bulls in the semi-final.

The final developed into a dramatic contest. Jéal scored twice for Deathwish and later put his team 3–2 ahead in extra time, bringing Smokin' Aces close to defeat.

The match turned again when Jéal accidentally scored an own goal, bringing the score back to 3–3 and sending the championship into Golden Goal.

Smokin' Aces completed the comeback when xSha scored the decisive Golden Goal, securing a 4–3 victory and the HaxBall Champions League title.

Championship Lineup (#20): vegeta • wroz • jasko • xSha

Feed-Me League

wroz also achieved success with Smokin' Aces in domestic competition. Serving as the team's captain, he helped guide the club to the Feed-Me League Season 27 championship.

The title gave wroz another notable team achievement alongside his HaxBall Champions League victory and reflected his leadership role within Smokin' Aces.

Playing Style

wroz was primarily deployed as a DM. His role is best understood within the collective structure of the Smokin' Aces championship team rather than through exceptional individual statistics or a reputation as one of the position's all-time greats.

Smokin' Aces were particularly effective through a structured defensive approach and counter-attacking play. Within that system, wroz operated behind the team's more attacking players and formed part of the balance that allowed the lineup to compete successfully throughout HaxBall Champions League #20.

His value was also reflected through continuity and leadership. His long-running partnership with jasko, from Trailer Park Penguins into Smokin' Aces, gave the team an established connection within its core.

Legacy

wroz's place in competitive HaxBall history is closely connected with the Smokin' Aces championship team of HaxBall Champions League #20.

Although he is not remembered as one of the defining DMs in the history of the game, becoming a HaxBall Champions League champion remains a significant achievement.

His career also included the Feed-Me League Season 27 title as captain of Smokin' Aces, while his repeated partnership with jasko linked the Trailer Park Penguins and Smokin' Aces periods of his career.

As part of the lineup of vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha, he contributed to the biggest European achievement in Smokin' Aces history and finished the campaign as a HaxBall Champions League champion.
