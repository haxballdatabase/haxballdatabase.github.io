Necro established himself among the greatest strikers in HaxBall history, earning recognition for his elite finishing, exceptional passing speed and accuracy, and decisive performances in major matches.

While his finishing was one of the most recognizable parts of his game, Necro was equally notable for the speed and accuracy of his passing. He was capable of releasing the ball almost immediately after receiving it, allowing attacks to continue at a very high tempo and making him dangerous not only as a goalscorer, but also as a player who could create opportunities for teammates.

One of the most unusual aspects of Necro's career was that he played exclusively alongside Misaj throughout his competitive career. From club competition to the Turkish National Team, every major chapter of Necro's career was shared with his best friend and long-time teammate, making their partnership inseparable from his identity as a player.

His major club achievements included two HaxBall Champions League titles, first with Angry Bulls in Edition #19 and later with Pafküf in Edition #22. Necro also played a major role for the Turkish National Team, most notably during the National Team Cup 2019 final against Portugal, where he scored the late equalizer that forced extra time before Turkey completed a dramatic 5–2 comeback.

Biography

Necro established himself as one of the leading strikers of his era through a style built around efficient finishing and extremely quick ball movement. His ability to play at a high tempo made him particularly effective in teams that relied on fast combinations and immediate attacking transitions.

A particularly unusual feature of his career was its connection to Misaj. Necro never played competitively without him, with the two remaining teammates throughout Necro's entire career. From club competition to international tournaments, every major stage of Necro's career featured the pair playing alongside each other.

Their connection extended beyond competition. Necro and Misaj were best friends away from the game and developed a similarly close understanding on the pitch. They shared major successes with Angry Bulls, Pafküf and the Turkish National Team.

Their playing styles also complemented one another. Necro's finishing and rapid distribution worked particularly well alongside Misaj's ability to create openings and move the attack into dangerous areas, producing several important combinations in major matches.

Necro's career contained several moments where his finishing proved decisive. He scored Angry Bulls' equalizer in the HaxBall Champions League #19 final and later produced another crucial equalizer for Turkey in the National Team Cup 2019 final.

His passing was equally important to his game. Necro regularly moved the ball with very little delay, combining speed and accuracy to maintain attacking rhythm and create opportunities before opposing defenses could reorganize.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Necro won the HaxBall Champions League twice, claiming his first title with Angry Bulls in Edition #19 before becoming champion again with Pafküf in Edition #22. Misaj was his teammate during both championship campaigns.

HaxBall Champions League #19: The Equalizer That Changed the Final

Necro's first HaxBall Champions League title came with Angry Bulls in Edition #19.

The team reached the final after defeating Trailer Park Penguins in the semi-finals, setting up a championship match against Stiff Wind.

The final carried major historical importance. Stiff Wind held the competition record with three HaxBall Champions League titles, while Angry Bulls were attempting to win their third and move level with them.

Stiff Wind took the lead and put Angry Bulls 1–0 behind, forcing them to chase the match.

The response came through the Misaj-Necro connection. Misaj used his trademark "X-fake" to create space before finding Necro, who converted the chance to score the 1–1 equalizer.

The finish became an important example of one of Necro's strongest qualities. With very little time to react, he completed the move efficiently and brought Angry Bulls back into the final.

The match remained level and continued into extra time. There, Misaj scored the decisive goal to complete the comeback and give Angry Bulls the championship.

The victory gave Necro his first HaxBall Champions League title and brought Angry Bulls level with Stiff Wind on three championships.

Championship Lineup (#19): nova • Misaj • ES Pierre • Necro

HaxBall Champions League #22: Best Friends Reunited

Necro returned to the top of European competition in Edition #22 as part of Pafküf.

The campaign reunited him with Misaj, his best friend and the teammate who had accompanied him throughout his entire competitive career. Their relationship had long combined a close friendship away from the game with a strong understanding during matches.

The Pafküf lineup brought together Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro. For Necro and Misaj, the campaign represented another chapter in a partnership that had already produced major success for both club and country.

Their familiarity was valuable to a Pafküf side built around players who already understood each other's tendencies. Necro's quick combinations and direct attacking play gave the team another natural connection between midfield and attack.

Pafküf's campaign nearly ended in the quarter-finals against Invincible. The team fell 3–0 behind early, but recovered to complete a dramatic comeback and keep the tournament alive.

After surviving the quarter-final, Pafküf defeated Wicked Guys in the semi-finals and advanced to the final against Romanian side FEAR.

The final was controlled by Pafküf from the early stages. By halftime, the team had already established a commanding 3–0 lead.

Pafküf completed the championship run, giving Necro his second HaxBall Champions League title. The victory was another major success shared with Misaj and gave the two best friends a second HaxBall Champions League championship together.

Championship Lineup (#22): Knap • Misaj • Jéal • Necro

International Career

National Team Cup 2019: Delivering When It Mattered Most

Necro represented the Turkish National Team at the National Team Cup 2019 alongside Misaj, Marble and goalkeeper Liéds.

His speed and accuracy of passing were already visible earlier in the tournament. In the quarter-final against Romania, Necro produced a sharp pass that created the opening goal for Marble as Turkey went on to win 4–0.

Turkey later reached the grand final against Portugal, whose team featured players such as Pringles and SAKY.

Portugal took control of the final and built a 2–0 lead, leaving Turkey in serious danger of defeat.

Misaj pulled one goal back to make it 2–1, setting up a dramatic final period as Turkey pushed heavily for the equalizer.

With approximately one minute remaining, Misaj played the ball into a crowded attacking area and Necro reacted quickly, forcing the ball home to score the 2–2 equalizer.

The goal saved Turkey from defeat and sent the final into extra time, becoming one of the most important goals of Necro's career.

Turkey then took control. Marble scored to make it 3–2 before Necro again demonstrated the speed and accuracy of his passing. He released a sharp pass to Marble, who finished the move to extend the lead to 4–2.

Turkey eventually completed a remarkable 5–2 comeback victory. Necro had contributed both the goal that kept the team alive and an important assist during extra time, producing one of the clearest examples of his ability to influence a major match both as a finisher and as a creator.

Playing Style

Necro played primarily as a striker and was particularly renowned for two qualities: his finishing and the speed and accuracy of his passing.

His finishing was built around efficiency. Necro did not always require long possession or several touches before shooting, making him especially dangerous when attacking moves developed quickly or when chances appeared in crowded areas.

His equalizer in the HaxBall Champions League #19 final and his late goal in the National Team Cup 2019 final are two of the clearest examples of his ability to convert important chances under pressure.

What separated Necro from many traditional strikers was how quickly and accurately he could move the ball after receiving it. Rather than slowing attacks down, he was capable of releasing fast, precise passes almost immediately, allowing teammates to continue combinations before defenders had time to reorganize.

This quality made him effective beyond goalscoring. Necro could finish an attack himself, but he could also become the player who accelerated the move and created the final opportunity for someone else.

His understanding with Misaj further strengthened this part of his game. Having played alongside the same long-term teammate throughout his career, Necro developed an unusually familiar attacking relationship in which quick passes and immediate combinations could be played with very little hesitation.

The National Team Cup 2019 provided a clear example. After scoring Turkey's late equalizer in the final, Necro later produced a quick and accurate pass for Marble during extra time, helping Turkey extend its lead during the decisive phase of the comeback.

The combination of elite finishing with exceptional passing speed and accuracy gave Necro a distinctive attacking profile and helped establish him among the greatest strikers in the game's history.

Legacy

Necro's legacy is closely connected to his ability to influence matches from the striker position in more than one way. His finishing made him a constant threat around goal, while the speed and accuracy of his passing allowed him to play an important role in creating attacks as well.

Several of his most memorable moments came in major finals. He scored the equalizer that brought Angry Bulls back into the HaxBall Champions League #19 final and later scored another crucial equalizer for Turkey in the National Team Cup 2019 final.

Another unusual part of Necro's legacy is that he never played competitively without Misaj. Across Angry Bulls, Pafküf and the Turkish National Team, the two remained teammates throughout Necro's entire career.

Their relationship became one of the central themes of his career. Best friends away from the game and highly compatible teammates on the pitch, Necro and Misaj shared major championships at both club and international level, with many of Necro's most important moments occurring alongside the same long-time partner.

With two HaxBall Champions League titles, a National Team Cup championship and decisive performances in some of the biggest matches of his era, Necro established a lasting reputation as one of the greatest strikers in HaxBall history.
