Liebe is a Turkish HaxBall player regarded as one of the strongest DMs of his time, particularly during the Flash period.

His most important club achievements came across consecutive HaxBall Champions League editions. Liebe became European champion with Stiff Wind in Edition #13 before reaching another final with Klub Emeryta in Edition #14.

The second of those finals became one of the most memorable matches of his career. With Klub Emeryta trailing Omertà 2-1, Liebe scored the 2-2 equalizer that kept his team alive before the championship was eventually decided by Golden Goal.

Liebe also achieved major success with the Turkish National Team, winning the Nations Cup in both 2016 and 2017 before reaching another international final in 2018. These campaigns gave him two major national-team titles and a further runner-up finish in addition to his HaxBall Champions League success.

Biography

Liebe became one of the most highly regarded Turkish DMs of his period and was especially prominent during the Flash years of competitive HaxBall.

His career included appearances for two important European teams, Stiff Wind and Klub Emeryta, with both clubs taking him to a HaxBall Champions League final.

His first major European breakthrough came with Stiff Wind in Edition #13. Playing at DM behind ROCKY BALBOA and Altidore, Liebe became part of the lineup that delivered the club's second HaxBall Champions League championship.

In the following edition, Liebe reached another final, this time representing Klub Emeryta. The achievement gave him consecutive HaxBall Champions League final appearances with two different teams.

His career also included considerable international success with Turkey. Liebe became a two-time Nations Cup champion, winning the tournament in consecutive editions in 2016 and 2017.

While Liebe continued playing into the HTML5 period, he was unable to reproduce the same level of major success he had achieved during the Flash era, where he collected his most important titles and reached the peak of his competitive career.

Stiff Wind

Liebe became part of Stiff Wind during one of the club's championship periods.

Stiff Wind had already won the HaxBall Champions League in Edition #9, but the lineup changed considerably before its next championship run. For Edition #13, the team featured Liéds at GK, Liebe at DM, ROCKY BALBOA at AM and Altidore at ST.

Liebe occupied the central DM role within a lineup containing considerable experience and attacking quality. His place in the team positioned him between Liéds in goal and the ROCKY BALBOA-Altidore attacking partnership ahead of him.

The campaign ended with Stiff Wind becoming HaxBall Champions League champions for the second time.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Liebe reached two consecutive HaxBall Champions League finals, doing so with two different teams.

His first final ended with a championship for Stiff Wind in Edition #13. One edition later, he reached the final again with Klub Emeryta, coming extremely close to adding a second consecutive title.

The two campaigns became the defining European stretch of Liebe's career and established him as a major DM during one of the strongest periods of Flash competition.

HaxBall Champions League #13: European Champion

Liebe won the HaxBall Champions League with Stiff Wind in Edition #13.

The championship lineup consisted of Liéds at GK, Liebe at DM, ROCKY BALBOA at AM and Altidore at ST.

Stiff Wind faced a difficult semi-final against Gladiators. The match ended 1-0 after ROCKY BALBOA created the decisive opportunity for Altidore, sending the team into the championship match.

In the final, Stiff Wind faced Lux Aeterna.

The attacking partnership ahead of Liebe became decisive. Altidore assisted ROCKY BALBOA twice before later adding a goal himself as Stiff Wind secured a 3-1 victory.

The result made Liebe a HaxBall Champions League champion and gave Stiff Wind the second European title in the club's history.

Championship Lineup (#13): Liéds • Liebe • ROCKY BALBOA • Altidore

HaxBall Champions League #14: One Goal Away

One edition after becoming champion with Stiff Wind, Liebe reached another HaxBall Champions League final, this time representing Klub Emeryta.

Klub Emeryta began the final with kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, with ANDREA later entering during the second half.

Their opponents were Omertà, whose lineup consisted of Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

Klub Emeryta struck first through DrG late in the opening half, but Omertà responded after the break. Mamba scored twice to turn the match around and give Omertà a 2-1 lead.

Liebe then produced one of the defining moments of his career. Following a mistake by Pringles, Klub Emeryta regained possession and Liebe scored the 2-2 equalizer, keeping the championship alive.

Neither team could find a winner during the remaining regulation period or the first stage of extra time, pushing the final into Golden Goal.

The deciding moment eventually came through Deliric, whose Golden Goal gave Omertà a 3-2 victory.

Liebe therefore finished as runner-up, narrowly missing the opportunity to win consecutive HaxBall Champions League titles with two different clubs.

Final Lineup (#14): kojo • Liebe • DrG • Echo

International Career

Liebe also enjoyed major success with the Turkish National Team.

He became a two-time Nations Cup champion, forming part of two different Turkish championship lineups in consecutive editions.

The first title came in 2016 alongside Liéds, Linas and Müllewa. Liebe then remained part of Turkey's championship story in 2017, this time playing alongside Misaj, Marble and ANDREA.

Liebe returned to another major international final at the 2018 Nations Cup. Turkey reached the championship match against Portugal, but the final ended in a 3-2 defeat, leaving Liebe with a runner-up finish.

Winning the competition with two different national-team lineups and later reaching the 2018 final strengthened Liebe's career beyond his club record and made international success an important part of his achievements.

Nations Cup 2016 - Big Easy: First International Championship

▸ Nations Cup 2016 - Big Easy

Liebe won his first major international title with Turkey at Nations Cup 2016 - Big Easy.

The Turkish championship lineup consisted of Liéds, Linas, Liebe and Müllewa.

The victory gave Liebe his first Nations Cup championship and established him as part of a successful Turkish national-team period that would continue into the following edition.

Championship Lineup (2016): Liéds • Linas • Liebe • Müllewa

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy: Back-to-Back International Champion

▸ Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

Liebe was again part of the Turkish National Team at Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, this time alongside Misaj, Marble and ANDREA.

The tournament was played on the Big Easy map, creating a different positional structure from conventional competitive HaxBall.

Turkey defeated the United Kingdom 3-1 in the quarter-finals before overcoming Russia 2-1 in the semi-finals.

The final placed Turkey against a strong Netherlands side featuring players such as xSha and Hulk.

Turkey recovered after falling behind early and completed the comeback to win the tournament. The result made Liebe a back-to-back Nations Cup champion, giving him major international titles in both 2016 and 2017.

Championship Lineup (2017): Misaj • Liebe • Marble • ANDREA

Nations Cup 2018: Final Against Portugal

Liebe returned to the Turkish National Team for the 2018 Nations Cup, reaching a third consecutive major international final.

Turkey entered the final against Portugal with Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Portugal fielded Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot.

Turkey took the lead late in the first half when Müllewa scored to make it 1-0. Portugal equalized in the second half through Bot.

The final also included an unusual fair-play moment after Turkey conceded during a situation affected by ping. Portugal deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to restore the score to 2-2.

The decisive goal came when SAKY found Bot, who scored to give Portugal a 3-2 victory. Liebe therefore finished the tournament as runner-up.

Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Final: Portugal 3-2 Turkey
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Playing Style

Liebe played primarily as a DM and was regarded as one of the highest-quality players in the position during his time, particularly throughout the Flash period.

His place in high-level lineups reflected that standing. At Stiff Wind, he occupied the DM position between Liéds and an experienced attacking pair of ROCKY BALBOA and Altidore during a championship-winning European campaign.

Liebe was also capable of contributing directly in decisive moments further forward. His equalizing goal against Omertà in the Edition #14 final remains the clearest example, with the DM scoring under championship pressure to force the match deeper into extra time.

Legacy

Liebe is remembered as one of the strongest Turkish DMs of his time and a player whose most successful period produced major achievements at both club and international level.

His European career peaked across two consecutive HaxBall Champions League editions. He became champion with Stiff Wind in #13 and came within one decisive goal of another championship with Klub Emeryta in #14.

The Edition #14 final remains particularly important to his individual story. With Klub Emeryta facing defeat, Liebe scored the 2-2 equalizer that extended the match into extra time and eventually Golden Goal.

His international record added another major dimension to his career. Liebe won back-to-back Nations Cup titles with Turkey in 2016 and 2017, doing so as part of two different championship lineups, before reaching another final in 2018.

Across those achievements, Liebe collected three major championships: one HaxBall Champions League title and two Nations Cup titles, while also reaching another HaxBall Champions League final and finishing runner-up at Nations Cup 2018.

Although he continued competing into HTML5, Liebe's strongest major results remained connected to his earlier years. His championship with Stiff Wind, dramatic second final with Klub Emeryta and consecutive international titles with Turkey formed the core of his competitive legacy.
