LaggerMet is an Israeli competitive HaxBall goalkeeper who competed at both European club and international level.

Rather than being regarded as one of the defining stars of Israeli HaxBall, LaggerMet established himself as a reliable role player who contributed within successful team structures.

His major achievements include winning HaxBall Champions League #18 with The Elders and winning Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy with Israel.

His defining individual moment came in the Big Easy final against Portugal, where LaggerMet scored the decisive goal in extra time to secure the international championship for Israel.

Biography

LaggerMet represented Israel during the later competitive era of HaxBall and competed as a goalkeeper at both European club and international level.

His career was not defined by being the central star of the teams he represented. Instead, LaggerMet operated as a dependable member of competitive lineups and contributed to teams containing several established high-level players.

That role ultimately produced success on both major stages of his recorded career. He became a HaxBall Champions League winner with The Elders and later played a decisive role in an international championship for Israel.

HaxBall Champions League Career

LaggerMet's principal European achievement came with The Elders during HaxBall Champions League #18.

HaxBall Champions League #18: The Elders

The Elders reached the Grand Final with a lineup consisting of LaggerMet, P4ER, SAKY and Vak.

Their opponents were Seeders, one of the strongest teams of the period.

The final remained scoreless throughout regulation time before Seeders eventually broke the deadlock in extra time through Yannex.

The Elders responded through Vak to make the score 1–1, but Seeders regained the lead at 2–1.

With less than two minutes remaining, Vak created the opportunity for SAKY to score the 2–2 equalizer, sending the championship into Golden Goal.

The deciding period lasted only 15 seconds. SAKY scored again almost immediately, completing the comeback and giving The Elders a dramatic 3–2 victory.

Championship Lineup (#18): LaggerMet • P4ER • SAKY • Vak
Final: The Elders 3–2 Seeders

International Career

LaggerMet represented the Israeli National Team as a goalkeeper and was part of the squad that reached the final of Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy.

He was not regarded as one of the main stars of the Israeli generation, but remained a trusted member of a national-team lineup containing several established players.

Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy: The Championship Goal

Israel reached the final of Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy with a lineup of Yannex, Isco, P4ER and LaggerMet.

Portugal entered the final with Pringles, Ter, Taziek and SAKY.

The final developed into a difficult and closely contested match. Neither team was able to decide the championship during regulation time, sending the match into extra time.

The decisive opportunity arrived when Ter attempted to play the ball backwards. The touch instead left possession directly in front of LaggerMet.

LaggerMet immediately took advantage.

He converted the opportunity and scored the championship-winning goal, securing Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy for Israel.

Israel Final Lineup: Yannex • Isco • P4ER • LaggerMet
Result: 🏆 Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy Winner
Championship Goal: LaggerMet

Role and Playing Profile

LaggerMet's primary position was goalkeeper. For Israel, however, he was also used in a versatile role when required, allowing the national team to adjust its structure depending on the lineup and match situation.

His profile was therefore closer to that of a dependable role player than one of the principal stars of his national scene. His contribution was based on performing the responsibilities required by the teams he represented.

The Nations Cup 2019 - Big Easy final provided his clearest individual moment. When the decisive opportunity appeared in extra time, LaggerMet converted it and delivered the championship for Israel.

Legacy

LaggerMet's competitive record is highlighted by success at both European club and international level.

As a goalkeeper, he was part of the The Elders lineup that won HaxBall Champions League #18 and later represented Israel in another championship-winning team.

His career was not defined by being one of the dominant individual stars of his generation. Instead, LaggerMet was a reliable player who contributed within successful lineups and fulfilled the responsibilities given to him.

His championship-winning goal for Israel nevertheless gave him a major individual moment. In one of the most important matches of his international career, LaggerMet scored the goal that decided the tournament.

The combination of a HaxBall Champions League title and an international championship gives LaggerMet a notable place in Israeli competitive HaxBall history.
