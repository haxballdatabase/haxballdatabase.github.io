Fritz is a German HaxBall player from the Flash era, best documented for his time with YeS AllStars. He was part of the team's run to the final of HaxBall Champions League #11.

In the HCL #11 final, Fritz lined up alongside Caccapupù, Chro and DrG against Angry Bulls. YeS AllStars eventually finished as runners-up after a 5–2 defeat.

Fritz also contributed directly to one of YeS AllStars' goals in the final, finding Chro, who scored his second goal of the match to reduce the score to 4–2.

Biography

Fritz competed during the Flash era and was part of YeS AllStars, one of the prominent teams of that period. His clearest documented major tournament appearance in the current archive comes from the team's HaxBall Champions League #11 campaign.

That run placed Fritz in a final lineup with Caccapupù, Chro and DrG, giving him a recorded appearance in one of the major European finals of the Flash era.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #11

Fritz reached the HaxBall Champions League #11 final with YeS AllStars. The final lineup consisted of Fritz, Caccapupù, Chro and DrG.

Their opponents were Angry Bulls, represented by Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

YeS AllStars took the lead through a DrG assist for Chro, but Angry Bulls gradually took control of the match and moved into a 4–1 lead.

Fritz then contributed directly to YeS AllStars' second goal. He found Chro, who finished the chance for his second goal of the final and reduced the score to 4–2.

Angry Bulls scored once more before the end of the match, completing a 5–2 victory and leaving Fritz and YeS AllStars as HaxBall Champions League #11 runners-up.
