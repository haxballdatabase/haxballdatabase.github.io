Linas is a Turkish HaxBall player who played primarily as a DM during the Flash era. At his strongest period, he was regarded as one of the leading DMs of the competitive scene.

Known for an effective and uncomplicated style of play, Linas preferred simple decisions, quick passing and positional discipline rather than unnecessary individual actions.

His major achievements include winning HaxBall Champions League #11 with Angry Bulls and becoming an international champion with Turkey at the 2016 Nations Cup.

Biography

Linas established himself during the Flash era, playing primarily in the DM position.

During his strongest period, he became one of the leading players in his position within the active competitive scene. His reputation was built less around individual flair and more around his ability to play efficiently within a team structure.

His most important club success came with Angry Bulls, where he became part of the lineup that won the club's first HaxBall Champions League championship.

Linas also represented Turkey internationally and added another major championship to his career by winning the 2016 Nations Cup.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Linas' principal HaxBall Champions League achievement came in Edition #11 with Angry Bulls.

The championship also became an important moment in the history of the club, as it represented Angry Bulls' first HaxBall Champions League title.

HaxBall Champions League #11: The First Angry Bulls Crown

Angry Bulls reached the HaxBall Champions League #11 final with a lineup of Abduction, Linas, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

Their opponents were Yes Allstars, represented by Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

Yes Allstars opened the scoring when DrG found Chro, who finished the opportunity to give his team a 1–0 lead.

Linas contributed directly to the response. After combining with Müllewa, he played a through ball back into Müllewa's path, allowing the striker to finish and level the match at 1–1.

Angry Bulls became increasingly effective as the final progressed and eventually took control of the match.

The game finished 5–2, giving Linas his first HaxBall Champions League championship and securing the first European title in Angry Bulls history.

Championship Lineup (#11): Abduction • Linas • ES Pierre • Müllewa
Final: Angry Bulls 5–2 Yes Allstars
Result: 🏆 Winner

International Career

Nations Cup 2016: International Champion

Linas represented Turkey at the 2016 Nations Cup.

The Turkish championship lineup consisted of Liéds, Linas, Liebe and Müllewa.

Turkey went on to win the tournament, giving Linas a major international championship alongside his HaxBall Champions League success.

Turkey: Liéds • Linas • Liebe • Müllewa
Result: 🏆 Winner

Playing Style

Linas played primarily as a DM and was known for keeping his game simple and efficient.

Rather than attempting unnecessary individual moves, he generally looked for the simple and effective option. Quick passes, sensible positioning and efficient decision-making formed the foundation of his play.

His approach allowed him to help maintain his team's structure and keep possession moving without slowing the game down. He did not need to dominate the ball individually in order to have an influence on the match.

This style was particularly suited to the DM position, where his ability to make straightforward decisions and release the ball efficiently helped connect the deeper and attacking areas of his teams.

At his peak during the Flash era, this combination of simplicity and effectiveness helped establish Linas as one of the leading DMs of his period.

Legacy

Linas is remembered as one of the leading DMs of his period during the Flash era, with a style built around simplicity, positioning and efficient decision-making.

His career included major success at both club and international level, winning HaxBall Champions League #11 with Angry Bulls and the 2016 Nations Cup with Turkey.

Rather than being defined by flashy individual play, Linas' reputation came from his ability to perform the DM role effectively within strong teams and make consistently useful decisions on the ball.
