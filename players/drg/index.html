DrG is a HaxBall player from Romania who competed during the Flash era. Alongside Deliric, he was regarded as one of the leading Romanian players of his era.

At a time when Romania had a relatively limited presence at the highest level of competitive HaxBall, DrG became one of the country's most notable players.

His recorded career includes two HaxBall Champions League finals, with Yes Allstars in Edition #11 and Klub Emeryta in Edition #14, as well as a run to the 2017 Nations Cup final with Romania.

Biography

DrG emerged as one of the leading Romanian players of the Flash era.

Alongside Deliric, he stood out among the relatively small Romanian presence competing at the highest level during the period.

His career featured repeated appearances in major finals. DrG reached the HaxBall Champions League final with both Yes Allstars and Klub Emeryta, while also representing Romania in the 2017 Nations Cup final.

HaxBall Champions League Career

DrG reached two HaxBall Champions League finals during his recorded career, representing two different clubs.

His first came with Yes Allstars in Edition #11, before returning to the championship match with Klub Emeryta in Edition #14.

HaxBall Champions League #11: First European Final

DrG reached his first recorded HaxBall Champions League final in Edition #11 with Yes Allstars.

Yes Allstars lined up with Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG, facing Angry Bulls in the final.

The match began well for DrG's side. He found Chro with a pass, and Chro converted the opportunity to give Yes Allstars an early 1–0 lead.

Angry Bulls responded and gradually took control of the match. Their attack eventually produced a 5–2 victory, leaving DrG and Yes Allstars as runners-up.

Final: Yes Allstars 2–5 Angry Bulls
Final Lineup: Caccapupu • Fritz • Chro • DrG
DrG: Created Chro's opening goal
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #14: A Romanian Duel

DrG returned to the HaxBall Champions League final in Edition #14, this time representing Klub Emeryta.

Klub Emeryta began the final with kojo, Liebe, DrG and Echo, while ANDREA also appeared during the second half. Their opponents were Omertà, whose lineup featured Pringles, Deliric, aguero10 and Mamba.

The match created an unusual meeting between two of Romania's leading players of the era, with DrG and Deliric representing opposing teams in a major European final.

DrG opened the scoring late in the first half, giving Klub Emeryta a 1–0 lead. Omertà responded after the break as Mamba scored twice to turn the match around, before Liebe equalized for Klub Emeryta to make it 2–2.

The match remained level through the main period of extra time and moved into Golden Goal.

The championship was ultimately decided in a direct physical battle between DrG and Deliric. Deliric managed to finish the opportunity, scoring the Golden Goal and giving Omertà a 3–2 victory.

Final: Klub Emeryta 2–3 Omertà
Final Lineup: kojo • Liebe • DrG • Echo
DrG: Scored the opening goal
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

International Career

DrG also represented Romania at international level, reaching the final of the 2017 Nations Cup.

Nations Cup 2017: International Run

DrG represented Romania alongside elite, Deliric and Kappa during the team's run to the 2017 Nations Cup final.

Romania faced Turkey, whose squad featured Liéds, Gilbert, Vanessa, Marble and Misaj.

For a Romanian scene with a relatively limited presence at the highest level of international HaxBall during the era, reaching the Nations Cup final represented an important achievement.

Romania ultimately finished as runner-up, giving DrG a major international final appearance alongside his two HaxBall Champions League finals.

Legacy

DrG's recorded career is defined by his repeated presence in major finals, reaching two HaxBall Champions League finals and a Nations Cup final with Romania.

Although those appearances ended in runner-up finishes, DrG was directly involved in important moments in both of his Champions League finals, creating the opening goal in Edition #11 and scoring the opening goal himself in Edition #14.

His career is also closely linked with Deliric. The pair represented Romania together during the country's Nations Cup final run, while their HaxBall Champions League #14 meeting placed them on opposite sides of a final that was ultimately decided through their direct Golden Goal battle.
