Müllewa is widely regarded as one of the greatest strikers in HaxBall history and an Angry Bulls legend. One of the leading strikers of the Flash era, he later continued competing and scoring after the game's transition to HTML5.

Müllewa was famously obsessed with scoring goals. His game revolved around finding the net, and that relentless hunger for goals became one of the clearest traits of his career. He seemed to sense goals before chances had fully developed, often recognizing danger and finishing opportunities before others had reacted.

Throughout his career, Müllewa became known above all for his instinct in front of goal. He was capable of producing goals from situations that did not initially appear dangerous, often finding a finish before defenders or goalkeepers could fully react.

His goalscoring success was not limited to the HaxBall Champions League. Müllewa also enjoyed repeated success in major leagues such as HaxLife and Feed Me, winning league championships and finishing as a top scorer on multiple occasions.

His connection with Angry Bulls forms a major part of his career. Müllewa won the HaxBall Champions League twice with the club and reached another final after its later revival, finishing his career with two titles from three HaxBall Champions League final appearances.

Müllewa also achieved major success with Turkey. He won ESL 2017 with the national team before reaching the 2018 Nations Cup final, where he scored Turkey's opening goal against Portugal.

Biography & Early Career

Müllewa established himself during the Flash era as one of the most dangerous goalscorers in competitive HaxBall and became closely associated with Angry Bulls. His goals were central to the club's early success, and over time he came to be regarded as one of the greatest players in Angry Bulls history.

Müllewa also developed a strong on-pitch understanding with captain ES Pierre. The two repeatedly played together during Angry Bulls' successful periods and understood each other's game well, giving the team a reliable connection between its structure and attack.

By the time Angry Bulls began winning HaxBall Champions League titles, Müllewa was already regarded as one of the finest strikers of his generation. His goals, final performances and long association with the club established him as an Angry Bulls legend.

HaxBall Champions League Career

Müllewa reached three HaxBall Champions League finals during his career, winning the competition twice with Angry Bulls before later returning with the revived club for another final appearance.

HaxBall Champions League #11: The First Crown

HaxBall Champions League #11 became one of the most important tournaments of Müllewa's career. Angry Bulls reached the final against Yes Allstars, whose lineup consisted of Caccapupu, Fritz, Chro and DrG.

The final also produced an interesting individual battle. Caccapupu was regarded as one of the best goalkeepers of the period and was particularly known for his aggressive, high-pressure style. Rather than simply remaining deep, he frequently attempted to close attackers down early and disrupt them before they could create a clean shooting opportunity.

That made his matchup with Müllewa especially notable. Müllewa was himself regarded as one of the finest strikers of the era, giving the final a direct battle between one of the period's strongest goalkeepers and one of its most dangerous goalscorers.

Yes Allstars opened the scoring first. DrG found Chro with a pass, and Chro finished cleanly into the corner to put Angry Bulls 1–0 behind.

Müllewa soon provided the response. He combined well with Linas, who eventually played a through ball back into his path. Müllewa finished the move perfectly to make it 1–1.

The final developed into an unusual contest. Yes Allstars controlled more possession for periods of the match, but Angry Bulls consistently looked more dangerous when attacking.

In the second half, Müllewa's understanding with ES Pierre became increasingly important. Pierre and Müllewa combined in attack before Müllewa scored again, giving Angry Bulls a 2–1 lead.

Only a short time later, the roles were reversed. Müllewa played the ball inside and Pierre finished the opportunity to extend the lead to 3–1.

With Yes Allstars beginning to lose control, a mistake from Caccapupu gave Müllewa another opportunity. He immediately punished the error, scoring his third goal of the final and completing a hat-trick to make it 4–1.

Yes Allstars responded when Fritz found Chro, who scored his second goal to reduce the deficit to 4–2, but the comeback came too late.

The final goal again involved the direct Müllewa-Caccapupu battle. Following a spamming duel between the two, the ball eventually went in off Caccapupu for an own goal, completing a 5–2 victory for Angry Bulls.

Müllewa therefore finished his first HaxBall Champions League final with a hat-trick, one of the defining performances of his career.

Championship Lineup (#11): Abduction • Linas • ES Pierre • Müllewa
Final: Angry Bulls 5–2 Yes Allstars
Müllewa: Hat-trick
Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #15: Back on the Throne

Four editions after his first championship, Müllewa returned to the final with Angry Bulls in HaxBall Champions League #15.

The lineup now featured Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa.

In the final, Angry Bulls faced fellow Turkish side Skins. Müllewa once again delivered on the biggest stage, scoring two goals. His second arrived shortly after the first, quickly establishing a 2–0 lead for Angry Bulls.

Misaj provided two assists during the final, with his connection with Müllewa becoming one of the major features of Angry Bulls' attacking performance.

Angry Bulls eventually completed a 3–1 victory. The result gave Müllewa his second HaxBall Champions League title.

Championship Lineup (#15): Liéds • Misaj • ES Pierre • Müllewa
Final: Angry Bulls 3–1 Skins
Müllewa: 2 Goals
Result: 🏆 Winner

HaxBall Champions League #21: Payback Time

Several seasons later, Müllewa became involved in one of the most personal stories of the later HaxBall Champions League era.

Following the events surrounding HaxBall Champions League #20 and the subsequent shutdown of Angry Bulls, ES Pierre reunited with Müllewa and Abduction. The three decided to revive Angry Bulls and bring the club back for another campaign.

By that point, relations between Müllewa and Misaj had deteriorated badly. The revival of Angry Bulls by Pierre and Müllewa pushed the divide even further.

After Angry Bulls had been rebuilt, Misaj took control of Slappers and assembled a new team with a clear objective: to defeat Angry Bulls.

There was no guarantee that the teams would actually meet. Slappers still had to survive the tournament and reached the final after defeating reigning champions Smokin' Aces, while the revived Angry Bulls advanced through the other side of the competition.

Their eventual meeting in the HaxBall Champions League #21 final therefore became an extraordinary twist. Müllewa had been one of Angry Bulls' central figures during its championship history, while Misaj had once won titles alongside him. They now lined up on opposite sides.

Angry Bulls arrived with a perfect record in HaxBall Champions League finals, having won all three of their previous appearances.

Slappers, however, took control immediately. They opened a 3–0 lead within the first three minutes and eventually won the match 7–2.

The result handed Müllewa and Angry Bulls their first HaxBall Champions League final defeat.

For Müllewa, the final became the opposite end of a career story that had begun with Angry Bulls' first title in #11.

International Career

Müllewa represented Turkey at international level, winning ESL 2017 and later reaching the final of the 2018 Nations Cup.

ESL 2017: International Champion

Müllewa became an international champion with Turkey in ESL 2017.

The final of the tournament was played on February 3, 2018, with Turkey facing Italy.

Turkey fielded Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa. The lineup was notable for being the same four-player combination that had previously won HaxBall Champions League #15 with Angry Bulls.

Turkey defeated Italy 2–1, successfully winning the tournament and giving Müllewa a major national-team championship.

Turkey: Liéds • Misaj • ES Pierre • Müllewa
Opponent: Italy
Final: Turkey 2–1 Italy
Result: 🏆 Winner

Nations Cup 2018: Final Against Portugal

Turkey reached the championship match against Portugal with a lineup of Altidore, Liebe, Whiplash and Müllewa.

Portugal fielded Pring, Ter, SAKY and Bot.

Near the end of the first half, Müllewa opened the scoring, giving Turkey a 1–0 lead.

Portugal equalized during the second half following a misunderstanding that left the ball in front of Bot, who converted the opportunity to make it 1–1.

The final then produced an unusual fair-play moment. Turkey conceded another goal during a situation affected by ping. Portugal chose not to retain the resulting advantage and deliberately opened its goal, allowing Turkey to score and restoring the match to 2–2.

The decisive moment came later when SAKY found Bot with a pass. Bot scored to make it 3–2, securing the Nations Cup title for Portugal and leaving Müllewa and Turkey as runners-up.

Turkey: Altidore • Liebe • Whiplash • Müllewa
Portugal: Pring • Ter • SAKY • Bot
Final: Portugal 3–2 Turkey
Müllewa: 1 Goal
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

From Flash to HTML5

Müllewa's career was not limited to the Flash era. After establishing himself as one of the strongest strikers of that period, he continued competing following HaxBall's transition to HTML5.

The transition changed many aspects of high-level play, but Müllewa's ability to score remained central to his game. Even as the competitive environment changed, he continued to be recognized as a dangerous attacking player.

His longevity across both eras strengthened his reputation beyond his early Angry Bulls championships, showing that his goalscoring ability was not tied exclusively to one version of the game.

Playing Style

Müllewa's game was built around one quality above everything else: scoring goals.

He was not necessarily known for being the most agile player on the pitch. What separated him was his ability to recognize a scoring opportunity before it had fully developed. Situations that appeared harmless could suddenly become dangerous once Müllewa became involved.

Müllewa had an unusual instinct for where goals would come from. He often appeared to feel an opportunity before it fully existed, anticipating loose balls, rebounds, defensive mistakes and small openings that other players might not immediately recognize.

His finishing instinct meant that teams did not always need to constantly create ideal chances for him. With Müllewa in attack, goals were rarely a problem. He could turn loose balls, physical battles, unexpected rebounds or small positional mistakes into genuine scoring opportunities.

This mentality made him almost obsessive in his pursuit of goals. Across competitions such as HaxLife and Feed Me, Müllewa repeatedly competed for league championships while also finishing as a top scorer on numerous occasions.

His partnership with ES Pierre was particularly effective because their strengths complemented one another. Pierre's understanding of team structure and timing often connected naturally with Müllewa's instinct for occupying dangerous spaces and finishing attacks.

Legacy

Müllewa is remembered as one of the great goalscorers in competitive HaxBall history and one of the central figures of Angry Bulls.

His performances in the club's first two HaxBall Champions League championships were particularly important. The hat-trick in the #11 final helped deliver Angry Bulls' first title, while his two-goal performance in #15 played a major role in bringing the club back to the top.

The same Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa combination that won HaxBall Champions League #15 later represented Turkey in ESL 2017. Turkey's 2–1 final victory over Italy gave Müllewa a major international championship.

He returned to another major international final at the 2018 Nations Cup, scoring Turkey's opening goal against Portugal before eventually finishing runner-up after a 3–2 defeat.

His success extended beyond major finals. In leagues including HaxLife and Feed Me, Müllewa won championships on multiple occasions and repeatedly finished among the leading goalscorers.

His long partnership with ES Pierre became another important part of Angry Bulls history. Their understanding on the pitch repeatedly appeared during the club's most successful periods.

Across the Flash and HTML5 eras, his instinct for goals remained the foundation of his reputation. Müllewa did not simply wait for obvious chances. He often seemed to sense where a goal could appear before the opportunity was fully visible.

With two HaxBall Champions League titles, an ESL championship, major international final appearances, multiple league championships and numerous top-scorer performances, Müllewa remains one of the most accomplished strikers of his generation.
