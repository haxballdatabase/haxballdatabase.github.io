Misaj is a Turkish HaxBall player regarded as the greatest HaxBall player of all time and was voted the 👑 No. 1 player in HaxBall history by its most prominent figures.

He holds the record for the most HaxBall Champions League titles with 5 championships, alongside 5 National Team Cup titles. Across his major international career, Misaj reached 10 major finals and won all 10, maintaining a perfect record on the biggest stages.

What made Misaj stand out was the sheer quality and elegance of his play. Primarily playing as a DM, he developed a style built around extraordinary technique, quick movements and an ability to make difficult actions look almost effortless. His gameplay became one of the most visually impressive styles of his era.

His influence did not end with his own generation. Many new-generation HaxBall players have looked up to Misaj and taken inspiration from the way he played. The appeal was not simply what he achieved, but how he made the game look while doing it, turning highly technical plays into something that appeared natural and effortless.

His career spans both the historic Flash era and the modern HTML5 era of competitive HaxBall.

Biography

Misaj emerged as one of the defining figures of competitive HaxBall during the Flash era. As his reputation grew, he became a regular presence at the highest level of international competition and began building the career that would eventually make him one of the game's most decorated players.

His Move to Angry Bulls

Four seasons after their first title, Angry Bulls brought Misaj into the team. His arrival added another major figure to the club's history, while also marking the beginning of what would become an extraordinary career.

On August 16, 2018, Misaj officially joined Angry Bulls, beginning a new chapter in his career. The impact was immediate. With Misaj joining the squad, Angry Bulls went on to win HaxTurkey, the active Turkish league at the time, securing the championship with two matches still remaining in the play-offs. They also lifted the League Cup, giving Misaj a trophy-winning start to his Angry Bulls career in his very first season.

What initially appeared to be another important addition to the squad would ultimately become a move that changed the future of the club. Misaj's arrival would play a major role in reshaping Angry Bulls and setting the stage for one of the most remarkable careers in competitive HaxBall.

Champions League & International Career

With 5 HaxBall Champions League titles and 5 National Team Cup titles, Misaj's international career remains unrivaled. His runs are defined by high-stakes victories, historic rivalries, and decisive individual performances.

HaxBall Champions League #15

At HaxBall Champions League #15, Angry Bulls returned to Champions League glory after a four-edition gap. Four seasons after their first title, the club had brought Misaj into the team, with ES Pierre continuing to provide leadership as captain.

Before reaching the final, Angry Bulls faced Chimera in the semi-finals. The opposing side featured established names including aguero10 and Fredklop. The tie proved highly competitive, with Angry Bulls narrowly advancing by a 3–2 aggregate score to secure their place in the final.

In the final against Turkish side Skins, Misaj and Müllewa formed a highly effective partnership. Misaj provided two assists, while Müllewa scored two goals, with the second goal arriving almost immediately after the first and giving Angry Bulls a decisive 2–0 lead.

Angry Bulls eventually won the final 3–1, securing their second Champions League title. The championship lineup was Liéds, Misaj, ES Pierre and Müllewa. For Misaj, it was the first Champions League title of his career.

Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy: The Start of National Glory

▸ Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy

Following his initial triumphs in the Champions League, Misaj was appointed captain of the Turkish National Team. Heading into Nations Cup 2017 - Big Easy, he made a bold strategic adjustment tailored specifically for the tournament's map mode. On Big Easy, the goalkeeper (GK) plays an exceptionally active role in build-up play and ball distribution—frequently touching the ball more than a traditional DM. Recognizing this strategic nuance, Misaj placed himself in goal to dictate play from the back. The core national squad consisted of Misaj, Liebe, Marble, and Andrea.

In the quarter-finals, Turkey squared off against a formidable United Kingdom side featuring GQKU, Splat, Flash, and Maddude. Outplaying their opponents, Turkey secured a decisive 3–1 victory. The semi-finals brought a hard-fought battle against Russia. In a tightly contested match, Misaj delivered a crucial assist for Andrea to break the deadlock, before Marble struck in the second half to guarantee a 2–1 win.

The grand final saw Turkey face a star-studded Netherlands team featuring international heavyweights like xSha and Hulk. After falling behind 1–0 early on, Misaj stepped up as captain, providing the game-changing assist for the 1–1 equalizer. The moment revitalized Turkey, propelling them to a monumental comeback victory over Holland. The title added another major international championship to Misaj's record during the historic Flash era.

ESL Final 2017: Defending the Crown

On February 3, 2018, Misaj produced one of the most astonishing individual performances of his entire career during the grand final of ESL 2017. Organised alongside the Nations Cup 2017 era, this edition held immense historic value as the final tournament ever hosted by ESL. Turkey entered the competition as defending champions following their 2015 victory, but fielded a drastically reformed lineup. With a brand-new roster, all eyes and immense external pressure rested squarely on Turkey's shoulders.

The final pitted Turkey against a notoriously tough Italy side known for their aggressive playstyle and immense spamming power. Despite the intensity of the match, Misaj delivered a complete masterclass on the pitch, effortlessly dismantling Italian defenders at will. It was rare for a defensive midfielder (DM) to exhibit such extraordinary faking and dribbling flair, leaving opposing players entirely helpless.

Turkey secured a thrilling 2–1 victory, successfully defending their ESL title and keeping the championship trophy in Turkish hands. Misaj's breathtaking individual dominance on the night stood out as a defining display of his generational talent.

HaxBall Champions League #19

At HaxBall Champions League #19, Misaj secured his second individual Champions League title with Angry Bulls in a blockbuster final against their fierce Turkish rivals, Stiff Wind—the most successful club in the tournament's history at the time.

Trailing 1–0 in a high-stakes encounter, Misaj produced a legendary clutch performance (1 goal, 1 assist) to turn the tide. He first executed his signature "X-fake" to set up Necro for the crucial equalizer, before stepping up in extra time to score the winning goal himself, sealing the championship in unforgettable style.

The comeback victory carried immense historical significance. It secured Angry Bulls' third Champions League trophy, bringing them level with Stiff Wind at the top of the all-time leaderboard. By defeating the competition's most decorated titan directly in the final, Angry Bulls not only conquered their greatest rival but also caught the record-holders to establish themselves as 3-time Champions League champions.

Nations Cup 2019: The Return

After a period in which the Turkish national team had struggled to secure titles and maintain its former international standing, Misaj returned to reclaim the captaincy for Nations Cup 2019—determined to restore the nation to the top of global competition. Turkey fielded a lineup built on long-standing synergy: captain Misaj played alongside his trusted long-time partner Necro and Marble, with Liéds anchoring the team in goal.

In the grand final, Turkey faced a formidable Portugal team boasting top-tier international stars such as Pringles and SAKY. Portugal's aggressive and disciplined setup caught Turkey off guard. Late in the first half, Portugal broke the deadlock to make it 1–0, and early in the second half, they quickly struck again to take a commanding 2–0 lead. With Turkey looking sluggish and out of form, victory seemed well out of reach.

However, Misaj delivered one of the most memorable grand final masterclasses of his career. Refusing to yield, classic Misaj stepped up and took complete command of the pitch, pulling a goal back himself to bring the score to 2–1 and reigniting Turkey's hopes.

The climax arrived in the dramatic closing minutes. Turkey launched a relentless assault, pressing Portugal deep into their own half. With just 1 minute remaining on the clock, Misaj delivered a brilliant pass into a chaotic goalmouth, where Necro scrambled the ball home for an unbelievable equalizer. The last-minute miracle sent commentators and fans into a absolute frenzy, forcing the match into extra time at 2–2.

In extra time, a fully locked-in Misaj maintained total dominance, elevating both his individual play and his team's spirit. Turkey had found their peak rhythm, and Marble scored the go-ahead goal to make it 3–2. With Portugal completely collapsing under the pressure, Misaj capped off his legendary performance in the final seconds with another assist to Necro, sealing a breathtaking 5–2 victory. The miracle turnaround stands as one of the greatest and most historic comebacks in HaxBall history.

HaxBall Champions League #20

HaxBall Champions League #20 marked a major turning point in the history of Angry Bulls.

During the semi-final against Deathwish, Angry Bulls goalkeeper Liéds experienced a severe ping problem which resulted in a goal being conceded. Under normal circumstances, Angry Bulls expected their opponents to respond with fair play, but Deathwish continued the match.

The situation became particularly significant because the Deathwish lineup included former Angry Bulls players Müllewa and Abduction, as well as Jéal, one of Misaj's closest friends.

Despite Misaj producing a remarkable individual performance of 3 goals and 1 assist, Angry Bulls lost the semi-final.

Misaj's Revenge

Approximately one to two months after the fall of Angry Bulls, ES Pierre decided to revive the team together with Müllewa and Abduction. However, the controversy of HCL #20 had drawn a clear line in the sand, and Misaj had no intention of returning to his former banner.

Fueled by a relentless desire for redemption and vengeance, Misaj chose a different path. He took over Slappers and assembled a powerhouse roster featuring Norbigo, Vichoexe, and Marble. His mission was singular and clear: to dismantle his former teammates and prove his absolute dominance.

The climax of this personal crusade arrived at HaxBall Champions League #21. After edging out defending champions Smokin Aces 1–0 in extra time in the semi-finals, Slappers faced the newly reformed Angry Bulls in the grand final. Angry Bulls had never lost a Champions League final in their history, but Misaj was about to rewrite that record in ruthless fashion.

Slappers unleashed an unprecedented storm from the opening whistle, racing to a 3–0 lead within the first three minutes. Misaj orchestrated a masterclass on the pitch, completely overwhelming Angry Bulls and driving Slappers to an astonishing 7–2 victory.

The historic rout handed Angry Bulls their first-ever Champions League final defeat and cemented the match as one of the most iconic events in the history of competitive HaxBall—a masterclass in revenge that reshaped the community forever.

Returning Home: Unfinished Business

Having fully accomplished his mission of vengeance with Slappers, Misaj sought to return to his roots. Before joining Angry Bulls back in Season 15, Misaj had displayed outstanding individual performances under the Pafküf banner, and it was only the eventual disbandment of Pafküf that originally paved the way for his historic transfer to Angry Bulls.

Having always maintained a deep emotional connection with Pafküf, driven by this strong bond and unfinished business, he reunited with his former home and long-time companions for a new Champions League campaign built on loyalty, deep friendship, and unmatched chemistry.

The roster brought together Necro—arguably Misaj's closest friend and one of his most compatible partners on the pitch—alongside Jéal. However, the team's most surprising decision came in goal. Instead of selecting a natural goalkeeper, Misaj placed full trust in Knap, an outfield player. Believing in Knap's raw talent and adaptability, Misaj designated him as the starting GK, a bold tactical move that stunned many observers.

While the squad entered the tournament with a singular focus on winning the championship, they were not widely considered the title favorites by external observers. However, Pafküf turned the tournament into a statement of pure dominance.

The tournament provided immediate drama in the quarter-finals against Invincible. Falling behind 3–0 early on, Pafküf looked to be on the brink of elimination. Yet, showing incredible resilience, the team launched a heroic comeback to win the match—a turning point that proved to be the single most crucial stepping stone to the trophy.

After overcoming domestic rivals Wicked Guys in the semi-finals, Pafküf advanced to the grand final to face FEAR, one of the most formidable Romanian powerhouses of the era. Despite FEAR's status, Pafküf's flawless team synergy and Misaj's accustomed authority dismantled the opposition. By halftime, Pafküf already held a commanding 3–0 lead. Misaj captivated the audience once again with a dominant 1 goal and 2 assists performance in the final, securing his 4th Champions League crown.

HaxBall Champions League #23: Misaj's Treble

Following back-to-back Champions League triumphs with Slappers (HCL #21) and Pafküf (HCL #22), Misaj embarked on his next chapter by joining Flaviu 1997. Originally a local Romanian club, founder and captain Flaviu transformed the organization into an international powerhouse, surrounding Misaj with top-tier talent: PiperSalvia, SAKY, and Marble.

The tournament's defining trial arrived in the semi-finals against aMp's FEAR, the formidable Romanian squad and HCL #22 finalist who had previously fallen to Misaj during his Pafküf era. Returning in Season 23 hungrier, more experienced, and determined to rewrite history, FEAR put up a fierce battle. Yet their ambitions crumbled against Misaj's relentless dominance. Standing as an insurmountable TITAN on the Champions League stage, Misaj shattered FEAR's redemption arc once more, propelling Flaviu 1997 straight into the Grand Final.

🎥 Semi-Final Record: FEAR

Flaviu 1997 fulfilled its potential by capturing the HaxBall Champions League #23 trophy, securing their first and only Champions League title. But the true story was Misaj himself. While rosters, teammates, and club badges constantly shifted over three seasons, one variable remained absolute: Misaj.

By carrying three completely different teams to back-to-back-to-back Champions League glory, he completed a personal Treble, proving that victory was not tied to a club, but to him. This historic run solidified his career total at an all-time record of 5 Champions League titles, marking one of the most dominant eras in HaxBall history.

The Mindset of a Winner

A defining element of Misaj's career was his unprecedented record in major finals: 10 major finals reached, and 10 major finals won. Across his entire career, he never lost a single major final match on the international stage.

While already a dominant force by nature, Misaj was particularly renowned for his ability to completely dictate and elevate his play in final matches. Far from being burdened by high-stakes pressure, he genuinely relished the atmosphere of grand finals, actively preferring the magnitude of championship matches over routine play. He possessed a rare capacity to make elite competition look effortless, routinely rendering formidable opponents look like ordinary players on the big stage. Peerless in terms of raw talent, he approached grand finals with supreme composure—treating them not as nerve-wracking tests, but as stages built for his absolute dominance.

Whether orchestrating a 7–2 demolition in HCL #21, delivering an extra-time winner in HCL #19, guiding the national team through high-stakes Nations Cup finals, or putting on a masterclass in HCL #22, his big-game performance remains unmatched. With an unbroken string of championship victories across both club and international levels, Misaj stands as arguably one of the most prolific winning figures in competitive gaming history.

Feed-Me & Domestic League Dominance

Although Misaj spent a relatively short period competing in the Feed-Me League, his time in the competition produced an exceptional collection of individual records.

Misaj achieved 5 Division 1 Assist Leader titles in the Feed-Me League, including an extraordinary run of three consecutive assist titles across Seasons 27, 28, and 29. Despite spending a comparatively short period in the league, he became the all-time record holder for the most Assist Leader titles in Feed-Me League history. He also holds the all-time record for the Most Assists in a Single Match, setting the benchmark with 11 assists in one game.

For a player whose natural role was Defensive Midfielder (DM), winning five Division 1 assist crowns was an extraordinary statistical outlier and one of the clearest examples of how different Misaj's playmaking profile was from that of a conventional DM.

Beyond his playmaking supremacy, Misaj proved to be an unstoppable offensive weapon during a brief stint as a forward. He won back-to-back Top Scorer titles in two consecutive seasons. During this run, he shattered the Flash era single-season goal record with 44 goals—the highest tally ever recorded in a single season throughout the entire Flash era.

Remarkably, even during his record-breaking top-scoring campaigns, Misaj did not play exclusively upfront. In several matches, he lined up at his natural Defensive Midfielder (DM) position and repeatedly scored from deep, proving that his goalscoring potency was a product of unmatched individual mastery rather than positional reliance.

Alongside Feed-Me, Misaj also collected numerous domestic league titles across major Turkish platforms like HaxLife and various local competitions throughout his career. While securing domestic league trophies stands as a career-defining peak for most players, for Misaj, winning local leagues became almost a formality—achievements so routine that they naturally remained secondary next to his monumental international hardware.

Playing Style

Misaj was primarily deployed as a DM, but his reputation was never defined simply by his position. What separated him was an exceptional level of technique and a style that allowed him to make difficult actions look remarkably natural.

His movements were quick, fluid and difficult to read, giving his gameplay a distinctive visual quality. Rather than appearing forced or overly complicated, even his most impressive actions often looked effortless, which became one of the main reasons his style attracted such a large following.

The "X-fake" became one of the techniques most closely associated with his name. Misaj was already performing the same movement during the Flash era, before it later became associated with the mechanics of the HTML5 version of the game.

Achievements

Misaj's major career record includes 5 HaxBall Champions League titles and 5 National Team Cup titles. He also reached 10 major finals and won all 10, giving him a perfect record in the most important matches of his career.

Legacy

Misaj's legacy is defined not only by his extraordinary collection of trophies, but by the way his presence shaped competitive HaxBall across different eras. He remained one of the game's most influential figures through the transition from Flash to HTML5, continuing to succeed at the highest level.

His 5 Champions League titles, 5 National Team Cup titles and perfect 10-for-10 record in major finals place him at the top of the historical record books. His victories with Skins, Angry Bulls, Slappers, Pafküf, and Flaviu 1997, together with the events surrounding HCL #20 and the legendary HCL #21 final, form some of the defining chapters of his career.

Beyond his unmatched mental fortitude, Misaj possessed a level of raw, innate mastery that set him apart. Put simply, Misaj could execute anything HaxBall physically provided to a player. He was not merely a master of tactics and mindset; his technical control, creativity and ability to exploit the game's mechanics made him one of the most complete players in competitive HaxBall history.

With his longevity, achievements and lasting influence on the way elite HaxBall could be played, Misaj is regarded as HaxBall’s greatest player of all time.
