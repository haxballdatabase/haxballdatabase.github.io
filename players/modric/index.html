Biography

Modric competed at a high level in both club and international HaxBall. His documented club record includes a run to the HaxBall Champions League #22 final with FEAR, while his international career produced a major championship with Romania in 2022.

Together, those results give Modric major-final appearances at both European club and national-team level, although the current archive still contains limited information about the wider details of his career.

HaxBall Champions League Career

HaxBall Champions League #22: Finalist with FEAR

Modric was part of FEAR during the club's strongest documented Champions League campaign, which ended with a place in the HaxBall Champions League #22 final.

In the final, FEAR faced Pafküf, whose lineup consisted of Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro.

Pafküf took control of the match in the first half and went into halftime with a 3–0 lead. FEAR was unable to recover after the break, and Pafküf completed a 4–0 victory.

The result left Modric and FEAR as HaxBall Champions League #22 runners-up.

International Career

Nations Cup 2022: International Champion

Modric was part of the Romania lineup that reached the Nations Cup 2022 final against Turkey.

Romania fielded Modric, aMp, Al1s and Hakut. Turkey's lineup consisted of Bullas, Gilbert, Vichoexe and Kyrai.

Romania won the tournament, giving Modric a documented Nations Cup championship.
