vegeta is a competitive HaxBall goalkeeper best known for his short but successful spell with Smokin' Aces, where he won HaxBall Champions League #20.

Unlike many players documented across several seasons, vegeta's competitive career was particularly brief. Despite playing for only a limited period, he produced an impressive season in goal and became an important part of one of Smokin' Aces' most successful lineups.

His strongest statistical achievement came in the Feed-Me League, where he finished as the season's clean sheet leader with 14 clean sheets in 22 matches. With the majority of his league appearances ending without conceding a goal, vegeta provided Smokin' Aces with a reliable presence behind the team's defense.

vegeta was primarily a goal-line goalkeeper. Rather than involving himself heavily in build-up play, his focus was much more direct: staying close to his goal, protecting the net and preventing the opposition from scoring.

Although he is not generally placed among the greatest goalkeepers in HaxBall history, his performances during this period established him as a good goalkeeper whose short career included both strong domestic numbers and a HaxBall Champions League title.

Biography

vegeta's competitive HaxBall career was unusually short compared with many players from the same period. His most notable spell came with Smokin' Aces, where he played as the team's goalkeeper.

Despite effectively being a one-season player, vegeta managed to combine strong league performances with success in European competition. His season with Smokin' Aces included both an excellent clean sheet record in Feed-Me and victory in HaxBall Champions League #20.

This gave his career an unusual profile: limited longevity, but a considerable amount of success within the period in which he competed.

Feed-Me League

vegeta's strongest individual statistical achievement came during his season with Smokin' Aces in the Feed-Me League.

Across a league season consisting of 22 matches, vegeta recorded 14 clean sheets, finishing as the competition's clean sheet leader. This meant that Smokin' Aces completed the majority of their league matches without conceding a goal with vegeta in goal.

For a goalkeeper whose competitive career lasted only a short period, the run was particularly notable. Rather than building his reputation through several seasons of sustained play, vegeta produced his strongest numbers almost immediately.

HaxBall Champions League Career

vegeta's major European achievement came in HaxBall Champions League #20, where he represented Smokin' Aces as goalkeeper.

The championship lineup consisted of vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha. With wroz and jasko providing structure through the middle and xSha operating as the team's main attacking threat, vegeta completed the lineup from goal.

HaxBall Champions League #20: Champion with Smokin' Aces

Smokin' Aces reached the final against Deathwish, completing vegeta's first major HaxBall Champions League campaign with an opportunity to win the title.

The final became a long and dramatic contest. Deathwish's Jéal scored twice before later putting his team 3–2 ahead in extra time.

Smokin' Aces remained alive when Jéal accidentally scored an own goal to make it 3–3, extending the match into Golden Goal.

The deciding moment eventually came through xSha, who scored the Golden Goal to complete a 4–3 victory. The result made vegeta a HaxBall Champions League champion in what became the defining tournament of his brief career.

Championship Lineup (#20): vegeta • wroz • jasko • xSha

Playing Style

vegeta was a traditional goal-line goalkeeper. His game was centered around protecting the goal rather than becoming heavily involved in possession or helping his team construct attacks from the back.

His priorities were straightforward: maintain his position, remain close to the goal and make it as difficult as possible for opponents to score. He generally preferred the safer defensive option rather than taking unnecessary risks with the ball.

This approach fit naturally into Smokin' Aces' organized defensive structure. The team's disciplined shape allowed vegeta to concentrate on his main responsibility, while his 14 clean sheets in 22 Feed-Me matches demonstrated how effective that formula could be over the course of a league season.

His career was too short to build the longevity or broader record associated with the game's greatest goalkeepers. However, during the period in which he competed, vegeta proved to be a dependable goalkeeper who was particularly effective at the most fundamental part of the position: keeping the ball out of the net.

Legacy

vegeta's career is remembered less for longevity and more for what he achieved within a very limited period of competitive play.

In a single season, he recorded 14 clean sheets across 22 Feed-Me matches and finished as the league's clean sheet leader, while also becoming a HaxBall Champions League champion with Smokin' Aces.

His traditional goal-line approach suited a team built around defensive organization. Rather than attempting to influence matches through elaborate build-up play, vegeta concentrated primarily on keeping his goal protected.

Although his short career does not place him alongside the most accomplished goalkeepers in HaxBall history, his performances showed that he was a good and effective goalkeeper during the period in which he competed.

His place in the historical record therefore comes from a brief but successful run: strong domestic goalkeeping numbers combined with membership of the vegeta, wroz, jasko and xSha lineup that won HaxBall Champions League #20.
