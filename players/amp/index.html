aMp is a Romanian HaxBall player and captain of FEAR, regarded as one of the leading DMs of his generation.

As one of the central figures of the Romanian competitive scene, aMp has led FEAR through several successful campaigns, including a run to the HaxBall Champions League #22 final and another deep campaign in Edition #23.

His career has also included international success with Romania. aMp reached the final of Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy, where Romania finished runner-up to Israel.

Still active in 2026, aMp continues to compete at a high level and remains one of the notable DMs of the current competitive scene.

An unusual aspect of his current era is the absence of an active HaxBall Champions League competition. Despite aMp and FEAR continuing to produce strong seasons, the lack of the competition has limited his opportunity to return to the European stage and pursue the championship that escaped him earlier in his career.

Biography

aMp became one of the central figures of the Romanian competitive scene through his performances with FEAR.

As captain of the team, he developed into both an important player and a leading figure within the club. FEAR became one of the strongest Romanian teams of its period and repeatedly competed near the highest level.

Individually, aMp established himself primarily as a DM. During his generation, he became regarded as one of the leading players in the position.

His career has included major final appearances at both club and international level, reaching the HaxBall Champions League final with FEAR and later representing Romania in a Nations Cup Big Easy final.

FEAR

aMp is most closely associated with FEAR, a Romanian team that became a significant presence during the later HaxBall Champions League era.

As the team's captain, aMp became one of the main figures behind FEAR's competitive identity and its repeated attempts to challenge the strongest teams of the period.

The club's strongest European run came in HaxBall Champions League #22, when FEAR reached the final. The following edition, the team again made a deep run and reached the semi-finals.

HaxBall Champions League Career

aMp's strongest documented HaxBall Champions League period came across Editions #22 and #23, when FEAR established itself as one of the major Romanian challengers in the competition.

HaxBall Champions League #22: The Final

FEAR produced its strongest HaxBall Champions League campaign in Edition #22, reaching the championship match under aMp's leadership.

In the final, the Romanian side faced Pafküf, whose lineup consisted of Knap, Misaj, Jéal and Necro.

Pafküf took control of the match during the first half and established a 3–0 lead by halftime.

FEAR was unable to recover during the second half, and Pafküf eventually completed a 4–0 victory. The result left aMp and FEAR as HaxBall Champions League #22 runners-up.

Team: FEAR
Final: Pafküf 4–0 FEAR
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

HaxBall Champions League #23: Another Deep Run

FEAR returned for HaxBall Champions League #23 after the disappointment of the previous final.

The Romanian side once again produced a strong tournament and reached the semi-finals, demonstrating that the previous campaign had not been an isolated result.

There, aMp's FEAR faced Flaviu 1997. FEAR's run ended in the semi-final, while Flaviu 1997 advanced and eventually won the competition.

International Career

aMp also represented Romania in international competition and reached a major final at Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy.

Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy: Final Against Israel

Romania reached the final of Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy, where aMp faced Israel.

The Romanian lineup consisted of El Drago, aMp, GirlontaR and Hakut.

Israel entered the final with Yannex, P4ER, Unique and Meyer.

Israel won the tournament, leaving aMp and Romania with a runner-up finish.

Romania: El Drago • aMp • GirlontaR • Hakut
Israel: Yannex • P4ER • Unique • Meyer
Result: 🥈 Runner-up

Current Era

aMp remains active in 2026 and continues to compete as captain of FEAR.

His continued performances have maintained his reputation as one of the leading DMs of the current generation.

However, his era presents a different competitive environment from earlier periods. At present, there is no active HaxBall Champions League competition providing the same European stage that existed during FEAR's #22 and #23 campaigns.

As a result, even though aMp and FEAR have continued to produce successful seasons, he has not had the opportunity to return to the competition and make another attempt at the championship.

This makes comparisons between aMp and players from previous generations more difficult. Earlier leading players regularly had the opportunity to build their historical records through HaxBall Champions League campaigns, while aMp's current prime has partly taken place without that stage being available.

Playing Style

aMp plays primarily as a DM.

His overall level in the position has established him among the leading DMs of his generation, combining his individual role with the added responsibility of captaining FEAR.

Rather than judging his career purely through trophy totals, aMp's standing is better reflected by his sustained level of play, his leadership of one of Romania's strongest teams and FEAR's repeated ability to compete deep into major tournaments.

Legacy

aMp's competitive story remains unfinished.

As captain of FEAR, he led the Romanian side to a HaxBall Champions League final and another semi-final appearance in consecutive editions, establishing the team as one of the notable challengers of the period.

His career also includes a major international final with Romania. At Nations Cup 2020 - Big Easy, aMp reached the championship match before finishing runner-up against Israel.

Individually, he has developed into one of the leading DMs of his generation and remains active at a high level in 2026.

His historical record is also shaped by circumstance. With the HaxBall Champions League currently absent from the competitive calendar, a significant part of aMp's active prime has taken place without access to the tournament that traditionally provided the largest European stage for players of previous generations.

For that reason, his eventual standing should not be evaluated solely by the absence of a HaxBall Champions League title. His level of play, leadership of FEAR and appearances in major club and international finals remain important parts of his profile while his competitive career continues.
